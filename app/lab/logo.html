The logo, alive

The flat logo is cut into 13 layers (tools/logo_layers.py): sticker, seven letters, the tower, the taxi, three sparkles. Hover over it, then click the taxi, the tower, a letter or a sparkle.

Try each one

  • Intro, about 1.5 s: sticker pops, CLUB drops in, NYC bounces up, the tower grows from behind, the taxi drives in and brakes, sparkles twinkle on, one shine.
  • Idle: a sparkle twinkles every few seconds; now and then the taxi’s engine jiggles.
  • Hover: the letters do a wave and a shine sweeps across (once every 1.6 s at most).
  • Click: taxi honks and hops, the tower sways, letters squash like jelly, sparkles spin.
  • Reduced motion: the logo fades in, nothing moves on its own.
