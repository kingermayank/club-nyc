Questions?

Is Club NYC free?

Yes. It’s free to play and there’s nothing to buy. Coins are earned in the game and can’t be bought or sold.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Club NYC runs in your web browser on a computer. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.

Can I play on my phone?

Not yet. The city needs a keyboard and a bigger screen, so it’s made for laptops and desktops.

Do I need an account?

No. Press Play now and you’re in as a guest. Link Google, X or Discord any time to keep your New Yorker everywhere.

How old do I need to be?

13 or older. We ask for your birth month and year to check, and we don’t keep them.

Is the game finished?

Club NYC is in early access: you can play right now, and new places and features land all the time.
