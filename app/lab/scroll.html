The fun stuff

There’s always something to do, on your own or with friends.

01

Your look, your rules

Shop the city for hats, tees, puffers, sneakers and the occasional foam crown. Try things on before you buy and change your look whenever you like.

  • 8 shops, from SoHo boutiques to a Fifth Avenue toy store
  • A signature color your favorite pieces wear
02

50 stamps for doing very New York things

Ride the subway. Coo at three Times Square pigeons. Sit on the steps of the Met. Every stamp comes with a hint, and every 5 you collect pays 25 coins.

  • Wear your favorite stamp as a title
  • Bonus coins every 5 stamps
03

Five silly jobs that pay real coins

Toss pizzas at Slice Factory, run the lunch line at Halal Rush, catch deliveries at Bagel Counters, steer the ferry and dance for tips in Showtime!

  • This-week and all-time leaderboards for every job
  • Quick rounds you can play in a couple of minutes
04

Make your place feel like home

Every New Yorker starts in a 5th-floor walk-up in the East Village. Fill it with furniture, throw a house party, then save up for a SoHo loft or a penthouse.

  • Friends can visit and sign your guestbook
  • House parties with your pick of music and vibe

Be a good neighbor

Club NYC is for ages 13 and up. A few simple rules keep the city friendly for everyone.

  • Be kindNo bullying, harassment or hate.
  • Keep it PG-13No dating, sexual content or slurs.
  • Keep it privateNo real names, addresses or socials.
  • Play fairNo cheating, bots or extra accounts.
  • Be yourselfDon’t pretend to be staff or another player.

Questions?

Is Club NYC free?

Yes. It’s free to play and there’s nothing to buy. Coins are earned in the game and can’t be bought or sold.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Club NYC runs in your web browser on a computer. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.

Can I play on my phone?

Not yet. The city needs a keyboard and a bigger screen, so it’s made for laptops and desktops.

How old do I need to be?

13 or older. We ask for your birth month and year to check, and we don’t keep them.

How do I play with my friends?

Share your invite link from the phone on your toolbar. Friends who open it land right next to you.
