Take the wheel of the Liberty Island ferry. Steer up and down to scoop up coins and dodge harbor traffic, and get your passengers to the island in one piece.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

Go to [Battery Park](places/battery-park-and-liberty-island.md), click **Ferry Tickets** (or **Board the Ferry**), then **Captain the Ferry**.

## How to Play {#how-to-play}

:::steps
1. **Cast off.** Press **Cast Off!** (or [[Space]]).
2. **Steer.** Move the ferry up and down to grab coins and dodge everything else. The ferry moves forward on its own.
3. **Survive.** You have **3 life rings**. Three bumps and you're turned back to port.
4. **Dock.** The last stretch is on autopilot ("Steady as she goes…"). Nothing can hit you there.
:::

## Scoring and Pay {#scoring-and-pay}

- **+1** for every coin you scoop up. Coins come in rows, between channel markers, and in a trail into the pier at the end.
- **Life rings** float by now and then. If you've lost a life, a ring gives it back. If you haven't, it's **+5 coins**.
- **Dock at Liberty Island** for a **+20** arrival bonus.
- **A bump** costs a life and slows you down for a moment.

Your pay is the coins you scooped plus the arrival bonus. Your score (for the leaderboard) is **coins scooped**, without the bonus.

### What's in the Water {#whats-in-the-water}

- **From the start:** logs, buoys, kayaks and tugboats.
- **Later:** sailboats that tack across your path, jet skis that weave (a flashing **!** warns you they're coming), tugboats towing barges, and kayak tours.
- **Seagulls** drop pizza slices on you. When you see **Incoming!**, a red ring marks where it'll land. Move.

There's always a way through: the harbor never blocks every lane at once.

## Tips {#tips}

- **Move the moment you see "Incoming!"**
- **At full health, life rings are worth 5 coins.** Grab them anyway.
- **Finishing pays +20,** so a careful trip beats a greedy one.

## Rewards {#rewards}

| Reward | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Captain!** stamp | Steer the ferry all the way to Liberty Island. |
| **Lady Liberty** stamp | Arriving at the island counts as visiting it. |
| **Ferry Captain Hat** | Your first collected trip. |
| XP | 15, plus half your coins (up to 60). |

Dock successfully and you step off the ferry onto [Liberty Island](places/battery-park-and-liberty-island.md#liberty-island).

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> The real Statue of Liberty ferry needs a ticket. The Staten Island Ferry next door is free and has a snack bar. Ours has seagulls. It evens out.
