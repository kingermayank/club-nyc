Joe's Pizza is short-staffed, so grab an apron. Pies ride a conveyor belt toward the oven. Your job: make each one exactly the way the ticket says before it gets there.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

Go to [Chinatown & Little Italy](places/chinatown-and-little-italy.md), step into **Joe's Pizza**, click **Order a Slice**, then **Work a Shift**.

## How to Play {#how-to-play}

:::steps
1. **Read the ticket.** It shows the sauce, the cheese and how many of each topping.
2. **Sauce it.** Click a sauce bottle (red or white), then hold the mouse button down and scribble it over the whole pie.
3. **Cheese it.** Click the cheese tub, then click the pie.
4. **Top it.** Click a topping bin, then click the pie once per piece. You can also drag from a bin and drop on the pie.
5. **Let it go.** When the pie matches the ticket, it stamps **READY!** and rides into the oven.
:::

The toppings, left to right: pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers, sausage, olives and basil.

## Scoring and Pay {#scoring-and-pay}

- A pie counts when the sauce covers it, it has cheese, and **every topping count is exact**. The order you add things in doesn't matter.
- **An extra topping ruins the pie** right away (the ticket says **OOPS!**). A couple of stray drops of the wrong sauce are forgiven; a third ruins it.
- **Pay:** 2 coins per correct pie, 3 coins each on a streak of 5 or more, and 4 coins each on a streak of 10 or more.
- **Dessert pizzas** pay double. Around the middle of the shift, the belt switches to candy toppings and sprinkles for two pies.
- **Full shift bonus:** finish all 30 orders with fewer than 5 burnt pies for **+10 coins**.

## How a Shift Goes {#how-a-shift-goes}

- A shift is **30 orders**, or ends early after **5 burnt pies**.
- The belt speeds up as you go: **Lunch Rush!** at order 8 and **Dinner Rush!** at order 20.
- Burning a pie slows the belt down a little, and good pies speed it back up.
- The first two orders come with on-screen hints ("Red sauce first!", "Now cheese!", "Toppings!").

Your score is **pizzas made**.

## Tips {#tips}

- **Protect your streak.** From pie 10 on, every correct pie pays double the base.
- **Scribble the sauce around the whole pie,** not in one spot. Coverage is checked in the middle and in two rings around it.
- **Stop clicking at READY.** You can still add to a ready pie until it rides off, and one extra topping ruins it.
- **Learn the keys.** [[Q]] [[W]] [[E]] and [[1]] to [[6]] are much faster than reaching for the bins.
