The line at the Halal Bros cart wraps around the block. Build what's in each customer's order bubble, then hand it over before they lose patience.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

Go to [Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md), click the **Halal Bros Cart**, then **Work the Lunch Rush**.

## How to Play {#how-to-play}

:::steps
1. **Grab a base.** A foil container for a platter, or a pita.
2. **Scoop.** Yellow rice (platters always need it), then the meat or falafel the customer wants, and salad if they asked for it.
3. **Squirt.** White sauce and hot sauce, as many squirts as the bubble shows.
4. **Serve.** Click the customer (or their bubble) to hand it over.
:::

Your first customer comes with arrows that walk you through a full order.

## Scoring and Pay {#scoring-and-pay}

- **Prices:** a platter is $8, a pita is $6, and a combo (chicken and lamb) is $1 more.
- **Perfect order:** full price plus a tip. Faster service means a bigger tip, and a streak of perfect orders adds a little more.
- **One small slip** (salad or a sauce count off, rice in a pita): half price and no tip. Your streak resets.
- **A big mistake** (wrong base, a platter without rice, the wrong meat): $0, and the customer storms off.
- **Pay:** your coins are your cash total divided by 5 (so $50 in the till is 10 coins).

### Special Customers {#special-customers}

- **Extra extra white sauce.** Some customers want three squirts. They'll tell you.
- **The construction crew.** A worker in a hard hat orders two platters for the crew. Serve both.
- **The ketchup tourist.** Wearing an I ♥ NY tee and holding a snow globe, asking for ketchup. Halal Bros does not do ketchup. You'll figure out the polite way to handle it.

## How a Shift Goes {#how-a-shift-goes}

- A shift is **2 minutes 30 seconds**. The clock ticks in the last 10 seconds, then the **CLOSED** sign goes up.
- Customers arrive faster as the rush builds, and get less patient.
- People who wait too long in line leave ("This line is crazy!").

Your score is **orders served**.

## Tips {#tips}

- **Speed pays.** The tip shrinks as a customer's patience runs out.
- **Tossing is free.** [[X]] never breaks your streak; it only costs a second.
- **Platters always need rice.**
- **Use the keys.** [[C]] [[1]] [[2]] [[W]] and a click is a whole chicken platter.
