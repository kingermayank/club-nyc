A rhythm game on the subway. Arrows scroll down four lanes in time with the music. Hit each one on the beat, keep your combo going and the riders will tip.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

Go to the [Times Sq–42 St Station](places/subway-station.md), click **Board the Train**, then **Showtime!**

## How to Play {#how-to-play}

:::steps
1. **Pick a difficulty.** **Easy** (the Local: all stops) or **Hard** (the Express: hold on!).
2. **Hit the arrows.** When an arrow reaches the target at the bottom of its lane, press the matching key.
3. **Keep the combo.** Every 10 in a row, you pull off a trick: a spin, a backflip or a pole trick.
4. **Collect your tips** when the train pulls into the station.
:::

## Controls {#controls}

| Action | Keys |
| --- | :---: |
| Left, up, down, right | [[←]] [[↑]] [[↓]] [[→]] or [[A]] [[W]] [[S]] [[D]] |
| Pause and resume | [[Esc]] or [[P]], or the pause button |

You can also tap the lanes. The game pauses itself if you switch tabs. From the pause card you can **Resume** or **End Show**.

## Scoring and Pay {#scoring-and-pay}

- **Perfect** is 100 points and **Good** is 50. Pressing too early counts as a miss, so don't mash.
- **Combo multiplier:** ×2 from a 10 combo, ×3 from 25 and ×4 from 50.
- **Riders:** a miss makes a rider look away until you string a few hits together again. One rider usually starts out unimpressed ("Not again...") and takes a longer streak to win over.
- **Tips** land in your hat as you go. Perfect hits, a big combo and more riders watching all mean bigger tips.
- **Rank:** S, A, B, C or D at the end, based on your accuracy. Your rank adds a tip bonus, and every performer gets at least 3 coins ("Here, kid.").

Your score is your **points**.

## How a Show Goes {#how-a-show-goes}

- One song, about a minute and a half. The show ends when the train arrives.
- **Hard** has nearly twice as many arrows as **Easy**, and tighter timing.
- The arrows are the same every time you play a difficulty, so practice really pays off.

## Tips {#tips}

- **Learn the chart.** It never changes, so every run gets easier.
- **Hard pays more,** if you can keep up.
- **Don't mash.** An early press is a miss.
- **Keep the riders watching.** More eyes on you means bigger tips.

## Rewards {#rewards}

| XP | 15, plus half your coins (up to 60). |
