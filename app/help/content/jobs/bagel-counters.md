The delivery guy at Bean There has a truck full of coffee sacks and bagel boxes, and a very relaxed attitude about how he unloads it. Catch everything he throws, carry it to the pallet, and don't drop the lot. A loving tribute to a certain famous coffee-bag game.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

Go to [SoHo](places/soho.md), step into **Bean There**, click the **Order Counter**, then **Help With the Delivery**.

## How to Play {#how-to-play}

:::steps
1. **Catch.** Move under the falling sacks and boxes to catch them on your head. You can carry up to 5.
2. **Deliver.** Walk left into the **DROP ZONE** by the pallet. Your stack unloads by itself.
3. **Dodge.** Cones, flower pots and dive-bombing pigeons knock your whole stack down.
:::

## Controls {#controls}

| Action | Mouse | Keys |

You move slower the more you carry.

## Scoring and Pay {#scoring-and-pay}

- **+1** for each item delivered.
- **Full load bonus +1** for unloading a full stack of 5.
- **Fresh bagel! +2** for the special bagels, from level 2.
- **Level bonus:** 2 times the level, for each level you finish.
- **Empty the whole truck:** +10 more.
- **You lose a life** if you catch a 6th item ("Too heavy!") or get hit by a hazard. Either way, you drop everything you're carrying.
- **Deli coffee** sometimes flies out when you're low on lives. Catch it for **+1 life**.

Your pay is your deliveries plus all your bonuses. Your score is **items delivered**.

## How a Shift Goes {#how-a-shift-goes}

- **5 levels** of 20 deliveries each, and **3 lives** (shown as coffee cups).
- Every level throws faster, with doubles and triples. Flower pots show up early, pigeons from level 3, and the last level is "empty the truck!"
- The shift ends when you're out of lives or the truck is empty.

## Tips {#tips}

- **Unload full stacks of 5,** but never catch a 6th.
- **Unload often in later levels.** One hit scatters everything you're carrying.
- **Keep moving.** Hazards aim at where you're standing, and pigeons dive at where you're heading.
