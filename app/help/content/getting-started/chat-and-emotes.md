Everyone in the same place can see what you say, in a speech bubble over your head. Here's how to talk, and how to say it without words.

## Talking {#talking}

1. Press [[Enter]] (or click the chat box on the toolbar).
2. Type your message. It can be up to 120 characters.
3. Press [[Enter]] to send it. Press [[Esc]] to clear the box instead.

Your words pop up in a bubble over your New Yorker for everyone in the place.

> [!NOTE] Who Can See My Chat?
> Only people in the same place (and the same copy of it) see what you say. When you leave, your words stay behind. Chat is kept for 72 hours so moderators can review reports, then it's deleted.

## Emotes {#emotes}

Click the smiley at the left of the chat box and pick an emoji: smiles, a laugh, heart eyes, cool shades, a slice of pizza, the Statue of Liberty, a taxi, coffee, a party popper and more. It pops up in a big bubble over your head.

Sending a single emoji in chat does the same thing.

## Moves {#moves}

Click **Moves** on the toolbar, or use the keys:

| Move | Key | Notes |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Dance | [[D]] | Keeps going until you move. Dance near others to start a [flash mob](events/flash-mobs.md). |
| Wave | [[W]] | City locals often wave back. |
| Cheer | [[C]] | For when the ferry docks or the Knicks win. |
| Jump | [[J]] | A little hop. |
| Sit | [[S]] | Sits on the nearest free seat, or right where you are. |

## Chat Safety {#chat-safety}

The city keeps chat friendly for everyone:

- **Links, emails and phone numbers are removed** automatically. Never share your real name, address, school or socials, and never move a chat to another app.
- **Strong swears and slurs are blocked.** Keep it PG-13.
- **Slow down if you're flooding.** Sending lots of messages very fast pauses your chat for 30 seconds.
- **Don't like what someone's saying?** Click them and choose **Mute** (hides their chat), **Block** or **Report**. [Safety & Community Rules](safety.md)
