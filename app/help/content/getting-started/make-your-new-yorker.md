Making your New Yorker takes two quick screens. Your look can be changed later from your [player card](shops-and-wardrobe.md#your-wardrobe), so don't overthink it. This is New York: nobody's looking at you anyway.

## Step 1: Pick a Look {#pick-a-look}

The first screen asks **Who's walking into Manhattan?**

![The first screen of the New Yorker maker, with eight ready-made looks, skin, hair and hair color rows, and a ring of colors around the avatar.](/help/img/creator-look.webp "Start from a ready-made look, then make it yours.")

- **Start from a look.** Click one of the eight ready-made outfits.
- **Surprise me.** The dice button rolls a random skin, hair and colors.
- **Skin, Hair and Hair Color.** Click through the rows to change each one.
- **Your color.** The ring of dots around your New Yorker sets your signature color, like Taxi Yellow, Hydrant Red, Bodega Blue or Pigeon Grey. Point at a dot to see its name. Your color shows up on your signature hoodie and around the city.

When you like what you see, press **That's Me**.

## Step 2: Pick a Name {#pick-a-name}

The second screen asks **What do New Yorkers call you?**

![The name screen, with two spinning name wheels, a box to type your own name, and the birthday fields.](/help/img/creator-name.webp "Spin a name or type your own.")

- **Spin a name.** Two wheels pair a word with a very New York word, like "LuckyPigeon", "RooftopKnish" or "DJPretzel". Click the arrows or scroll the wheels, or press the dice button to spin both.
- **Or type your own.** Up to 14 characters.
- A green check means the name is yours. If it's taken, spin again or add a number.

### Name Rules {#name-rules}

- 2 to 14 characters: letters, numbers and underscores (`_`) only.
- Every name is unique, so if someone already has it, you'll need another.
- Names are checked by the same filter as chat. Keep it friendly: see [names and outfits](safety.md#names-and-outfits).
- Your name is what everyone in the city sees on your name tag.

## Step 3: Your Birthday and the Rules {#birthday}

Club NYC is for ages 13 and up. Before you walk in:

1. Pick your **birth month and year**. We only use them to check your age, and we don't keep them.
2. Tick the box to agree to the [Terms](/terms.html), [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) and [Community Rules](/rules.html).
3. Press **Walk into the City**. Your MetroCard prints and you're in.

> [!NOTE] Under 13?
> We'll ask you to double-check the date first, in case of a typo. If you're under 13, you'll see a friendly "Sorry, not yet!" and we hope to see you when you're 13.

## What You Start With {#what-you-start-with}

- **50 coins** to get you going.
- **Your outfit** from the look you picked, in your wardrobe.
- **A free welcome gift**, waiting at Sal's hot dog cart in Times Square (a cap, beanie, shades, foam crown or MetroCard lanyard). If your starting look already came with one of those, Sal will happily sell you a hot dog instead.
- **A MetroCard** with your name and member number. When it flips in at the corner, click it to save a picture of it.

## Changing Your Look Later {#change-later}

Press [[P]] (or click yourself) to open your player card:

- **Wardrobe** tab: everything you own. Click to wear it.
- **Look** tab: skin, hair, hair color and your color.

Your name can't be changed by you after it's set. If a moderator resets a name that breaks the rules, you'll be asked to pick a new one.

> [!NYC] Fashion Advice From the Bodega Cat
> Wear whatever you want. You will see someone on the subway dressed as a lobster, and you will both pretend that's normal.
