Making your New Yorker takes three quick screens, then a stop at the station for your MetroCard. Your look can be changed later from your [player card](shops-and-wardrobe.md#your-wardrobe), so don't overthink it. This is New York: nobody's looking at you anyway.

## Step 1: Pick a Look {#pick-a-look}

The first screen asks **Who's walking into Manhattan?** and gives you three ways in.

![The first screen of the New Yorker maker, with three choices: Use a Photo, Surprise Me and Build My Own.](/help/img/creator-look.webp "Three ways to start. You can change everything after.")

- **Use a Photo.** Take a photo with your camera or upload one, and the game suggests a New Yorker to match: skin tone, hair, glasses, facial hair and face shape. The photo is read on your own computer. It is never uploaded and never saved. See [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html#photo-match).
- **Surprise Me.** A random New Yorker, ready to go. **Roll Again** makes another.
- **Build My Own.** Pick every detail yourself.

Whichever you choose, the next screen lets you change anything, in three tabs:

- **Basics.** Skin, hair and hair color.
- **Face.** Face shape, eye color, brows and nose.
- **Extras.** Glasses, facial hair, freckles and lashes.

After a photo, a sparkle marks the closest guesses. Light can fool a camera, so if the skin tone or hair is a little off, the right one is usually the tile next door.

**Your color.** The ring of dots around your New Yorker sets your signature color, like Taxi Yellow, Hydrant Red, Bodega Blue or Pigeon Grey. Point at a dot to see its name. Your color shows up on your signature hoodie and around the city.

When you like what you see, press **That's Me**.

## Step 2: Pick a Name {#pick-a-name}

The second screen asks **What do New Yorkers call you?**

- **Type a name.** Up to 14 characters. A green check means it's yours. If it's taken, try another or add a number.
- **Or sign in with Google or X.** Playing as a guest? Sign in here and your New Yorker is saved to that account, so it follows you to any device. You come right back to this screen with your look kept, and pick your name. Already have a New Yorker on that account? You go straight to them.

### Name Rules {#name-rules}

- 2 to 14 characters: letters, numbers and underscores (`_`) only.
- Every name is unique, so if someone already has it, you'll need another.
- Names are checked by the same filter as chat. Keep it friendly: see [names and outfits](safety.md#names-and-outfits).
- Your name is what everyone in the city sees on your name tag.

## Step 3: Agree to the Rules {#birthday}

Club NYC is for ages 13 and up. Before you walk in:

1. Tick the box to confirm you're 13 or older and agree to the [Terms](/terms.html), [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) and [Community Rules](/rules.html).
2. Press **Walk into the City**.

## Step 4: Your MetroCard and the Train {#metrocard}

Before the city, a quick stop at the Times Sq–42 St station:

1. **The photo booth.** Pick a face (Smile, Big Grin or Surprised) and press **Snap**. Your photo lands on a fresh MetroCard with your name and member number.
2. **Stamp it.** Click your card to stamp it NEW YORKER. Press **Save My Card** to keep a picture of it.
3. **Swipe in.** Drag your card through the turnstile reader, not too fast and not too slow. (The first swipe always asks you to swipe again. That's the real New York experience.)
4. **Ride in.** The train takes you to Times Square, and you walk up the subway stairs into the city.

On a keyboard: **Enter** snaps, stamps and heads to the train, and **Space** swipes. **Skip Intro** (top right) takes you straight to Times Square.

> [!NOTE] Under 13?
> Club NYC isn't for you yet. We hope to see you when you're 13!

## What You Start With {#what-you-start-with}

- **50 coins** to get you going.
- **Your outfit** from the look you picked, in your wardrobe.
- **A free welcome gift**, waiting at Sal's hot dog cart in Times Square (a cap, beanie, shades, foam crown or MetroCard lanyard). If your starting look already came with one of those, Sal will happily sell you a hot dog instead.
- **A MetroCard** with your photo, name and member number, printed and stamped at the station.

## Changing Your Look Later {#change-later}

Press [[P]] (or click yourself) to open your player card:

- **Wardrobe** tab: everything you own. Click to wear it.
- **Look** tab: skin, hair, hair color and your color.

Your name can't be changed by you after it's set. If a moderator resets a name that breaks the rules, you'll be asked to pick a new one.

> [!NYC] Fashion Advice From the Bodega Cat
> Wear whatever you want. You will see someone on the subway dressed as a lobster, and you will both pretend that's normal.
