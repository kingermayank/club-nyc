Your first day in the city comes with a guide (Rosa), a to-do list (New in Town) and a welcome gift (from Sal). Here's how it all works.

## Rosa's Tour {#rosas-tour}

Rosa is your building's super. Right after you make your New Yorker, she offers a quick tour. It has four short stops:

:::steps
1. **Walk around.** Click anywhere on the ground to walk there. Rosa marks a glowing spot to try.
2. **Things that glow are clickable.** Carts, shops and doors light up when you point at them. Click them for snacks, clothes and jobs that pay coins.
3. **Say hi and make friends.** Type in the chat box and press [[Enter]] to talk to the room. Click any New Yorker to add them as a friend.
4. **Go anywhere.** The map and the subway take you all over the city. Your first-day list at the top left shows what to try next.
:::

![Rosa's tour card, pointing at the chat box with Back, Skip Tour and Next buttons.](/help/img/tour.webp "Rosa keeps it short. She has a boiler to fix.")

- Use **Next** and **Back**, or the [[→]] and [[←]] keys. [[Enter]] also goes to the next step.
- **Skip Tour** (or [[Esc]]) ends it any time.
- Want it again later? Press [[?]] or click the **?** button at the top left. It's also in the **Phone** menu as **How to Play**.

## New in Town {#new-in-town}

Your first-day list sits at the top left of the screen. It shows one step at a time, with a hint and a **Show Me** button that points you to the right spot. If it's in another place, Show Me takes you there first. Click the list's title to see every step.

| Step | How | Reward |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Take a Walk | Click anywhere on the ground. | 10 coins |
| Pick Up Your Welcome Gift | Click Sal's glowing hot dog cart in [Times Square](places/times-square.md). | 15 coins |
| Make a Friend | Click any New Yorker, then **Add Friend**. | 30 coins |
| Work a Shift | Try a [job](jobs/README.md): the pizzeria, the halal cart or the ferry. | 35 coins |
| Ride the Subway | Click the MetroCard on your toolbar (or press [[U]]) and pick a stop. | 20 coins |

That's **110 coins** in all. Each step also earns [XP](levels-and-xp.md). Finish the list and you're officially a New Yorker: Rosa sends her congratulations, you get an Everything Bagel bean bag for your apartment, and you earn the **Local** [stamp](stamps.md).

> [!TIP] Make a Friend, the Easy Way
> The city locals (the computer-run New Yorkers walking around) count as friends too. Wave at one, then click them and press **Add Friend**. Some want to hang out a bit before they say yes: wave or dance with them and try again.

## Your Welcome Gift {#welcome-gift}

Sal runs the hot dog cart in Times Square. The first time you click it, he offers a free welcome gift. Pick one:

- NYC Cap
- Big Apple Beanie
- City Shades
- Foam Liberty Crown
- MetroCard Lanyard

You'll put it on right away. After that, Sal's cart sells hot dogs (2 coins). If your starting look already had one of these, Sal skips straight to the hot dogs.

## After New in Town: Today in the City {#today-in-the-city}

Once your first-day list is done, the same spot shows **Today in the City**: three small challenges a day, the same three for everyone. Each pays 15 coins, and finishing all three pays a 25 coin bonus. New ones arrive at midnight New York time. See [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) for the full list.

## Coming Back {#coming-back}

- **Daily refill.** The first time you drop by each day, you get free coins. The refill grows with the days you visit this week, and it never shrinks if you miss a day. [How it works](coins-and-shopping.md#daily-refill)
- **Your stuff is saved.** Your New Yorker, coins, wardrobe and apartment are saved to the city. As a guest, they live in this browser until you [link an account](account.md#link-an-account).
- **Missed the tour?** A one-time note from Rosa offers to show you around.
