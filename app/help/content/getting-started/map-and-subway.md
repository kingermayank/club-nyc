You can walk between neighboring places, but to get across town, use the map, the subway or a taxi. Travel is always free.

## The City Map {#city-map}

Press [[M]] or click **Map** on the toolbar.

![The Club NYC map of Manhattan, with landmarks to click and a You Are Here pin.](/help/img/map.webp "Click where you want to go.")

- **Click a landmark** to go straight there. A **You Are Here** pin shows where you are now.
- **My Place** (bottom corner) takes you home to your [apartment](apartment/README.md).
- **Subway entrances** on the map open the subway map.
- **The Statue of Liberty** is ferry-only, so clicking it takes you to the ferry dock at [Battery Park](places/battery-park-and-liberty-island.md).
- **Wall Street** has its own pin, by The Bull.
- **Places marked Coming Soon** are still being built, like Brooklyn (Coming Soon). Club NYC is Manhattan only for now.

> [!NYC] Map Reading Tip
> Uptown is up. Downtown is down. Brooklyn is "basically the same as Manhattan, just better," according to everyone who lives there.

## The Subway {#subway}

Press [[U]] or click the MetroCard (**Subway**) on the toolbar. You can also read the **Subway Map** poster in the station, or click **Board the Train** in the [Times Sq–42 St Station](places/subway-station.md) and choose **Ride the Subway**.

![The Club NYC Subway map, with colored lines and stations from 86 St down to South Ferry.](/help/img/subway-map.webp "Point at a station to see where it goes, then click to ride.")

Point at a station to see where it lets you out, then click to ride. The trip takes a few seconds in a swaying subway car, and your first ride earns the **Straphanger** [stamp](stamps.md).

## Taxis {#taxis}

Click **Hail a Taxi** in [Times Square](places/times-square.md) or outside [The Met](places/the-met.md) (the street sign on [Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md) works too). Your New Yorker shouts "TAXI!" and the map opens: pick anywhere to go.

## Walking Between Places {#walking-between}

Neighboring places connect through the exits at their edges:

- **Midtown:** Times Square, Rockefeller Center, Fifth Avenue, Central Park and The Met, in a row. Grand Central is next to Times Square, and the subway station is under both.
- **Downtown:** Chelsea Market, the High Line, SoHo, Chinatown & Little Italy and the Brooklyn Bridge.
- **Lower Manhattan:** Battery Park, One World Trade and Wall Street, in a row, with Wall Street leading on to the Brooklyn Bridge. The ferry goes from Battery Park to Liberty Island, and an elevator goes up to Sky Deck 102.

Inside buildings, look for the door, stairs or elevator back out.
