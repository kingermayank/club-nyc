You can play Club NYC with just the mouse, but these keys make life faster. In the game, point at any toolbar button to see its key.

## In the City {#in-the-city}

### Moves {#moves}

:::keys
[[D]] Dance (until you move)

[[W]] Wave

[[C]] Cheer

[[J]] Jump

[[S]] Sit down
:::

### Panels {#panels}

:::keys
[[M]] Map

[[U]] Subway

[[N]] Club NYC Times

[[F]] Friends & Leaderboards

[[P]] Player Card & Wardrobe

[[H]] My Apartment

[[I]] Invite Friends

[[T]] Text a Friend

[[?]] How to Play (Rosa's tour)
:::

### Chat {#chat}

:::keys
[[Enter]] Start typing, then send

[[Esc]] Clear the chat box
:::

### Everywhere {#everywhere}

:::keys
[[Esc]] Close the open panel

[[→]] [[←]] Next and back in Rosa's tour

[[S]] Skip to the curtain call at a Broadway show
:::

## In the Jobs {#in-the-jobs}

Each job shows its keys on its how-to screen before you start. In every job, [[Esc]] asks if you want to leave; press it again to go. On the results card, [[Esc]] takes you back to the city.

| Job | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| [Slice Factory](jobs/slice-factory.md) | Mouse to sauce, cheese and top. [[Q]] red sauce, [[W]] white sauce, [[E]] cheese, [[1]] to [[6]] toppings, [[Esc]] or right-click to put a tool down, [[M]] music |
| [Halal Rush](jobs/halal-rush.md) | [[C]] container, [[P]] pita, [[1]] to [[5]] rice, chicken, lamb, falafel, salad, [[W]] white sauce, [[H]] hot sauce, [[X]] toss, then click the customer |
| [Bagel Counters](jobs/bagel-counters.md) | Mouse, or [[←]] [[→]] |
| [Showtime!](jobs/showtime.md) | [[←]] [[↑]] [[↓]] [[→]] or [[A]] [[W]] [[S]] [[D]], [[Esc]] or [[P]] to pause |
| [Ferry Captain](jobs/ferry-captain.md) | [[↑]] [[↓]] or [[W]] [[S]], or move the mouse. [[Space]] to cast off, [[H]] for the horn |
