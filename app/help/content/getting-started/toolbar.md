The toolbar runs along the bottom of the screen. Point at any button to see its name and its key.

![The toolbar along the bottom of the game screen: emotes, moves, the chat box, map, subway, newspaper, phone, friends, player card, stamp book, apartment and sound buttons.](/help/img/toolbar.webp "The toolbar, left to right.")

| Button | Name | Key | What it does |

| {tb:chat} | **Chat** | [[Enter]] | The chat box. Type and press [[Enter]] to talk to everyone in the place. [More on chat](getting-started/chat-and-emotes.md) |

| {tb:metrocard} | **Subway** | [[U]] | The subway map. Click a station to ride there. |
| {tb:news} | **Club NYC Times** | [[N]] | The city paper: news, the bodega cat's advice and this week's leaders. [The Club NYC Times](events/club-nyc-times.md) |

## The Phone Menu {#phone}

Click the **Phone** button for:

- **Invite Friends** [[I]]: your personal invite link. Friends who use it land right next to you. [Inviting Friends](community/inviting-friends.md)
- **Text a Friend** [[T]]: send a friend who's online a quick text like "Meet Me Here". They get a button that brings them to you. [Texting Friends](community/friends.md#text-a-friend)
- **How to Play** [[?]]: Rosa's tour, again.
- **Help Center**: opens this site in a new tab.

## The Top of the Screen {#top-of-screen}

The toolbar isn't the only place with buttons:

- **Top left:** the name of the place you're in, the **?** button (Rosa's tour), the **envelope** for your [postcards](community/postcards-and-notices.md), and your [first-day list](getting-started/first-day.md#new-in-town) or [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md).
- **Top right:** the **online** count (click it to see who's around), your **level** ring (click it for your player card) and your **coins**.

> [!TIP] Can't Click Anything?
> While a panel is open, the toolbar waits for you. Press [[Esc]] or the red **X** to close the panel first.
