Club NYC is in early access, so the city changes often. Here's what's new, newest first. For first looks at what's coming, join the [Discord](/discord).

## October 24, 2026 {#2026-10-24}

- **The sled race moved to the High Line.** No snow in the city, so the Pilgrim Hill Sled Race is now the [High Line Citi Bike Dash](multiplayer-games/citi-bike-dash.md): up to four riders on Citi Bikes from Gansevoort St to 34th St, past the Sundeck, through the Chelsea Market Passage and over a ramp at the Spur. Dodge tour groups, planters and a runaway gelato cart. Its sign is on [The High Line](places/high-line-and-chelsea-market.md) now, and your best times, wins and stamps came along (the stamps are now called Gansevoort to 34th and Top of the Line). Bikes aren't allowed on the real High Line. Nobody told these racers.

## October 23, 2026 {#2026-10-23}

- **Real New York names.** The shops and food spots now go by their real names: [Louis Vuitton](places/fifth-avenue.md#louis-vuitton), [Saks Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md#saks-fifth-avenue) and the Adel's Famous Halal Food cart on Fifth Avenue, [Supreme](places/soho.md#supreme), [Nike](places/soho.md#nike) and [Ralph's Coffee](places/soho.md#ralphs-coffee) in SoHo, and Joe's Shanghai, Ten Ren Tea and [Joe's Pizza](places/chinatown-and-little-italy.md#joes-pizza) downtown. Everything you own stays yours.
- **The toy store is now Saks Fifth Avenue.** Saks sells shoes (from the 10022-SHOE floor), coats, jewelry and shades. The toys, balloons and dress-up moved to [I ♥ NY Souvenirs](places/times-square.md#i-love-ny-souvenirs) in Times Square. The floor piano went with the toys, so the Heart and Soul stamp is retired.

## October 21, 2026 {#2026-10-21}

- **Multiplayer games are here.** Race up to three friends in the Pilgrim Hill Sled Race (now the [High Line Citi Bike Dash](multiplayer-games/citi-bike-dash.md)), sprint the platform in [Rush Hour Run](multiplayer-games/rush-hour-run.md) with up to nine, or settle it at the [Carb-Jitsu](multiplayer-games/carb-jitsu.md) table. Find them on the new **Games** tab ([[F]]), with Quick Match, lobbies you can share with a code, and city locals to fill any empty seat. Nine new stamps come with them. [Multiplayer Games](multiplayer-games/README.md)

## October 20, 2026 {#2026-10-20}

- **Lower Manhattan opens.** Three new places: [One World Trade](places/one-world-trade.md) with the 9/11 Memorial and the Survivor Tree, [Sky Deck 102](places/one-world-trade.md#sky-deck-102) at the top of the tower, and [Wall Street](places/wall-street.md), home of The Bull and the Pizza Index. Walk there from Battery Park, or take the subway to WTC Cortlandt or Wall St.
- **Picture cards everywhere.** Click a landmark for a picture, a short real history, one New York joke and something to do: strike a pose, make a wish, row a boat, climb to Lady Liberty's crown.
- **Art and magnets.** The Brooklyn Bridge painter now has a [gallery of originals](places/brooklyn-bridge.md#gallery) for your walls, and the souvenir stand sells nine [fridge magnets](places/brooklyn-bridge.md#magnets) for your fridge at home.
- **Photo booths in places.** Squeeze in under the I ♥ NY neon or up on Sky Deck 102. [Photo Booths](events/photo-booths.md)
- **New stamps:** Bull Market, Cloud Nine, Art Collector, Magnetic Personality and Crown Jewel. Never Forget and Say Cheese can now be earned too. [Stamps](stamps.md)
- **Levels & XP.** Everything you do now earns XP: shifts, stamps, challenges, new places, new friends. Your level shows in a ring at the top right, every new level pays coins, and your title comes from your level, from Tourist to Club NYC Legend. [Levels & XP](levels-and-xp.md)
- **Text a Friend.** Open the Phone (or press [[T]]) to send a friend a quick "Meet Me Here". They get a button that brings them right to you. [Text a Friend](community/friends.md#text-a-friend)
- **Sign in with email.** No password: we email you a link that signs you in. Guests can link their email from the player card, too. [Account & Sign-In](account.md#email)
- **A new paper every day.** The Club NYC Times now has a fresh edition every New York day, with NYC Moments, live sports standings and back issues all the way to No. 1. [The Club NYC Times](events/club-nyc-times.md)
- **The waitlist.** Before the doors open, the website takes sign-ups for the waitlist, and we email everyone on it the day the city opens. [The Waitlist](account.md#waitlist)
- **The Help Center.** You're reading it. Press [[/]] to search. It's also in the Phone menu in the game.
- **The Phone, tidied up.** It now has Invite Friends, Text a Friend, How to Play and the Help Center. Snowballs are off until it's cold enough.
- **The map, simplified.** No more tabs: every place is right on the map, and jobs live inside their places.

## Early Access {#early-access}

The city opened its doors with:

- 20+ places across Midtown, Downtown and the harbor, from Times Square to Liberty Island.
- Five jobs: Slice Factory, Halal Rush, Bagel Counters, Showtime! and Ferry Captain, each with weekly leaderboards.
- Shops all over town, and a wardrobe to match.
- Your own apartment: decorate it, open it up, throw a party, or move up to a loft or a penthouse.
- Stamps, daily challenges, the Dance Jam, flash mobs and Neighborhood Wars.
- Friends, invites, chat with a safety filter, and Report, Block and Mute on every player card.
