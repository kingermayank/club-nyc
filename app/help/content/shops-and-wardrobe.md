## How Shopping Works {#how-shopping-works}

![A shop catalog: items on the right with prices, and your New Yorker on the cover trying one on.](/help/img/catalog.webp "Point at anything to try it on.")

1. **Click a shelf, rack or counter** in a shop. The catalog opens to that section. Use the tabs to see everything, and the arrows to turn pages.
2. **Point at an item to try it on.** Your New Yorker on the catalog cover wears it right away.
3. **Click the price to buy it.** It goes straight into your wardrobe, and you put it on.
4. Already own it? The button says **Wear** instead.

Short on coins? "Not enough coins. Try a job around town!" [Earning coins](coins-and-shopping.md#earning-coins)

> [!TIP] Snacks Count Too
> Food you buy (hot dogs, pretzels, slices, boba) goes in your hand and stays in your wardrobe's **In Hand** section. Clicking the same cart again puts it back in your hand for free.

## Your Wardrobe {#your-wardrobe}

Open your player card with [[P]], by clicking yourself, or with **Player Card & Wardrobe** on the toolbar. Shop mirrors and changing rooms open it too. It has three tabs:

- **Wardrobe.** Everything you own, with chips to filter: All, Hats, Glasses, Neck, Tops, Bottoms, Shoes and In Hand. Click an item to wear it. Click it again to take it off (tops, bottoms and shoes always stay on: you're in New York, not a dream).
- **Look.** Change your skin, hair, hair color and your color (your signature color, like Taxi Yellow or Bodega Blue).
- **Account.** Sign-in options, your member number and more. It shows when you're playing online. [Account & Sign-In](account.md)
