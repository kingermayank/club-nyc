The city keeps in touch in two calm, quiet ways: postcards you can read when you like, and short notices that come and go.

## Postcards {#postcards}

Click the **envelope** at the top left of the screen. A badge shows how many are new.

![The postcard viewer, showing a postcard from Rosa with arrows to flip between postcards.](/help/img/postcards.webp "Postcards from the city.")

- Use the arrows to flip through them ("Postcard 1 of 3").
- **Recycle** throws one away.
- Your 30 newest postcards are kept.

| Postcard | From | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome to the City! | Rosa | After your first tour. Has a button to take you to SoHo. |
| Your First Paycheck! | The job's crew | After your first paid shift. |
| Dance Jam Soon! | DJ Cadenza | Now and then, when a Dance Jam is about to start. Has a button to go to Times Square. |

No postcards yet? "The city writes when something's happening: invites, paychecks, news from your building (mostly about the elevator)."

## Notices {#notices}

Things happening right now show up as small notices at the bottom right, one at a time:

- A friend request (with **Accept** and **Later**)
- A friend coming online, texting you, or throwing a party (with a **Go** button)
- A guestbook note or a like on your apartment
- Your neighborhood team's weekly result

Notices about things people do to you chime. Ambient ones, like friends coming online, are silent. To quiet the ambient ones, click the **bell** in **Friends & Leaderboards** ([[F]]).

> [!NOTE] Calm by Design
> Club NYC never nags you to come back, never counts down to make you hurry, and never takes anything away for missing a day.
