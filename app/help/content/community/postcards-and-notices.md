The city keeps in touch with short notices that come and go, and your [iFruit](getting-started/toolbar.md) keeps a list of them.

## Notices {#notices}

Things happening right now show up as small notices at the bottom right, one at a time:

- A friend request (with **Accept** and **Later**)
- A friend coming online, texting you, or throwing a party (with a **Go** button)
- A guestbook note or a like on your apartment
- Your neighborhood team's weekly result

On your iFruit, the **bell** at the top of the screen opens them all, and you can answer friend requests right there.

Notices about things people do to you chime. Ambient ones, like friends coming online, are silent. To quiet the ambient ones, click the **bell** in **Friends & Leaderboards** ([[F]]).

> [!NOTE] Calm by Design
> Club NYC never nags you to come back, never counts down to make you hurry, and never takes anything away for missing a day.
