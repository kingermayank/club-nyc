The best way to play Club NYC is with people you know. Your invite link brings them straight to you.

## Your Invite Link {#your-link}

Open the **Phone** on the toolbar and pick **Invite Friends** (or press [[I]]).

![The invite panel, with your personal link, a Copy Link button and ways to share it.](/help/img/invite.webp "Anyone who opens your link lands right next to you.")

- **Copy Link** copies your personal link (it ends in /join/ and your name).
- **Share** it by text, WhatsApp, X or email, right from the panel.
- When someone opens it, they see "*YourName* invited you to Club NYC!", make their New Yorker, and **land right next to you**. You're friends from the start.

Invites work when you're playing online. The invite is remembered in your friend's browser for a week, so they don't have to start right away.

## Rewards {#rewards}

| Who | What | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your friend | **50 coins** welcome bonus | When they arrive |
| You | **100 coins** | Once they've played for 10 minutes |
| You | **50 XP** | When a friend joins |
| You | **Bring a Friend**, **Your Crew** and **Mayor of the Block** stamps | At 1, 3 and 10 friends |

Up to 5 invites a day pay coins, and up to 50 in all. Invites are for real friends: farming extra accounts gets them removed. [Play fair](/rules.html#play-fair)

## Other Ways to Bring People In {#other-ways}

- **The City tab** ([[F]]) has a quick link to share when the city is quiet.
- **Your MetroCard.** When you make your New Yorker, you can save a picture of your MetroCard. It shows your invite link.
- **Leaderboard brags.** A top 3 spot on a weekly job leaderboard gives you a **Tell Your Friends** button that copies a brag with your link in it.

> [!NYC] Invitation Etiquette
> "You HAVE to come, it's so fun, it's like twenty minutes from you." It is never twenty minutes. Except here, where it's one click.
