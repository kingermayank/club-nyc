Club NYC is better with people. Everything social lives in the **Friends & Leaderboards** panel: press [[F]] or click the waving hand on the toolbar.

![The Community panel's City tab: how many New Yorkers are in the city, friends out and about, and the busiest places right now.](/help/img/community-city.webp "The City tab: where everyone's hanging out right now.")

## The Tabs {#tabs}

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | How many New Yorkers are in the city right now, friends who are out and about, and the busiest places, each with a **Go** button. (Online only.) |
| **Friends** | Friend requests, your friends (online first) with where they are, a button to visit their place, people in the place you're in, and your blocked list. [Friends](community/friends.md) |
| **Games** | The multiplayer games: Quick Match, Create Lobby, and a box for a friend's code. [Multiplayer Games](multiplayer-games/README.md) |
| **Leaderboards** | This week's best shifts at every job, for everyone or just your friends. [Leaderboards](community/leaderboards.md) |
| **Neighborhoods** | Neighborhood Wars: this week's standings and your team. [Neighborhood Wars](community/neighborhood-wars.md) |

The **bell** button turns city updates on or off. When it's off, friends coming online and other ambient notes stay quiet. Things people do to you (like a friend request) still show.
