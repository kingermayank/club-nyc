Friends see where each other are, can visit each other's apartments any time, and can text each other to meet up.

## Adding a Friend {#adding-a-friend}

1. **Click a New Yorker** to open their player card.
2. Click **Add Friend**.
3. They get a notice ("wants to be friends!") with **Accept** and **Later** buttons. When they accept, you'll hear about it.

![Another player's card, with Wave, Add Friend and Visit Their Place buttons, and Mute, Block and Report underneath.](/help/img/player-card-other.webp "A player card: say hi, add a friend, or visit their place.")

If they'd already asked you, you're friends right away. You can also add people from **Friends & Leaderboards** ([[F]]): the **Friends** tab lists everyone in the place you're in, with **Add Friend** buttons.

> [!TIP] City Locals Can Be Friends Too
> The computer-run city locals can be your friends. Some want to hang out a bit first: **wave** at them or **dance** with them (a flash mob counts), then ask again. Locals from your own neighborhood team say yes more easily.

## Friend Requests {#friend-requests}

Requests show up as a notice when you're online, and wait in the **Friends** tab under **Friend Requests** with **Accept** and **Ignore** buttons. Ignoring a request quietly removes it.

## Finding Your Friends {#finding-friends}

Press [[F]] and open **Friends**. Online friends are at the top with where they are.

- **Go** takes you to them: across the city, to their copy of a busy place, or into their apartment.
- **The home button** visits their place, even when they're offline.
- When a friend comes online, you get a notice with a **Go** button (unless city updates are muted with the bell).

## Text a Friend {#text-a-friend}

Open the **Phone** on the toolbar and pick **Text a Friend** (or press [[T]]). Choose a friend who's online and send one of these:

| Text | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| **Meet Me Here** | "Meet me in Times Square!" with a **Go** button that brings them to you |
| **Say Hi** | A friendly hello, with a **Say Hi Back** button |
| **Play a Game?** | An invite to play, with a **Go** button |

Texts are presets only, between friends only, so there's nothing to moderate. You can send one every few seconds per friend. Texting works when you're playing online.

## Visiting a Friend's Place {#visiting}

Friends can visit each other's apartments any time, even when the owner's away. Click their card's **Visit Their Place**, or the home button in your friends list. [Visitors & Guestbooks](apartment/visitors-and-guestbooks.md)

## Unfriending, Muting and Blocking {#unfriend-mute-block}

From someone's player card:

- **Unfriend** removes them from your friends (no announcement).
- **Mute** hides their chat and emotes for this session.
- **Block** hides them and their chat, and stops friend requests and guestbook notes between you. They aren't told. Unblock them any time from the **Blocked** list at the bottom of the Friends tab.
- **Report** sends their recent chat to the moderators. [Safety & Community Rules](safety.md)
