Every [job](jobs/README.md) has its own leaderboard. Your best shift is your score, and every week is a fresh start.

## Where to Find Them {#where}

- **After every shift,** on the results card, with **This Week**, **All Time** and **Friends** tabs.
- **Any time,** in **Friends & Leaderboards** ([[F]]) on the **Leaderboards** tab. Pick a job, then **This Week** or **All Time**, and **Everyone** or **Friends**.
- **In Times Square,** where the billboards show each week's #1 players.

![The Leaderboards tab, with a chip for each job and the top players this week.](/help/img/community-leaderboards.webp "This week's leaders, with your own spot at the bottom.")

## What Counts {#what-counts}

| Job | Ranked by |
| --- | --- |
| [Slice Factory](jobs/slice-factory.md) | Pizzas made |
| [Halal Rush](jobs/halal-rush.md) | Orders served |
| [Bagel Counters](jobs/bagel-counters.md) | Items delivered |
| [Showtime!](jobs/showtime.md) | Points |
| [Ferry Captain](jobs/ferry-captain.md) | Coins scooped |

- **Your best single shift** counts, not your total.
- **This Week** resets every Monday. **All Time** never does.
- **Friends** shows you and your friends only.
- The board shows the top 25 (top 50 for Friends). If you're not on it, your own row is added at the bottom, with who to beat next: "You're #12 of 80 · beat Ana_NYC's 22 to climb."
- **New Best!** shows on your results card whenever you beat your own record.

> [!TIP] Bragging Rights
> Finish in the weekly top 3 and the results card offers **Tell Your Friends**: it copies a brag with your score and your [invite link](community/inviting-friends.md).

## Playing Offline {#offline}

When you play offline, you're ranked against the city locals instead, and you can **challenge** a local friend to beat your best. They'll try within a couple of minutes. Win and you get 10 coins. Lose and you get a **Rematch** button.

> [!NYC] Healthy Competition
> Somewhere in this city, someone is practicing Halal Rush at 2 a.m. to beat your score. Respect it.
