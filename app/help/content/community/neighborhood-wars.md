Every New Yorker reps a neighborhood. **Neighborhood Wars** is a friendly weekly competition between four teams. Every coin you earn at a [job](jobs/README.md) adds points to your team's score.

## The Teams {#the-teams}

| Team | Turf |
| --- | --- |
| **Downtown Slices** | SoHo, Chinatown and the Village. Cobblestones, dumplings, dollar slices. |
| **Midtown Lights** | Times Square, Broadway and Fifth. Big lights, bigger dreams. |
| **Uptown Acorns** | Central Park and Museum Mile. Squirrels, steps, and serious culture. |
| **Harbor Gulls** | Battery Park, Lady Liberty and the West Side. Ferries, sunsets, seagulls. |

## Joining a Team {#joining}

After your first paid shift at a job, you'll be asked to pick a neighborhood. You can also pick one any time from **Friends & Leaderboards** ([[F]]) on the **Neighborhoods** tab.

![The Neighborhoods tab, with this week's standings for the four teams and how many points you've added.](/help/img/community-neighborhoods.webp "Neighborhood Wars: this week's standings.")

Joining gives you a **team scarf** to wear. Pick carefully: there's no switching teams once you've picked.

## Scoring {#scoring}

- **Every coin you earn at a job** adds a point to your team for the week.
- The **Neighborhoods** tab shows this week's standings, how long until the reset, and how many points you've added.
- Online, the standings count every real player's points, plus a baseline from the city locals.

## The Weekly Winner {#weekly-winner}

The week resets every Monday. If your team won the week before:

- **50 coins** for you, with a note that your team won last week.

If another team won, you'll see who took it, and the new week starts fresh. You share in a team's win only for weeks you played in. Keep an eye on the Times Square billboards: they show which team is leading.

> [!NYC] Rivalry Report
> Downtown says Midtown is for tourists. Midtown says Uptown goes to bed at 9. Uptown says the Harbor smells like seagulls. The Harbor agrees, proudly.
