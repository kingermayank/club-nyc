Once you've finished your [first-day list](getting-started/first-day.md#new-in-town), the box at the top left of the screen becomes **Today in the City**: three small challenges for the day. Everyone in the city gets the same three, so you can compare notes.

![The Today in the City box at the top left, with three challenges and their progress bars.](/help/img/today-in-the-city.webp "Click the box's title to see all three challenges.")

## How It Works {#how-it-works}

- **Three challenges a day.** Click the box's title to see all three, with a progress bar for each.
- **15 coins and 15 XP** for each one you finish.
- **A 25 coin bonus** (and 30 XP) for finishing all three: "Overachiever."
- **New challenges** arrive at midnight New York time. The box shows how long until the next ones.
- **No pressure.** Unfinished challenges simply go away at midnight. Nothing is lost.

## Every Challenge {#every-challenge}

Each day's three are picked from this list:

| Challenge | How |
| --- | --- |
| Work 2 Shifts | Any [job](jobs/README.md) counts: pizza, halal, bagels, the ferry or the subway show. |
| Earn 100 Coins | Jobs, the Dance Jam, refills: it all adds up. |
| Visit 4 Different Places | Hop on the subway or click a taxi. |
| Say Hi in Chat 3 Times | Press [[Enter]], type, [[Enter]]. Be a good neighbor! |
| Dance at the Dance Jam | [Times Square](events/dance-jam.md), every 8 minutes. Get on the floor and press [[D]]. |
| Grab 2 NYC Snacks | Hot dogs, pretzels, slices, boba. A balanced diet. |
| Buy Something New to Wear | Supreme in SoHo, I ♥ NY in Times Square, or Fifth Avenue if you're feeling fancy. |
| Sign a Guestbook | Visit someone's apartment and leave a note. Compliment the square footage. |
| Share Your Invite Link | Phone, then Invite Friends ([[I]]). |
| Ride the Subway Twice | Click the MetroCard on your toolbar ([[U]]). |
| Start or Join a Flash Mob | Press [[D]] near other New Yorkers. |
| Take a Group Photo | Find a [photo booth](events/photo-booths.md): the I ♥ NY neon in Times Square, or Sky Deck 102. Everyone nearby squeezes in. |

## Your Week {#your-week}

Once you've dropped by on two or more days in a week, a little calendar chip in the box shows how many days you've visited (like 3/7). It's just for fun: your [daily refill](coins-and-shopping.md#daily-refill) grows with it, and nothing is ever taken away for missing a day.
