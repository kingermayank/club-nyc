You don't need an account to play. You can start as a guest and link one later, keeping everything.

## Before Launch: The Waitlist {#waitlist}

Before the city opens to everyone, pressing Play shows a **Coming Soon** page instead of the game.

1. Type your email and press **Join the Waitlist** (on the Coming Soon page, or on the [Club NYC home page](/#join)).
2. You may get an email asking you to **confirm** your spot. Click the link inside.
3. **That's it.** We'll email you the day the doors open.

You don't need an account to join, and every waitlist email has a one-click unsubscribe link. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html#waitlist) explains what we keep and why.

## The Front Door {#front-door}

When you open the game, the front door offers:

- **Play Now:** jump in as a guest. No sign-up. You'll make your New Yorker next.
- **Google**, **X** or **Discord:** sign in with an account you already have.
- **Continue With Email:** we email you a link that signs you in. No password needed.
- **Play Offline Instead:** play on your own, saved in this browser. [Playing offline](community/playing-online.md#offline)

Some sign-in options may not show up while we're still setting them up. Guest play always works.

## Guest Play {#guest-play}

When you press **Play Now**, you get a real New Yorker saved to the city, the same as everyone else's. The only difference: it's tied to **this browser**.

- Your New Yorker, coins, wardrobe and apartment are saved as you play.
- If you clear your browser's cookies or switch computers, a guest account can't be recovered.
- **To keep your New Yorker on every device, link an account.** It takes a few seconds and you keep everything.

## Link an Account {#link-an-account}

1. Open your player card ([[P]]) and click the **Account** tab.
2. Choose **Link Google**, **Link X**, **Link Discord** or **Link Email**.
3. Follow the sign-in steps. You come right back to the city with everything you had.

> [!WARNING] Already Used That Account?
> If the Google, X, Discord or email you pick is already linked to a different New Yorker, you'll be signed in as that New Yorker instead. Your guest stays behind in this browser.

## Signing In With Email {#email}

1. Click **Continue With Email** at the front door, type your email and click **Email Me a Link**.
2. Check your inbox (and spam folder). The link works **once**, for **15 minutes**.
3. Click it, and you're in.

Didn't get it? Click **Send It Again**, or **Use a Different Email** if there was a typo. If a link expired or was already used, ask for a fresh one.

## Your Account Tab {#account-tab}

The **Account** tab on your player card shows how you're signed in, your **New Yorker number** (your place in line when you joined the city), and how many days you've visited this week. It only shows when you're playing online.

## Signing Out {#signing-out}

If you've linked an account, the Account tab has a **Sign Out** button. Your progress is saved first, then you're back at the front door. Guests don't have a sign-out button, because signing out would leave the guest behind.

## Your Name {#your-name}

You choose your name when you make your New Yorker, and it's yours. You can't change it yourself. If a moderator resets a name that breaks the [rules](safety.md#names-and-outfits), you'll be asked to pick a new one.

## Age and Agreeing to the Rules {#age}

- Club NYC is for ages **13 and up**.
- We ask for your **birth month and year** once, to check. We don't keep them.
- You agree to the [Terms](/terms.html), [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) and [Community Rules](/rules.html) when you make your New Yorker. When they change in a way that matters, you'll be asked to agree again the next time you play.

## Playing on More Than One Device {#devices}

With a linked account, sign in on any computer and your New Yorker is there. You can be in the city in **one window at a time**: opening a second one asks "Playing somewhere else?" with **Play Here Instead**.

## Moving Offline Progress Online {#offline-to-online}

Played offline first? When you come online, your progress comes with you (up to 1,000 coins of it). You'll be asked for your birthday and to agree to the rules, and to pick a new name if yours is taken.

## Deleting Your Account {#delete}

1. Open your player card ([[P]]) and click the **Account** tab.
2. Click **Delete Account…**
3. Type your name to confirm, then click **Delete Forever**.

This permanently removes your New Yorker: coins, wardrobe, apartment, friends and scores. **It can't be undone.** Reports about rule-breaking and moderation records are kept for safety. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) has the details.

## Suspended or Banned {#suspended-or-banned}

If your account is suspended, the game tells you when you can come back. If it's banned, it can't play Club NYC anymore. Either way, you'll see the reason. Think it's a mistake? The [Community Rules](/rules.html#consequences) explain how to appeal.

> [!NYC] Security Tip
> Your MetroCard and your password have something in common: never hand either one to a stranger who says they're "with the city."
