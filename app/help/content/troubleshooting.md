Something not working? Find your problem below. Most fixes take a few seconds.

## Loading and Devices {#loading}

:::details The game won't load, or it's stuck on "Opening the city…"
1. **Refresh the page.**
2. **Check your internet connection.**
3. **Update your browser.** Club NYC works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
4. **Try turning off browser extensions** that block scripts or ads, then refresh.
5. **Try another browser.** If that works, the first one may need its cache cleared.
:::

:::details I pressed Play and it says "Coming Soon"
The city isn't open yet. Join the waitlist on that page and we'll email you the day the doors open. Got a preview code from the team? Use the link they sent you. [The Waitlist](account.md#waitlist)
:::

:::details It says "Best on a computer"
You're on a phone or a small tablet. Club NYC needs a keyboard and a bigger screen, so it's made for laptops and desktops. Use **Copy** or **Email It to Myself** to send the link to your computer, or **Remind Me Tonight**. If you'd like to try anyway, **Try It Anyway** is there too.
:::

:::details The game is slow or choppy
- Close other tabs and apps, especially video.
- Make sure your browser's hardware acceleration is turned on (in your browser's settings).
- Very busy places have more people to draw. Try a quieter one.
:::

:::details The game looks small or blurry
The city scales to fit your browser window. Make the window bigger, or use your browser's full-screen mode. Zooming the page with [[Ctrl]] and [[+]] can make things blurry, so reset it with [[Ctrl]] and [[0]] (⌘ on a Mac).
:::

## Connection {#connection}

:::details It says "Reconnecting…"
Your connection to the city dropped for a moment. The game reconnects by itself, so give it a few seconds. If it keeps happening, check your internet connection.
:::

:::details It says "The city is restarting" or "Back in a bit!"
We're updating the game or doing maintenance. You'll be reconnected automatically. Nothing you've done is lost.
:::

:::details It says "You were signed out"
Your sign-in expired. Click **Reload** and sign back in. Guests: if your browser's cookies were cleared, the guest account can't be recovered. [Link an account](account.md#link-an-account) to stop that from happening.
:::

:::details It says "Playing somewhere else?"
You opened Club NYC in another window or on another device, and only one can be in the city at a time. Click **Play Here Instead** to move to this one.
:::

## Controls {#controls}

:::details I can't click anything
A panel is probably open. Press [[Esc]] or click its red **X** to close it. If Rosa's tour is running, follow the step or click **Skip Tour**.
:::

:::details The keyboard shortcuts don't work
- If you're typing in the chat box, keys type letters instead. Press [[Esc]] to clear it, or click the city.
- If a panel or a job is open, most shortcuts wait until it's closed.
- Click the game once so the browser knows you're playing.
:::

:::details There's no sound
- Check the **Sound On/Off** button on the toolbar.
- Check that the browser tab isn't muted, and your computer's volume is up.
- Browsers don't play sound until you click something on the page. Click anywhere in the city.
:::

## Coins, Jobs and Stamps {#coins-jobs-stamps}

:::details I didn't get paid for a shift
- **Did you leave early?** Leaving a job with the red **X** doesn't pay.
- **Daily limit.** Job pay tops out at 1,500 coins a day. The results card tells you when you hit it.
- **"That run didn't count"** means the shift finished impossibly fast, or its score was higher than that job allows. That run pays nothing (the card shows 0 coins). Play it through normally and it pays.
- Coins arrive when you press **Play Again** or **Back to the City** on the results card.
:::

:::details I stopped earning coins
You've probably reached a daily limit. Coins start again at midnight New York time, and your scores and XP still count. [Daily Limits](coins-and-shopping.md#daily-limits)
:::

:::details A stamp didn't unlock
Check its hint in your Stamp Book: some need a specific number (like 15 pizzas in one shift), and party stamps only count while a party is on. [Every stamp and how to get it](stamps.md)
:::

:::details My snack disappeared
Were you at Battery Park? The seagulls are professionals. You still own it: put it back on from the **In Hand** section of your wardrobe.
:::

## Account {#account}

:::details My progress is gone
- **Guests:** your New Yorker is tied to the browser you made it in. A different browser, a private window or cleared cookies starts fresh. Go back to the original browser if you can, and [link an account](account.md#link-an-account) so it can't happen again.
- **Linked accounts:** make sure you're signing in with the same Google, X, Discord or email you linked.
:::

:::details My email sign-in link didn't arrive, or it doesn't work
- Check your spam folder.
- Links work once, for 15 minutes. If it expired, the front door asks for your email again: request a fresh one.
- Typo? Click **Use a Different Email**.
:::

:::details My name was taken, or it isn't allowed
Names are unique, 2 to 14 characters, and can only use letters, numbers and underscores. Spin the name wheels or add a number. Names also go through the chat filter. [Name Rules](getting-started/make-your-new-yorker.md#name-rules)
:::

:::details I can't chat
- You may be sending messages too fast: chat pauses for 30 seconds.
- A moderator may have paused your chat. The game tells you until when.
- If several players reported you, your chat pauses until a moderator takes a look.
:::

:::details I can't get into someone's apartment
Their door is closed. Friends can visit any time; everyone else needs an Open House or a public party. Add them as a friend, or wait for an open house. [Visiting](apartment/visitors-and-guestbooks.md#visiting)
:::
