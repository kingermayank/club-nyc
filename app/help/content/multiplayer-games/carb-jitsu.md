Every pizzeria in the city has one table where the big arguments get settled. At the [99¢ Slice](places/chinatown-and-little-italy.md#99c-slice), it's Carb-Jitsu: two players, a hand of five cards each, one very serious food fight.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

- **Quick Match** or **Create Lobby** on the **Games** tab ([[F]]), or a friend's code or link. [Finding a Game](multiplayer-games/README.md#finding-a-game)
- The chalkboard by the window table at the 99¢ Slice.
- Nobody around? **Play a Local**, and a city local sits down across from you.

## The Rules {#the-rules}

1. **Both players pick a card at the same time** from a hand of 5: click it, or press [[1]] to [[5]]. You have 15 seconds; if the clock runs out, it picks for you.
2. **The cards flip together. Food beats food:**
   - **Pizza beats Pretzel** (it's a whole meal).
   - **Pretzel beats Bagel** (knots beat holes).
   - **Bagel beats Pizza** (breakfast comes first).
3. **Same food?** The higher number (2 to 12) wins. Equal numbers tie.
4. **The winner keeps the card** on their tray. Every card also has a borough color: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx or Staten Island.
5. **Win the match** with three of one food from three different boroughs, or one of each food from three different boroughs.

### Special Cards {#special-cards}

Specials only work when their card wins the round:

| Card | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Rent Control** | Next round, the lower number wins. |
| **Rent Hike** | Their next card is worth 3 less. |
| **Tow Truck** | Tows away one of their tray cards from the same borough. |

## Winner Stays On {#winner-stays-on}

Up to 6 people can sit at a table. The winner keeps the seat, the next in line plays them, and everyone else watches. Letting the clock play 4 rounds in a row, or staying disconnected for 30 seconds, forfeits the match.
