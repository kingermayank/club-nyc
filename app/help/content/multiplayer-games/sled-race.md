Race down Pilgrim Hill against up to three other sledders, or the city locals when nobody's around. A wipeout costs you a second, never the race, so it's never over until the bottom.

## Where to Play {#where-to-play}

- **Quick Match** or **Create Lobby** on the **Games** tab ([[F]]), or a friend's code or link. [Finding a Game](multiplayer-games/README.md#finding-a-game)
- The signpost to Pilgrim Hill on the [Central Park](places/central-park.md) lawn.

## How a Race Goes {#how-a-race-goes}

- **The hill pulls you along** at cruising speed. You steer and hop.
- **Cocoa cups** give you a speed boost (some float in the air after ramps). **Ice** is free speed. **Deep snow**, bumps and snowmen slow you down.
- **Rocks, logs, pine trees, boulders and the cart** make you wipe out for about a second, then you're off again. Nobody is ever knocked out.
- **Every race is a new hill**, built from stretches like the Rock Garden, the Log Jam (aim for the gap), the Slalom, the Moguls, the Ice Rink, Big Air (everyone launches off the ramp), the Pine Grove, Snowman Row and the Narrows. A banner tells you what's coming.
- **The Runaway Cart.** Sal's hot dog cart got away from him at the top. It's still going, and it honks.
- **Falling behind?** Ride right behind another sledder for a **slipstream**, and if you're far behind the leader you get a little **tailwind**.

The first sledder to the bottom wins. Once someone finishes, everyone else has 20 seconds; anyone still on the hill gets "Didn't finish" and is placed by how far they got.

## Modes {#modes}

- **Free-for-All:** every sledder for themselves.
- **Uptown vs Downtown:** two teams, points by place, tubes in team colors.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> The real Pilgrim Hill, by the East 72nd Street entrance, is where New Yorkers have sledded for generations. The Pilgrim statue at the top has been there since 1885 and has seen every wipeout since. Lunch trays work as sleds. Nobody knows why.
