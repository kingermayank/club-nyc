Race up the High Line on a Citi Bike against up to three other riders, or the city locals when nobody's around. A wipeout costs you about a second, never the race, so it's never over until 34th St.

> [!NOTE] Looking for the Pilgrim Hill Sled Race?
> It moved to the High Line when the snow didn't come. Your best times, wins and stamps came with it.

## How a Race Goes {#how-a-race-goes}

- **You ride north**, from the start line at Gansevoort St to the finish at 34th St. Street signs count off the blocks.
- **Your bike keeps you at cruising speed.** You steer and hop.
- **Iced coffee** gives you a speed boost (some float in the air after the ramp). **Fresh pavement** is free speed. The **planting beds**, the **café tables** in the Chelsea Market Passage, old rails and selfie lines slow you down.
- **Tourists, the park ranger, loungers, birch trees, planters and the cart** make you wipe out for about a second, then you're off again. Nobody is ever knocked out.
- **Every race is a new ride**, built from stretches like the Tour Group (hop the pigeons, steer round the tourists), the Planters (aim for the gap), Old Rail Tracks, Fresh Pavement, the Birch Grove and the Runaway Gelato Cart. A banner tells you what's coming.
- **Some stretches are always where they really are:** the **Sundeck** at 14th St (weave between the rolling loungers, splash through the water feature), the **Chelsea Market Passage** (stay in the middle), **10th Ave Square** at 17th St (find the gap in the selfie line), and **the Spur** at 30th St, where somebody left a ramp.
- **The Runaway Gelato Cart.** It got away from its owner at the 14th St entrance. It's still going, and it rings its bell.
- **Falling behind?** Ride right behind another rider for a **slipstream**, and if you're far behind the leader you get a little **tailwind** off the Hudson.

The first rider to 34th St wins. Once someone finishes, everyone else has 20 seconds; anyone still riding gets "Didn't finish" and is placed by how far they got.

## Modes {#modes}

- **Free-for-All:** every rider for themselves.
- **Uptown vs Downtown:** two teams, points by place, pennants in team colors.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> The High Line was built in 1934 to lift freight trains off Tenth Avenue, which New Yorkers called Death Avenue. The last train ran in 1980 with three cars of frozen turkeys. It reopened as a park in 2009, rails and all. Bikes are not allowed. Nobody told these racers.
