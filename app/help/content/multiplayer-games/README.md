The [jobs](jobs/README.md) are solo. These three games are for playing **with each other**: short rounds of one to two minutes with whoever's around, and a rematch one click away. Nobody around? City locals fill the empty seats, so you can always play.

:::cards
- [Pilgrim Hill Sled Race](multiplayer-games/sled-race.md) {img:/help/img/mp-sled.webp} 2 to 4 sledders down Central Park's favorite hill.
- [Rush Hour Run](multiplayer-games/rush-hour-run.md) {img:/help/img/mp-runner.webp} Up to 9 runners on a rush-hour subway platform.
- [Carb-Jitsu](multiplayer-games/carb-jitsu.md) {img:/help/img/mp-carbs.webp} Pizza beats Pretzel. Pretzel beats Bagel. Bagel beats Pizza.
:::

## Finding a Game {#finding-a-game}

You can join from anywhere:

- **The Games tab.** Press [[F]] (or **Friends & Leaderboards** on the toolbar) and open **Games**.
- **A sign in the city.** Click a game's sign to walk over and open its card. While you're nearby, it shows who's waiting.
- **A code.** Got a 4 letter code from a friend? Type it into **Got a Code From a Friend?** on the Games tab and press **Join**.
- **A link or an invite.** A friend's game link (or an invite notice with **Join**) takes you straight to their lobby.

![The Games tab in the Community panel: three game cards with Quick Match, Create Lobby and Leaderboard buttons, and a box for a friend's code.](/help/img/mp-games-tab.webp "The Games tab: play with whoever's around, or with the city locals.")

### Quick Match or Create Lobby {#quick-match-or-create-lobby}

- **Quick Match** drops you into an open public lobby (one with your friends first, then the busiest), or opens a new one. It starts by itself: 15 seconds after someone else joins, or 25 seconds if you're on your own (city locals fill in). If everyone presses **Ready**, it starts right away.
- **Create Lobby** opens an invite-only lobby and makes you the host. You choose **Free-for-All** or **Uptown vs Downtown**, **City Locals On** or **Off**, **Public** or **Invite Only**, and when to start. The host can also remove someone. If the host leaves, the longest-seated player takes over.

## The Lobby {#the-lobby}

![A Pilgrim Hill Sled Race lobby: the code and Copy Link at the top, the players and open seats, how to play, the settings, Start with Locals, and quick reactions along the bottom.](/help/img/mp-lobby.webp "A lobby: the code, the seats, how to play, and a big button that says what it does.")

- **The code and Copy Link** at the top are for your friends. **Invite** sends online friends a notice with a **Join** button.
- **The big button** always says what it does: **Start Race**, **Ready**, **Take a Seat**, **Play Again**. [[Enter]] presses it.
- **Start with Locals** starts right now, with city locals in the empty seats.
- **Reactions and phrases** (👋 🔥 😂 👏, "GG!", "One More?", "So Close!", "Rematch!") pop up over your seat. No typing needed.
- **Hide** keeps your seat and shrinks the lobby to a chip at the top of the city, so you can walk around. It pops back up when your round starts.
- **Leave** is always on screen. [[Esc]] leaves a lobby, and in the middle of a race it asks first.

### Joining Late, Leaving, and Rematches {#late-and-rematches}

- **Arrived mid-round?** You watch, then get a seat for the next round.
- **After a match**, everyone who played sees the results and sits out until they press **Play Again**, so nobody gets pulled into a race mid-read.
- **Dropped connection?** Your lobby seat waits 25 seconds for you, and mid-race you have 12 seconds to come back.

## Prizes {#prizes}

The city decides every result, so places, prizes and leaderboards are always fair. Everyone who plays a match through gets coins and XP. Leaving early pays nothing, and a match that ends too quickly doesn't count.

| Result | With other players | Against city locals only |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| 1st place | 20 coins | 8 coins |
| 2nd place | 12 coins | 4 coins |
| 3rd place | 8 coins | 4 coins |
| Everyone else | 5 coins | 4 coins |
| Uptown vs Downtown: win / lose | 15 / 6 coins | 8 / 4 coins |
| Carb-Jitsu: win / lose | 15 / 5 coins | 6 / 3 coins |

- **XP:** 20 for playing a match through, plus 30 for 1st, 15 for 2nd or 5 for 3rd when you have rivals. A winning team counts as 1st. [Levels & XP](levels-and-xp.md)
- **Daily limit:** game prizes top out at 300 coins a day, separate from job pay.

## Leaderboards {#leaderboards}

Every game has its own board, for **wins** and for **best score**, this week (resets Monday) or all time, for everyone or just your friends. You'll find it with the **Leaderboard** button in a lobby, the top 5 on the results screen, and on the Games tab. A win only counts against a real New Yorker. Best scores always count.

## Making Friends at the Table {#friends}

Everyone in a lobby and on the results screen has an **Add Friend** button. **Copy Result** copies a brag with your lobby link, for your group chat.
