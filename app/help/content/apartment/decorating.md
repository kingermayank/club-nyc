Decorating happens right in your apartment, with your furniture in a tray at the bottom of the screen.

## How to Decorate {#how-to-decorate}

![Decorating mode: furniture in a tray along the bottom of the screen, ready to drag into the apartment.](/help/img/decorate.webp "Click an item to place it, drag to move, double-click to flip.")

:::steps
1. **Go home** ([[H]]) and click **Decorate**.
2. **Place it.** Click a piece in the tray. It appears in your apartment.
3. **Move it.** Drag it where you want it.
4. **Flip it.** Double-click to mirror it left or right.
5. **Put it away.** Drag it down into the tray to send it back to storage.
6. **Done.** Click **Done** when it looks right.
:::

> [!TIP] Tight Squeeze
> Furniture has to sit on the floor. Drop something where it doesn't fit and it goes back to storage ("Classic New York apartment"). Rugs can go anywhere.

There's no limit on how much you can place, but visitors see the first 40 pieces. Nobody needs more than 40 pieces in a walk-up. Nobody needs more than 4.

## Furniture That Does Things {#furniture-that-does-things}

When you're not decorating, click your furniture:

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Sofas, chairs, benches, beds | Sit (or lie) down. Guests can too. |
| Record Player | Drops the needle on some music. |
| Jukebox | Music, and an invitation to dance (press [[D]]). |
| Retro TV | Turns on and off. |
| Lamps and the Neon Sign | Light up. |
| Bodega Cat Bed | Purrs. |
| Arcade Cabinet | "One more game…" Starts Slice Factory, Ferry Captain or Bagel Counters at random. Same pay as the real thing. |
