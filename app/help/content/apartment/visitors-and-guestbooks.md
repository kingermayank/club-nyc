Apartments are better with visitors. Here's how to drop by someone else's place, and how to let people into yours.

## Visiting Someone's Place {#visiting}

- **From their player card:** click a New Yorker, then **Visit Their Place**.
- **From your friends list:** press [[F]], open **Friends**, and click the home button next to a friend.

Who can come in:

| Whose place | When you can visit |
| --- | --- |
| A friend's | Any time, even when they're offline. |
| Anyone else's | During their **Open House** or a public **party**. Otherwise their door is closed: add them as a friend, or wait for an open house. |
| A city local's | Any time. They'll even say hi. |

While you're visiting, the buttons at the top let you **Sign Guestbook**, **Like** their place, or **Go Home**.

## Signing a Guestbook {#signing}

Click **Sign Guestbook** and pick a quick note, or **write your own** (up to 120 characters). Your note is waiting for them the next time they're home. You can leave one note per apartment per hour. Keep it kind: notes follow the [community rules](safety.md).

> [!TIP] Easy Daily Challenge
> "Sign a Guestbook" is one of the [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) challenges. Any apartment counts, including a city local's.

## Your Guestbook {#your-guestbook}

Click **Guestbook** at home to read the notes people left you. A badge shows how many are new. If you've been out a while and your place is furnished, you might come home to a few notes from the neighbors.

## Open House {#open-house}

Click **Open House** at home to open your door. The button switches to **Open House: ON**.

- **Anyone can visit** while it's on, not just friends.
- **City locals drop by** every so often, look around, and often leave a note and a like.
- Click it again to close the door.

While it's on, the place name at the top left says **Open House**, for you and your visitors.

## Likes {#likes}

Visitors can **like** your place once each. The heart button at home shows your total. It's the closest thing Club NYC has to a five-star review, minus the complaint about the radiator.

## Stamps {#stamps}

| Stamp | How |
| --- | --- |
| House Call | Visit a friend's apartment. |
| Dear Diary | Sign a friend's guestbook. |

> [!NYC] Guestbook Etiquette
> "Great place! So much natural light!" is the correct thing to write, regardless of the amount of natural light.
