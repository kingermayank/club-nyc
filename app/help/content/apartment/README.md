Every New Yorker gets a home: a charming (tiny) 5th-floor walk-up in the East Village, with exposed brick, a radiator with opinions and a view of three water towers. It's free, it's yours, and nobody can raise the rent.

![Your 5th-floor walk-up, with the apartment buttons along the top: Decorate, Catalog, Guestbook, Open House, likes, Party and Listings.](/help/img/apartment.webp "Home sweet (tiny) home.")

## Getting Home {#getting-home}

- Press [[H]], or click **My Apartment** on the toolbar.
- On the [map](getting-started/map-and-subway.md#city-map), click **My Place**.
- To leave, click the **Leave** door. The map opens so you can pick where to go.

Your first time home, you'll find three housewarming gifts in storage: a Bean Bag, a Monstera and a Floor Lamp. Hit **Decorate** to place them.

## Your Apartment Buttons {#buttons}

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Decorate** | Place, move and flip your furniture. [Decorating](apartment/decorating.md) |
| **Catalog** | Buy furniture from the Flatbush Flea catalog. |
| **Guestbook** | Read notes visitors left you. A badge counts new ones. [Visitors & Guestbooks](apartment/visitors-and-guestbooks.md) |
| **Open House** | Let friends (and anyone passing by) drop in while you're home. |
| **Likes** | How many visitors have liked your place. |
| **Party** | Throw a house party with music and lights. [House Parties](apartment/house-parties.md) |
| **Listings** | StreetCheesy, for when you're ready to move. [Moving Homes](apartment/moving-homes.md) |

## Around the Walk-Up {#around-the-walk-up}

Your apartment has its own little stories. Click around:

- **The window:** water towers, fire escapes and a slice of the Empire State. Open it and a pigeon moves in.
- **The radiator:** it clanks. Bang on the pipe and your neighbor bangs back.
- **The stove:** for storing sweaters, as is New York tradition. Or **Cook Something** (toast, in a pan).

### Your Fridge {#your-fridge}

Click the **Fridge · Magnets** to see your magnets. Every magnet you buy at the [Brooklyn Bridge souvenir stand](places/brooklyn-bridge.md#magnets) ends up on your fridge door, nine in all. Missing ones show what's left to find. (The fridge in the loft works too.)

## Things to Know {#things-to-know}

- **Your home is private** unless you open it up. Friends can visit any time; everyone else only during an Open House or a public party.
- **Each home keeps its own furniture.** If you move, the old place stays decorated, and your storage comes with you.
- **No city locals wander in** uninvited, and there are no coins on your floor. (It's an apartment, not a subway platform.)
