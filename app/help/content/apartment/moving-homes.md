When the walk-up starts feeling small (it will, it's tiny), check the listings on **StreetCheesy**: cheesy listings, real views, no broker fee.

## Opening the Listings {#opening-listings}

Go home ([[H]]) and click **Listings**.

![StreetCheesy listings: the walk-up, the Cast-Iron Loft and the Billionaires' Row Penthouse, with prices and perks.](/help/img/streetcheesy.webp "StreetCheesy. Cheesy listings, real views.")

## Buying and Moving {#buying-and-moving}

1. Click a listing, then **Sign the Papers** to buy it. (Changed your mind? **Keep Looking**.)
2. You move in right away and earn the **New Digs** stamp.
3. Homes you already own say **Move In**. The one you live in says **You Live Here**.

Not enough coins yet? StreetCheesy tells you exactly how many more you need. [Earning coins](coins-and-shopping.md#earning-coins)

> [!NOTE] What Happens to Your Furniture
> Each home keeps its own furniture where you placed it, so moving back later is easy. Your storage (furniture you haven't placed) comes with you to every home.
