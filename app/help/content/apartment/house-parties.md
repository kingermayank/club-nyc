Every apartment can throw a party, from the walk-up to the penthouse terrace (which was built for it). Parties are free to throw.

## Throwing a Party {#throwing-a-party}

![The party panel: the party song, three vibes, who's invited, and a Start the Party button.](/help/img/house-party.webp "Your place, your party.")

:::steps
1. **Go home** ([[H]]). Parties only happen at your place.
2. **Click Party.** (In the penthouse, the DJ Nook opens it too.)
3. **Pick a vibe.** It changes the lights.
4. **Choose who's invited:** **Friends**, or **Everyone in the City**.
5. **Click Start the Party.**
:::

### Music {#music}

Every party plays **Empire State of Mind** by JAY-Z featuring Alicia Keys. Everyone at the party hears it from the same spot in the song.

### Vibes {#vibes}

| Vibe | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Rooftop Glow | String lights, golden hour |
| Disco Fever | A mirror ball and colored spots |
| Bodega Rave | Neon green and pink |

During a [seasonal party](events/seasonal-parties.md), a seasonal vibe joins the list.

## During the Party {#during-the-party}

- **You're the DJ.** A chip shows what's playing. Open the party panel again to switch the vibe or who's invited, **Invite With a Link**, or **End the Party**.
- **Your friends get a heads-up** that you're throwing a party, with a **Go** button to come over.
- **City locals drop by** while the party's small ("Heard there's a party!").
- Guests dance, chat and hear the same music you do.

## Host Tips {#host-tips}

Keep your party going for **90 seconds** with **3 or more guests** (city locals count) and you earn:

- **Party host tips: 30 coins** (up to twice a day)
- **20 XP** (up to twice a day)
- The **Host with the Most** stamp, the first time

Guests earn the **Plus One** stamp just for showing up.
