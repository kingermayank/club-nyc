Club NYC has its own words, and New York has a lot of its own too. Here's a quick guide to both.

## Game Words {#game-words}

### City Local {#city-local}

A computer-run New Yorker who lives in the city. Locals have names, homes, favorite spots and opinions. They wave back, join flash mobs, visit open houses and can be your friends. As more real players arrive, they give up their spots.

### Coins {#coins}

Club NYC's money, shown at the top right. Earned in the game, never bought. [Coins & Shopping](coins-and-shopping.md)

### Dance Jam {#dance-jam}

A 3 minute dance party in Times Square every 8 minutes. [Dance Jam](events/dance-jam.md)

### Flash Mob {#flash-mob}

What happens when you start dancing and the New Yorkers around you join in. [Flash Mobs](events/flash-mobs.md)

### Job {#job}

A mini-game that pays coins, like Slice Factory or Halal Rush. One play is a **shift**. [Jobs](jobs/README.md)

### Lobby Code {#lobby-code}

The 4 letter code for a multiplayer game lobby, like "K7QX". It leaves out letters and numbers that are easy to mix up (no 0, O, 1, I or L), so it survives being read out loud. [Multiplayer Games](multiplayer-games/README.md)

### Level {#level}

Your rank in the city, set by your XP. Each level has a title, from Tourist to Club NYC Legend. [Levels & XP](levels-and-xp.md)

### MetroCard {#metrocard}

Your Club NYC ID card, made when you join, and the subway button on your toolbar. Your daily refill tops it up with coins.

### New in Town {#new-in-town}

Your first-day list: five steps worth 110 coins. [Your First Day](getting-started/first-day.md#new-in-town)

### Neighborhood Wars {#neighborhood-wars}

The weekly competition between four neighborhood teams. [Neighborhood Wars](community/neighborhood-wars.md)

### Open House {#open-house}

When your apartment door is open to anyone, not just friends. [Open House](apartment/visitors-and-guestbooks.md#open-house)

### Player Card {#player-card}

The card that opens when you click a New Yorker: their name, level, team and buttons like Add Friend, Visit Their Place and Report. Your own card has your wardrobe. Press [[P]].

### Rosa {#rosa}

Your building's super. She gives you the tour and writes you postcards. She also knows about the elevator. Don't ask about the elevator.

### Stamp {#stamp}

An achievement for doing something very New York, collected in your Stamp Book. [Stamps](stamps.md)

### StreetCheesy {#streetcheesy}

The listings app for buying a new home. Cheesy listings, real views. [Moving Homes](apartment/moving-homes.md)

### Today in the City {#today-in-the-city}

Three small challenges a day, the same for everyone. [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md)

### XP {#xp}

Experience points. Everything you do earns them, they only go up, and they set your level. [Levels & XP](levels-and-xp.md)

## New York Words {#new-york-words}

### Bodega {#bodega}

A corner store that sells everything: sandwiches, coffee, lottery tickets, phone chargers. Usually has a cat. The cat is in charge.

### Bodega Cat {#bodega-cat}

The cat in charge of the bodega. In Club NYC, the bodega cat writes the advice column in the Club NYC Times.

### Bullet {#bullet}

The colored circle with a letter or number on subway signs. Each color is a trunk line.

### Chopped Cheese {#chopped-cheese}

A bodega sandwich of chopped beef, onions and melted cheese on a hero roll. A local legend, like Pizza Rat but delicious.

### Fold {#fold}

How you eat a New York slice: fold it in half lengthwise. Using a fork and knife causes a scene.

### Halal Cart {#halal-cart}

A street cart serving chicken or lamb over rice with white sauce and hot sauce. The line is the sign of a good one.

### Pizza Rat {#pizza-rat}

A real-life subway celebrity who once dragged an entire slice down the stairs. Legends say he's still out there.

### Schmear {#schmear}

A generous spread of cream cheese on a bagel. Ordering "a schmear" is very New York.

### Showtime {#showtime}

What subway dancers shout before performing on a moving train. Stand back and hold onto the pole.

### Stoop {#stoop}

The front steps of a building. Stoop sitting is a New York pastime, and Level 6 in Club NYC.

### Straphanger {#straphanger}

A subway rider, from the days when standing passengers held on to leather straps. Also a stamp.

### Walk-Up {#walk-up}

An apartment building without an elevator. Your first Club NYC home is on the 5th floor of one. Free gym.

> [!NYC] Bonus Word
> **"Fuggedaboutit."** Means yes, no, forget it, of course, and "this pizza is incredible," depending on tone. Use with confidence.
