Stamps are Club NYC's achievements: little proof-you-did-it badges for very New York things, from buying a dollar slice to captaining the ferry. They live in your **Stamp Book**.

## Your Stamp Book {#stamp-book}

Click **Stamp Book** on the toolbar.

![The Stamp Book, with a cover showing your stamp count and a page of stamps, some earned and some still marked with question marks.](/help/img/stamp-book.webp "Stamps you haven't earned yet show a ? and a hint.")

- The cover shows how many you've collected.
- Stamps are sorted into three pages: **Around Town** (easy), **Explorer** (a bit of effort) and **Experts** (bring your A game).
- A stamp you haven't earned yet shows **???**, but its hint is always there.

## Stamp Rewards {#rewards}

- **25 XP** for every new stamp. [Levels & XP](levels-and-xp.md)
- **25 coins** for every 5 stamps you collect.
- **A Paper Party Hat** when you collect your 5th stamp.

> When the city throws a [seasonal party](events/seasonal-parties.md), party stamps appear in your book while it's on. Snow-season stamps come back when it's cold enough for snowballs.
