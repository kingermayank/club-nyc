There's always something going on in Club NYC. Some events run on the city's clock, and some you start yourself.

:::cards
- [Dance Jam](events/dance-jam.md) {icon:disco-ball} Every 8 minutes in Times Square. Dance for 20 seconds, earn 20 coins.
- [Flash Mobs](events/flash-mobs.md) {icon:dancer} Start dancing anywhere, and the city joins in.
- [House Parties](apartment/house-parties.md) {icon:party-popper} Your place, your playlist, your guests.
- [Photo Booths](events/photo-booths.md) {icon:camera} Squeeze in with everyone nearby and save the strip.
- [The Club NYC Times](events/club-nyc-times.md) {icon:newspaper} News, advice from the bodega cat, and this week's leaders.
- [Seasonal Parties](events/seasonal-parties.md) {icon:pumpkin} The city throws a party now and then. Free costumes included.
:::

## What's On and When {#schedule}

| Event | When | Where | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Dance Jam](events/dance-jam.md) | Every 8 minutes, for 3 minutes | Times Square | 20 coins, 10 XP, the Dance Jam stamp |
| [Flash Mob](events/flash-mobs.md) | Whenever you start one | Anywhere | Flash Mob and Times Square Takeover stamps |
| [House Party](apartment/house-parties.md) | Whenever you throw one | Your apartment | 30 coins and 20 XP for the host, stamps for everyone |
| [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) | New challenges every day | Everywhere | Up to 70 coins a day |
| [Neighborhood Wars](community/neighborhood-wars.md) | All week, resets Monday | Every job | 50 coins for the winning team |
| [Seasonal Parties](events/seasonal-parties.md) | A few times a year | The party's place | Free costumes, party stamps |

> [!NYC] Event Planning, New York Style
> Every event in New York is either "a whole thing" or "not a whole thing." The Dance Jam is a whole thing.
