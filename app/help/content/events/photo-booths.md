Photo booths live in a few spots around the city. Step in, and the New Yorkers next to you squeeze into the frame.

![A finished photo-booth strip with four pictures of New Yorkers posing, and Save Photo and Done buttons.](/help/img/photo-booth.webp "Four poses: Smile!, Silly!, Squeeze In! and Big Finish!")

## Where to Find One {#where}

| Booth | Where | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I ♥ NY** | [I ♥ NY Souvenirs](places/times-square.md#i-love-ny-souvenirs), Times Square | Click the **I ♥ NY · Photo Booth** neon, then **Pose Under the Neon** |
| **Sky Deck 102** | [One World Trade](places/one-world-trade.md#sky-deck-102), 102nd floor | Click the **Sky Deck Photo Booth** |

Each booth has its own frame and stickers. During a [seasonal party](events/seasonal-parties.md), a party booth may open too.

## Taking a Strip {#taking-a-strip}

:::steps
1. **Step in.** Up to three New Yorkers near you join the picture: city locals, and real players who are your friends.
2. **Click Take 4 Photos.** The booth counts down four poses: **Smile!**, **Silly!**, **Squeeze In!** and **Big Finish!**
3. **Keep it.** **Save Photo** downloads the strip as a picture. Where your browser supports it, **Share** sends it straight to an app. **Done** closes the booth.
:::

Your first strip earns the **Say Cheese** [stamp](stamps.md), and "Take a Group Photo" in [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) counts it.

> [!TIP] Bring Your Crew
> Want your friends in the picture? Add them as [friends](community/friends.md) first, then stand together by the booth.

> [!NYC] Photo Etiquette
> In a New York photo booth, everyone squeezes in, nobody's ready, and the best picture is always the one where someone blinked.
