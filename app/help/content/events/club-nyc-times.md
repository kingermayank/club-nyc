All the news that fits, and some that doesn't. **The Club NYC Times** comes out every day, and it's the same paper for everyone in the city.

## Reading the Paper {#reading}

Press [[N]] or click **Club NYC Times** on the toolbar. Reading it for the first time earns the **Newshound** [stamp](stamps.md).

![The front page of the Club NYC Times, with today's headline, a picture, and the Inside box listing the sections.](/help/img/newspaper.webp "Today's paper. Still 25¢.")

- **Today's edition** opens first. Scroll down to read the whole paper.
- **Inside**, on the front page, jumps straight to a section.
- **Previous Edition** and **Next Edition** flip through back issues, one day at a time, all the way back to No. 1.

## What's Inside {#whats-inside}

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Front Page** | The day's big story, and what's inside. |
| **NYC Moments** | The stories New Yorkers still bring up at brunch, with "On This Day" when something real happened on today's date. |
| **Metro & Style** | City news, from transit miracles to real estate listings that call a closet "cozy". |
| **Sports** | Neighborhood Wars standings and this week's top scores at every job, live in today's paper. |
| **Opinion** | **Ask the Bodega Cat**, the city's most respected (and only) feline advice columnist. |
| **Classifieds** | Apartments, help wanted, missed connections, and the occasional secret about the city. |

> [!TIP] Read the Classifieds
> The paper has a way of mentioning things in the city that are worth checking out. Some of them might even be useful.

## Back Issues {#back-issues}

Every day has its own edition, and stories never repeat back to back. Back issues keep the sports standings as they were that day, so you can look up how your team was doing last Tuesday.

> [!NYC] From the Editor
> The Club NYC Times regrets nothing, except the time we called a bagel "toasted" on the front page. We have apologized to the bagel.
