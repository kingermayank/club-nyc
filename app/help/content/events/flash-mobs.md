In Club NYC, a flash mob is one dance move away. Start dancing and see who joins.

## Starting a Flash Mob {#starting}

:::steps
1. **Stand near other New Yorkers.** Anywhere works: a busy street, a park, a shop.
2. **Press [[D]]** (or **Moves**, then **Dance**) and keep still. Don't sit down.
3. **Watch them join.** City locals nearby start dancing, and real players who are dancing nearby count too. A badge above you shows how many are in.
:::

| Dancers | What happens |
| :---: | --- |
| 3 | "A flash mob is forming! Keep dancing…" You earn the **Flash Mob** stamp. |
| 6 (Times Square) | "TIMES SQUARE TAKEOVER! The whole square is dancing!" You earn the **Times Square Takeover** stamp, and 6 tip coins drop to pick up. |
| 7 (anywhere else) | "The whole block is dancing!" with 6 tip coins to pick up. |

The mob ends when you stop dancing, move or leave. You'll hear how many joined: "What a flash mob: 7 dancers!"

> [!TIP] Locals Love to Dance
> City locals sometimes start it for you: "Dance with me! Press D". Dancing with a local also helps them warm up to being your [friend](community/friends.md).

## Stamps {#stamps}

| Stamp | How |
| --- | --- |
| Flash Mob | Start a dance with 3+ New Yorkers. |
| Times Square Takeover | Get 6 people dancing in Times Square at once. |

"Start or Join a Flash Mob" is also one of the [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) challenges.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> When a big enough crowd dances in Times Square, the billboards dance too. You didn't hear it from us.
