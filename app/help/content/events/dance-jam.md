The Dance Jam runs on the city's clock: a 3 minute jam every 8 minutes, the same time for everyone. It's the easiest 20 coins in the city.

![The Dance Jam in Times Square: New Yorkers and city locals dancing together in the middle of the plaza.](/help/img/dance-jam.webp "The Dance Jam, every 8 minutes in Times Square.")

## How to Join {#how-to-join}

:::steps
1. **Be in [Times Square](places/times-square.md)** when a jam starts. You'll see "DANCE JAM! Get to the middle of the plaza and dance (D)".
2. **Click Join the Dance Jam** (it shows how much time is left). Your New Yorker walks to the dance floor and starts dancing.
3. **Keep dancing for 20 seconds** in the middle of the plaza. Press [[D]] if you stop.
4. **Tips!** You earn **20 coins** and the **Dance Jam** stamp.
:::

City locals crowd in when the jam starts, so you're never dancing alone.

## Rewards {#rewards}

| Reward | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| **20 coins** ("Dance Jam tips!") | Once per jam, up to 8 jams a day |
| **10 XP** | Up to 8 a day |
| **Dance Jam** stamp | The first time |
| A [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) challenge | When "Dance at the Dance Jam" is one of today's three |

## When's the Next One? {#next-one}

- Jams start every 8 minutes and last 3 minutes, on the same clock for everyone in the city.
- Now and then, DJ Cadenza sends a [postcard](community/postcards-and-notices.md) when a jam is about to start, with a button that takes you to Times Square.
- Nothing follows you around the city counting down. If you miss one, another starts in a few minutes.

> [!TIP] Make It a Takeover
> Get 6 New Yorkers dancing in Times Square at once for the **Times Square Takeover** stamp. During a jam, that's the easiest it'll ever be. [Flash Mobs](events/flash-mobs.md)

> [!NYC] Dance Floor Rules
> There are no rules. There is one guy who does the worm every single time. Let him have this.
