## What Is Club NYC? {#what-is-club-nyc}

Club NYC is a cozy online New York you play in your web browser. You make a New Yorker, explore Manhattan from Times Square to Liberty Island, work silly jobs for coins, decorate a tiny apartment and hang out with real people. It's free, there's nothing to download, and there's nothing to buy: coins are only ever earned in the game.

If you remember waddling around an island, meeting strangers, playing mini-games and finding secret rooms, you already know the vibe. Just swap the snow for subway steam.

> [!NYC] A Word From the Pigeons
> The city is open 24/7. The pigeons are open 24/7. Nobody has ever seen a pigeon sleep, and frankly we'd rather not know.
