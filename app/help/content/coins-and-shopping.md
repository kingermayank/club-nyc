Coins are Club NYC's money. You start with 50, and everything you do around the city can earn more. Your balance is always at the top right of the screen.

> [!IMPORTANT] Coins Can't Be Bought
> There's nothing to buy in Club NYC with real money, and coins can't be bought, sold or traded. Anyone offering "free coins" for your password is running a scam. [Report them](safety.md#report-a-player).

> [!TIP] The Fastest Way to Earn
> **Jobs.** A good shift pays far more than anything else. Pick the one you enjoy most and climb its leaderboard. On a busy day, add the Dance Jam and the three daily challenges.

### The Daily Refill {#daily-refill}

The first time you drop by the city each day (New York time), your MetroCard gets a free refill. It grows with each day you visit in the same week:

| Visit this week | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Refill | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 | 35 | 40 |

It's **not a streak**: missing a day never takes anything away, the count just doesn't go up. Every week starts fresh on Monday. Visiting all seven days in a week earns the **Regular** stamp, and visiting on 30 different days earns **True New Yorker**.

### Daily Limits {#daily-limits}

So nobody can farm coins with a script, the city has daily limits (New York time). They're far above what a keen player earns:

- **Job pay:** up to 1,500 coins a day.
- **Multiplayer game prizes:** up to 300 coins a day.
- **Everything else** (challenges, tips, refills, bonuses): up to 600 coins a day.

If you reach a limit, you'll see a note like "That's all the coins the city hands out today. Even New York has a budget." Your scores and XP still count. Coins start again tomorrow.

## Spending Coins {#spending-coins}

| What | Where | Coins |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Clothes and accessories | [The shops](shops-and-wardrobe.md#the-shops) | 3 to 260 |
| Snacks (hot dogs, slices, boba…) | Carts and counters around the city | 1 to 6 |
| Furniture | Flatbush Flea, in your [apartment](apartment/decorating.md) | 20 to 150 |
| Original paintings | The [Brooklyn Bridge](places/brooklyn-bridge.md#gallery) gallery | 10 to 15 |
| Fridge magnets | The [Brooklyn Bridge](places/brooklyn-bridge.md#magnets) souvenir stand | 3 to 5 |
| A bigger home | [StreetCheesy](apartment/moving-homes.md) | 1,500 or 4,000 |
| A Broadway show | [Times Square](places/times-square.md#broadway-theater) | 5 |
| A carriage ride, or a rowboat | [Central Park](places/central-park.md) | 4, or 3 |
| The ferry (as a passenger) | [Battery Park](places/battery-park-and-liberty-island.md) | 2 |
| A wish, a gumball, a busker tip, a soda | Around the city | 1 |

The map, the subway, taxis and the ferry home are always free.
