## The Basics {#the-basics}

:::details What is Club NYC?
A cozy online New York you play in your web browser. You make a New Yorker, explore 20+ places from Times Square to Liberty Island, work silly jobs for coins, decorate your apartment and hang out with real people. [Getting Started](getting-started/README.md)
:::

:::details Is Club NYC free?
Yes. It's free to play and there's nothing to buy. Coins are earned in the game (from jobs, challenges, stamps and a daily refill) and can't be bought or sold. [Coins & Shopping](coins-and-shopping.md)
:::

:::details Do I need to download anything?
No. Club NYC runs in your web browser on a computer. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
:::

:::details Can I play on my phone or tablet?
Not yet. The city needs a keyboard and a bigger screen, so it's made for laptops and desktops. On a phone, the game offers to send the link to your computer: copy it, email it to yourself, or set a reminder for tonight. Bigger tablets with a keyboard may work, but they aren't officially supported.
:::

:::details Do I need an account?
No. Press **Play Now** and you're in as a guest. To keep your New Yorker on every device, link Google, X, Discord or your email from your player card any time. [Account & Sign-In](account.md)
:::

:::details How old do I need to be?
13 or older. When you make your New Yorker we ask for your birth month and year to check, and we don't keep them.
:::

:::details Can I play right now?
If pressing Play shows a **Coming Soon** page, the city isn't open yet. Join the waitlist with your email and we'll email you the day the doors open. [The Waitlist](account.md#waitlist)
:::

:::details What happens when I join the waitlist?
We save your email address and may ask you to confirm it with one click. When the city opens, we email you to say so. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and we never sell your address. [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html#waitlist)
:::

:::details Is the game finished?
Club NYC is in early access: new places and features land all the time. See [What's New](whats-new.md) and [Coming Soon](places/coming-soon.md).
:::

## Playing {#playing}

:::details What is there to do?
Explore 20+ places, work five jobs for coins, shop for outfits, collect stamps, level up, decorate your apartment, dance at the Dance Jam in Times Square, start flash mobs, throw house parties and take on three new challenges every day.
:::

:::details How do I move?
Click anywhere on the ground. Your New Yorker walks there. Anything that glows when you point at it can be clicked. [Moving Around](getting-started/moving-around.md)
:::

:::details How do I get across town quickly?
Press [[M]] for the map or [[U]] for the subway, then click where you want to go. Travel is free. [Map, Subway & Taxis](getting-started/map-and-subway.md)
:::

:::details How do I sit down?
Click a bench, chair or step, or press [[S]] near one.
:::

:::details How do I change my outfit?
Press [[P]] (or click yourself) to open your player card, then click anything in the **Wardrobe** tab to wear it. [Shops & Your Wardrobe](shops-and-wardrobe.md#your-wardrobe)
:::

:::details Where do I get my free welcome gift?
At Sal's hot dog cart in Times Square. Click the cart and pick one. [Your First Day](getting-started/first-day.md#welcome-gift)
:::

:::details Can I play Rosa's tour again?
Yes. Press [[?]], click the **?** at the top left, or open the **Phone** and choose **How to Play**.
:::

## Coins & Progress {#coins-and-progress}

:::details What's the fastest way to earn coins?
Jobs. A good shift pays more than anything else, and each job has a leaderboard to climb. Add the Dance Jam and the daily challenges on top. [Earning Coins](coins-and-shopping.md#earning-coins)
:::

:::details Why did I stop getting coins?
You may have reached a daily limit (job pay tops out at 1,500 coins a day, and other rewards at 600). They reset at midnight New York time. Your scores and XP still count. [Daily Limits](coins-and-shopping.md#daily-limits)
:::

:::details What's the difference between coins and XP?
Coins are for spending. XP is for showing how far you've come: it only goes up, and it sets your level and your title. [Levels & XP](levels-and-xp.md)
:::

:::details Can I choose my title?
No. Your title comes from your level, from Tourist all the way to Club NYC Legend. [The Ladder](levels-and-xp.md#the-ladder)
:::

:::details Will my progress be saved?
Yes. Your New Yorker, coins, wardrobe and apartment are saved to the city as you play. As a guest, your progress lives in this browser until you link an account.
:::

:::details What happens if I miss a day?
Nothing bad. There are no streaks to lose. Your daily refill just doesn't grow that day, and every week starts fresh on Monday. [The Daily Refill](coins-and-shopping.md#daily-refill)
:::

## Friends & Safety {#friends-and-safety}

:::details How do I play with my friends?
Open the **Phone** on your toolbar (or press [[I]]) to get your invite link. Friends who open it land right next to you, and you're friends from the start. You can also add anyone you meet: click them, then **Add Friend**. [Inviting Friends](community/inviting-friends.md)
:::

:::details Can I play games with my friends?
Yes. Press [[F]] and open **Games** for the Pilgrim Hill Sled Race, Rush Hour Run and Carb-Jitsu. **Create Lobby** gives you a 4 letter code and a link to share; friends type the code on their Games tab or open the link. [Multiplayer Games](multiplayer-games/README.md)
:::

:::details How do I find a friend in the city?
Press [[F]], open **Friends**, and click **Go** next to their name. Or text them **Meet Me Here** from the Phone. [Friends](community/friends.md)
:::

:::details Is Club NYC safe?
We work hard on it. Chat is filtered, and links, emails and phone numbers are removed automatically. Every player's card has Report, Block and Mute, and moderators review reports along with the recent chat. [Safety & Community Rules](safety.md)
:::

:::details How do I report someone?
Click the player, then **Report** on their player card, and pick what's going on. You can also block them, so you won't see them or their chat anymore. [Report a Player](safety.md#report-a-player)
:::

:::details Someone asked for my personal info. What do I do?
Don't answer. Block them and report them. Never share your real name, address, school, socials or passwords, and never move a chat to another app. [Keep Yourself Safe](safety.md#keep-yourself-safe)
:::

## Account & Privacy {#account-and-privacy}

:::details Can I change my name?
Not yourself. Your name is set when you make your New Yorker. If a moderator resets a name that breaks the rules, you'll get to pick a new one.
:::

:::details What information do you collect?
As little as we can. Guests don't give us an email address. If you sign in with Google, X, Discord or email, we get what we need to sign you in. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) has the details, including how to delete your account.
:::

:::details How do I delete my account?
Player card ([[P]]), then the **Account** tab, then **Delete Account…** Type your name to confirm. It can't be undone. [Deleting Your Account](account.md#delete)
:::

:::details Is Club NYC part of New York City or any of its businesses?
No. Club NYC is an independent game inspired by the real city. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the City of New York, the MTA or any business or brand it pokes fun at.
:::

:::details I found a bug. Where do I tell you?
Thank you! Tell us in the [Discord](/discord), with your in-game name and what happened. [Feedback & Bug Reports](feedback.md)
:::
