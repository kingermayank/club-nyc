Coins are for spending. **XP** is for showing how far you've come. Everything you do in Club NYC earns XP: shifts at the jobs, stamps, the day's challenges, new places and new friends. XP only ever goes up, and it sets your **level** and your **title**.

## Where to See Your Level {#where-to-see-it}

- **The level ring** at the top right, next to your coins, fills up as you earn XP. Point at it to see your level, title and XP. Click it to open your player card.
- **Your player card** ([[P]]) shows "Level 5 · Local", your total XP, a progress bar and how much XP you need for the next level.
- **Other players' cards** show their level too.

![Your player card, with your level, title, XP bar and the XP you need for the next level.](/help/img/player-card.webp "Your player card shows your level and how close the next one is.")

When you earn XP, a little "+25 XP" floats off the ring. When you level up, there's confetti and a note: "Level 6! You're now a Stoop Sitter."

## Earning XP {#earning-xp}

| What you do | XP | How often |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Finish a [job](jobs/README.md) shift | 15 to 60 | Every shift (15, plus half the coins it paid) |
| Play a [multiplayer game](multiplayer-games/README.md) through | 20 to 50 | Every match (more for a podium finish) |
| Earn a new [stamp](stamps.md) | 25 | Once per stamp |
| Finish a [New in Town](getting-started/first-day.md#new-in-town) step | 20 | Once per step |
| Finish a [Today in the City](today-in-the-city.md) challenge | 15 | Up to 3 a day |
| Finish all of today's challenges | 30 | Once a day |
| Visit a place for the first time | 10 | Once per place |
| Make a new friend | 15 | Once per friend |
| A friend joins through your [invite](community/inviting-friends.md) | 50 | Once per friend |
| Come back to the city (daily refill) | 10 | Once a day |
| Dance at the [Dance Jam](events/dance-jam.md) | 10 | Up to 8 a day |
| Host a [house party](apartment/house-parties.md) | 20 | Up to 2 a day |

You can earn up to **2,000 XP a day** (New York time), which is far more than anyone needs.

## Level-Up Rewards {#level-up-rewards}

Every new level pays a coin bonus, once: **20 coins times the level**, up to 200. Level 2 pays 40 coins, Level 3 pays 60, and from Level 10 on, every level pays 200.

> [!TIP] Leveling Up Fast
> Your first evening gets you to Commuter: finish New in Town, work a few shifts and visit some new places. After that, the steadiest XP comes from jobs and the daily challenges.

> [!NYC] On Titles
> "Rent-Stabilized" is level 15 because it's the hardest thing to get in New York. We checked.
