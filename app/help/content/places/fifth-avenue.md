Fifth Avenue is where the city goes shopping, and where the best chicken over rice in Club NYC is served from a cart.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click Fifth Avenue.
- **Walk:** from [Rockefeller Center](places/rockefeller-center.md) or [Central Park](places/central-park.md).
- **Subway:** 5 Av/59 St.
- **Elevator:** coming down from the [Empire State Building](places/empire-state-building.md) observation deck lands you here.

## Toy Palace {#toy-palace}

![Inside Toy Palace: a giant floor piano, a candy cane slide, a gumball machine and a very large teddy bear.](/help/img/place-toy-palace.webp "Toy Palace, Fifth Avenue.")

- **Giant Floor Piano.** Walk across the keys to play, or click it and choose **Play a Tune**. Play 8 notes in one visit for the **Heart and Soul** stamp.
- **Candy Cane Slide.** Wheee.
- **Gumball Machine.** 1 coin a gumball. Most are red, blue, yellow or green. Some are not.
- **Toy Train.** All aboard.
- **Shelves:** Robots & Rockets, Balloons and the Toy Counter open the toy catalog.
- **Giant Teddy.** He's 15 feet tall and he's not for sale. He does accept hugs: **Give Him a Hug**.

## Maison Malle {#maison-malle}

The city's most exclusive boutique: Handbags, Ready-to-Wear, Jewelry & Shades, and the Boutique Desk. Everything here costs between 80 and 260 coins, and anything you buy earns the **Big Spender** stamp. The **Velvet Sofa** is free to sit on, which is the best deal in the store. Ask about the VIP staircase for an appointment (the next one is in 2029), and strike a pose with the mannequins.

## Secrets to Find {#secrets}

- **Feeling lucky?** Toy Palace's gumball machine hides a rare color that pays you back, and then some.
- **The steaming manhole.** Click it to find out where New York's steam really comes from.
- **East or West?** Click the street sign to learn why Fifth Avenue matters for every address in Manhattan.
