## Saks Fifth Avenue {#saks-fifth-avenue}

The department store across from St. Patrick's Cathedral, on Fifth Avenue since 1924. Its catalog has three sections:

- **The 10022-SHOE Floor.** Loafers, boots and kicks. The real shoe floor at Saks is so big it has its own zip code: 10022-SHOE.
- **Coats & Jackets.** A camel coat, a trench, a leather jacket and a sharp suit.
- **Jewelry & Shades.** Pearls, a gold chain, a silk scarf, gold shades and round specs.

Prices run from 15 to 260 coins. Every holiday season the real Saks lights up its whole facade with a light show.

> [!TIP] Looking for toys?
> The toy taxis, robots, rockets, teddy, balloons and dress-up (the tutu, the propeller beanie, the bodega cat ears) are at [I ♥ NY Souvenirs](places/times-square.md#i-love-ny-souvenirs) in Times Square now.

## Louis Vuitton {#louis-vuitton}

The Paris trunk-maker, in business since 1854: Handbags, Ready-to-Wear, Jewelry & Shades, and the Boutique Desk. Everything here costs between 80 and 260 coins, and anything you buy earns the **Big Spender** stamp. The **Velvet Sofa** is free to sit on, which is the best deal in the store. Ask about the VIP staircase for an appointment (the next one is in 2029), and strike a pose with the mannequins.
