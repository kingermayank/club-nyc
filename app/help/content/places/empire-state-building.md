The best view in Club NYC. You do need a ticket.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click the Empire State Building. You arrive on 34th Street, outside the front doors.
- **Subway:** 34 St–Herald Sq.
- **Going up:** walk in under the stone eagles, cross the lobby and take the **Elevator** to the 86th floor. The elevator asks for a ticket: **86th Floor Deck** is 6 coins, **Deck and Binoculars** is 8. A ticket is good until midnight in New York, so you can ride up and down all day. You can also buy one at the **Ticket Desk**.
- **Leave:** the **Elevator** takes you back down to the lobby. The ride down is free. From 34th Street, the blue arrow leads to [Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md).

### The Tower Viewers {#tower-viewers}

The viewers are coin-operated, like the real ones: **One Look** is 1 coin and **All-Day Looks** is 3. The **Deck and Binoculars** ticket covers them. Click a viewer to look through its binoculars, then drag to look around and scroll to zoom. Labels point out the sights: the Chrysler Building, Central Park, the city's water towers and the sunset over New Jersey. Keep looking and you might spot something floating by.
