The best view in Club NYC, no ticket line required.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click the Empire State Building.
- **Subway:** 34 St–Herald Sq.
- **Leave:** the **Elevator** takes you down to [Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md).

## Things to Do {#things-to-do}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Look around the city up close | Two **Tower Viewers** | Free |
| Learn about the spire | **The Spire** | Free |
| Learn about the Chrysler Building | **Chrysler Building** | Free |

### The Tower Viewers {#tower-viewers}

Click a viewer to look through it, then move your mouse to look around. Labels point out the sights: the Chrysler Building, Central Park, the city's water towers and the sunset over New Jersey. Keep looking and you might spot something floating by.
