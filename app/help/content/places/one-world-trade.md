One World Trade Center stands 1,776 feet tall, a nod to the year of the Declaration of Independence. The plaza at its foot holds the 9/11 Memorial, the Survivor Tree and the doors to the observatory elevators.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click One World Trade.
- **Walk:** from [Battery Park](places/battery-park-and-liberty-island.md#battery-park), or through the Oculus from [Wall Street](places/wall-street.md).
- **Subway:** WTC Cortlandt.

## The 9/11 Memorial {#memorial}

Two reflecting pools sit in the footprints of the Twin Towers, with the names of the people killed in the attacks of 2001 and 1993 inscribed in bronze around them. Click the **9/11 Memorial** to learn about the memorial and the towers.

- **Place a White Rose:** a memorial tradition.
- **Visit the Memorial Exhibit:** a short history, in four parts: the Twin Towers, the Memorial, the Survivor Tree and the Tribute in Light.

Visiting the memorial earns the **Never Forget** stamp.

## Sky Deck 102 {#sky-deck-102}

![Sky Deck 102: floor-to-ceiling windows over Lower Manhattan, tower viewers, a café counter and the Sky Portal in the floor.](/help/img/place-sky-deck.webp "Sky Deck 102, about 1,250 feet up.")

The elevator ride takes under a minute (your ears will notice). At the top:

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Look out over the harbor and Midtown | Two **Tower Viewers** | Free |
| Stand on a view of the street, 1,250 feet down | **The Sky Portal**: **Jump on It** or **Lie Down and Look** | Free |
| Hot cocoa or a deli coffee, very high up | **Cloud Nine Café** | 3 |
| A photo strip with the skyline | **Sky Deck Photo Booth** ([Photo Booths](events/photo-booths.md)) | Free |
| Wave at Lady Liberty, or hop to the Empire State or the Brooklyn Bridge | Click them through the windows | Free |
| Head back down | **Elevator Down to the Plaza** | Free |

Reaching the top earns the **Cloud Nine** stamp.

> The Oculus, the white transit hall next to the plaza, was designed to look like a bird being set free. Ten minutes inside and you'll find every train except yours.
