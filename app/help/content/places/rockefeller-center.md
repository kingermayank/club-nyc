Midtown's favorite rink. Walk onto the ice to skate, wave on live TV, and count the flags (please don't count the flags).

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click Rockefeller Center.
- **Walk:** from [Times Square](places/times-square.md) or [Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md).
- **Subway:** 47–50 Sts–Rockefeller Ctr.

## Things to Do {#things-to-do}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Skate | Walk onto the ice, or click **The Rink** and **Skate a Lap** | Free |
| Rent Ice Skates | **Skate Rentals** | 15 |
| Wave on live TV | **TV Studio · Wave on Air!** | Free |
| Strike Prometheus's pose, or toss a coin in his fountain | **Prometheus** | Free, or 1 for a wish |
| Salute about 200 flags | **The Flags** | Free |
| Take a seat | Benches (press [[S]] near the front ones) | Free |

### Skating {#skating}

Walk onto the ice and you start skating, a bit faster than walking. For a proper lap, click **The Rink** (or the **Rink Gate**) and choose **Skate a Lap**: your New Yorker circles the rink by themselves. Click anywhere to stop. **Do a Spin** is also available, results may vary.

The **Ice Skates** from Skate Rentals (15 coins) are yours to keep and wear anywhere. If you don't own skates yet, a tip pops up when you arrive.

### The TV Studio {#tv-studio}

Click the TV Studio window: your New Yorker waves, says "Hi Mom!" and your face appears on the studio's screen, **ON AIR**, for a few seconds.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> The gilded bronze statue over the rink is Prometheus, and he's been bringing fire to humanity (and photo ops to tourists) since 1934. The big tree goes up in November.
