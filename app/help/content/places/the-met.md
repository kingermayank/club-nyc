![The Met in Club NYC: the grand steps, the fountains, a hot dog cart and a pretzel cart.](/help/img/place-the-met.webp "Meet me on the steps.")

The Metropolitan Museum of Art, on Museum Mile. The galleries are still being hung, but the steps are open, and the steps are the point.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click The Met.
- **Walk:** from [Central Park](places/central-park.md).
- **Subway:** 86 St.

## Things to Do {#things-to-do}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Sit on the steps like a real New Yorker | **The Met Steps** | Free |
| Make a wish | Either **Fountain** | 1 |
| A museum-grade hot dog | **Hot Dog Cart** | 2 |
| A pretzel on the steps (iconic) | **Pretzel Cart** | 2 |
| Hail a taxi | **Hail a Taxi** | Free |
| Learn about the banners (and take a photo) | **The Banners** | Free |

### Making a Wish {#making-a-wish}

Click a fountain, then **Toss a Coin (1)**. Your New Yorker makes a very New York wish (a rent-stabilized apartment, a seat on the L train at rush hour, the perfect bagel), and you earn the **Make a Wish** stamp. Chelsea Market's waterfall takes wishes too.
