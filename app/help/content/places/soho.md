SoHo is Club NYC's style district: three shops in a row, a street vendor with "totally real" designer shades, and the best coffee below 14th Street.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click SoHo.
- **Walk:** from [the High Line](places/high-line-and-chelsea-market.md) or [Chinatown & Little Italy](places/chinatown-and-little-italy.md).
- **Subway:** Prince St.

## Supreme {#supreme}

![Inside Supreme: clothing racks, a denim table, mannequins and a mirror.](/help/img/place-thread.webp "Supreme. Minimalism, but make it neon.")

The skate shop that opened on Lafayette Street in 1994 and never stopped drawing lines. The racks open the catalog by section: **Tops & Dresses**, **Jackets & Coats**, **Denim & Bottoms** and **Accessories**. The **Supreme Catalog** and **Checkout** show everything. Point at an item to try it on before you buy. The **Mirror** and **Fitting Room** open your wardrobe. [How shopping works](shops-and-wardrobe.md)

## Nike {#nike}

**The Wall** holds the sneakers, and **Extras** has the rest. Sit on the **Try-On Bench**, get your feet measured by the **Foot Measurer**, and click the **Turntable** to dance. The vault in the back holds the grails, which are not for sale. Not even for you.

## Ralph's Coffee {#ralphs-coffee}

![Inside Ralph's Coffee: the order counter, an espresso machine, coffee sacks and cozy tables.](/help/img/place-bean-there.webp "Ralph's Coffee. This is my office now.")

Ralph Lauren's café, the one with the forest-green cups. Click the **Order Counter**:

- **Deli Coffee (3).** The blue cup. Earns **The Usual** stamp.
- **Oat Latte (4).** The barista writes your name on the cup. Nearly. Earns **Who's Mayonnaise?**
- **Everything Bagel (2).** Toasted? In New York? Bold.
- **Help With the Delivery.** Starts the [Bagel Counters](jobs/bagel-counters.md) job.

Grab the **Armchair** or a table to sit. The **Book Nook** upstairs is coming soon.
