SoHo is Club NYC's style district: three shops in a row, a street vendor with "totally real" designer shades, and the best coffee below 14th Street.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click SoHo.
- **Walk:** from [the High Line](places/high-line-and-chelsea-market.md) or [Chinatown & Little Italy](places/chinatown-and-little-italy.md).
- **Subway:** Prince St.

## THREAD {#supreme}

![Inside THREAD: clothing racks, a denim table, mannequins and a mirror.](/help/img/place-thread.webp "THREAD. Minimalism, but make it neon.")

The skate shop that opened on Lafayette Street in 1994 and never stopped drawing lines. The red sign and **This Week's Drop** open the drop, priced like the resale line around the block:

| Piece | Coins |
| --- | :---: |
| Box Logo Hoodie | 800 |
| THREAD Brick (yes, the real 2016 one) | 300 |
| Box Logo Tee | 240 |
| THREAD Skate Deck | 180 |
| Camp Cap | 120 |
| THREAD Cookies | 40 |

The racks open the rest by section: **Tops & Dresses**, **Jackets & Coats**, **Denim & Bottoms** and **Accessories**. **Checkout** shows everything. Point at an item to try it on before you buy. The **Mirror** and **Fitting Room** open your wardrobe. [How shopping works](shops-and-wardrobe.md)

## Sneaker Vault {#sneaker-vault}

**The Wall** holds the Sky Highs: the Sky High 1 in Chicago, Bred and Royal, the Sky High 4 Military Black, the Sky High 11 Concord and the Sky High 3 White Cement (170 to 230 coins). **Lows & Uptowns** has the Slam Low Panda and all-white Stoop Oness, which New Yorkers call Uptowns (110 to 115). Point at a pair to try it on. **Extras** has the rest. Sit on the **Try-On Bench**, get your feet measured by the **Foot Measurer**, and click the **Turntable** to dance. The vault in the back holds the grails, which are not for sale. Not even for you.

## Starducks Coffee {#ralphs-coffee}

![Inside Starducks Coffee: the order counter, an espresso machine, coffee sacks and cozy tables.](/help/img/place-bean-there.webp "Starducks Coffee. This is my office now.")

The coffee shop on every corner, with the green cups and your name spelled almost right. Click the **Order Counter** and pick from the menu board:

| Coffee | Coins | From the case | Coins |
| --- | :---: | --- | :---: |
| Starducks Coffee (the green cup) | 4 | Chocolate Chip Cookie | 3 |
| Oat Latte (your name, nearly) | 4 | Black and White Cookie | 3 |
| Cold Brew | 5 | Croissant | 3 |
| Deli Coffee (the blue cup) | 3 | Everything Bagel | 2 |

The Deli Coffee earns **The Usual**; the Oat Latte earns **Who's Mayonnaise?** **Help with the Delivery** at the bottom of the board starts the [Bagel Counters](jobs/bagel-counters.md) job.

Grab the **Armchair** or a table to sit.
