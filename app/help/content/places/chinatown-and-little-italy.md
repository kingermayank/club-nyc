## Joe's Pizza {#joes-pizza}

![Inside Joe's Pizza: the counter, the pizza oven, the soda fridge and a wall of signed headshots.](/help/img/place-pizza.webp "Everything is 1 coin.")

The first Joe's Pizza opened on Carmine Street in 1975. This one is in Little Italy. Click **Order a Slice**:

- **Plain Slice (1).** Fold it like a local. Earns the **Slice of Life** stamp.
- **Work a Shift.** Starts the [Slice Factory](jobs/slice-factory.md) job. They're short-staffed.
- **With a Fork and Knife.** A gasp ripples through the shop. Let's pretend that didn't happen.

The **Soda Fridge** sells an ice-cold soda for 1 coin, and there are four tables to sit at. The chalkboard by the window table is where the city plays [Carb-Jitsu](multiplayer-games/carb-jitsu.md).

## Secrets to Find {#secrets}

- **The Wall of Fame** has signed headshots. Nobody recognizes any of them. Click it and **Pose for the Wall** anyway.
- **The pizza boxes** are ready for delivery. Superhero not included.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> Mulberry Street once held a huge Italian community. Today it's a few blocks of red-sauce restaurants, plus the Feast of San Gennaro every September.
