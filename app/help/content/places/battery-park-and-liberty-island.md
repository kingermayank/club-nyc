The harbor is where Manhattan meets the water. Battery Park is the ferry dock, and Liberty Island is the reason everyone's on the ferry.

## Battery Park {#battery-park}

![Battery Park's ferry dock, with the ferry, the ticket booth, benches and Lady Liberty in the distance.](/help/img/place-battery-park.webp "The ferry dock at the tip of Lower Manhattan.")

### Getting Here {#getting-here-battery-park}

- **Map:** click Battery Park Ferry. (Clicking the Statue of Liberty brings you here too: the island is ferry-only.)
- **Subway:** South Ferry.
- **Walk:** from [One World Trade](places/one-world-trade.md).
- **Leave:** walk out toward One World Trade.

### Things to Do {#things-to-do-battery-park}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Ride the ferry to Liberty Island | **Ferry Tickets** or **Board the Ferry**, then **Ride as a Passenger** | 2 |
| Steer the ferry yourself | **Ferry Tickets** or **Board the Ferry**, then **Captain the Ferry** ([Ferry Captain](jobs/ferry-captain.md)) | Free, and it pays |
| Sit and watch the boats | Benches | Free |
| Practice your life-ring toss | **Life Ring**, then **Practice a Toss** | Free |
| Learn about Lady Liberty and old Battery Park | **Statue of Liberty**, the **Lamp Post** | Free |

> [!WARNING] Guard Your Snacks
> The Battery Park seagulls are professionals. If you're holding a hot dog, pretzel, slice, bagel, donut, gelato or dumplings, one may swipe it. Don't worry: you still own it. Grab it again from your [wardrobe](shops-and-wardrobe.md#your-wardrobe). Drinks are safe.

## Liberty Island {#liberty-island}

### Getting Here {#getting-here-liberty-island}

- **Ferry only:** from Battery Park, as a passenger (2 coins) or as the captain in [Ferry Captain](jobs/ferry-captain.md) (dock at the island to arrive here).
- **Leave:** click the **Ferry Back to Manhattan**, then **All Aboard!** The trip back is free.

### Things to Do {#things-to-do-liberty-island}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Climb the 354 steps to the crown | **Statue of Liberty**, then **Climb to the Crown** | Free |
| See the torch, read the pedestal | **Statue of Liberty**, then **See the Torch** or **Read the Pedestal** | Free |
| Foam crowns and torches | **Souvenir Kiosk** | 10 to 12 |
| Strike the Liberty pose | **Souvenir Kiosk**, then **Strike the Liberty Pose** | Free |
| Look around the harbor | Two **Tower Viewers** | Free |
| Learn about the fort under the statue | **Fort Wood** | Free |
| Wave at Manhattan | **Manhattan Skyline** | Free |
| Take a seat | Bench | Free |

### Climbing to the Crown {#crown}

Click the statue and choose **Climb to the Crown**: up the pedestal, then the narrow spiral stairs, 354 steps in all. At the top, the crown's 25 windows look out over the whole harbor. The climb earns the **Crown Jewel** stamp.

The viewers show One World Trade, Lower Manhattan and Battery Park across the water, and a close look at the torch, the crown's 25 windows and the date on her tablet.

## Stamps Here {#stamps}

| Stamp | How |
| --- | --- |
| Lady Liberty | Visit Liberty Island. |
| Crown Jewel | Climb the 354 steps to Lady Liberty's crown. |
| Captain! | Steer the ferry all the way to Liberty Island. |

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> Lady Liberty was a gift from France in 1886. She's 151 feet from heel to torch, her copper skin is only 2.4 mm thick, and she was brown when she arrived. The green took about 30 years. She also wears a size 879 sandal.
