![Central Park in Club NYC: Bow Bridge over the Lake, the boathouse, a horse carriage and park benches.](/help/img/place-central-park.webp "The Lake and Bow Bridge.")

843 acres of green in the middle of Manhattan. Ours is a little smaller, but the squirrels are just as bold.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click Central Park.
- **Walk:** from [Fifth Avenue](places/fifth-avenue.md) or [The Met](places/the-met.md).
- **Subway:** 59 St–Columbus Circle.

## Things to Do {#things-to-do}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Take a carriage ride around the park | **Horse Carriage Ride** | 4 |
| Row around the Lake | **Rowboat**, or the **Boathouse Café**, then **Rent a Rowboat** | 3 |
| Hot cocoa by the lake | **Boathouse Café** | 3 |
| A warm pretzel | **Pretzel Cart** (or the Boathouse Café) | 2 |
| Pop the question to a bridge | **Bow Bridge**, then **Pop the Question** | Free |
| Sit and people-watch | **Park Benches** (or **Take It to a Bench** from a snack card) | Free |
| Chat with the wildlife | Ducks and squirrels | Free |
| Race down Pilgrim Hill | The **Pilgrim Hill** signpost on the lawn ([Sled Race](multiplayer-games/sled-race.md)) | Free, and it pays |

### The Carriage Ride {#carriage-ride}

A lap around the park for 4 coins. The horse is named Biscuit. Your first ride earns the **Clip-Clop** stamp.

## Secrets to Find {#secrets}

- **Every duck has an opinion.** Click all three.
- **That squirrel is eyeing your snack.** Saying hi to a squirrel while you're holding a snack is a bold choice. (If one takes it, you still own it: hold it again from your wardrobe.)
- **Bow Bridge** has a question for you. Click it and find out what happens when you kneel.
