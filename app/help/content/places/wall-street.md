### The Pizza Index {#pizza-index}

The big board tracks the prices that really matter: the dollar slice, the subway fare, a plain bagel, chicken over rice, and pigeons per bench. Call whether **SLICE** goes up or down next. Being right pays nothing but bragging rights, which on Wall Street is the point.

### The Bull {#the-bull}

In 1989 an artist dropped a 7,000-pound bronze bull near the stock exchange overnight, as a surprise for the city. Rubbing a bull for luck is a New York tradition, and so is the line to do it. Rub it once for the **Bull Market** stamp.
