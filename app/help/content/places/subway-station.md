The station under Times Square is where the subway lives, and where the [Showtime!](jobs/showtime.md) crew practices.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Walk:** take **Subway · Times Sq–42 St** from [Times Square](places/times-square.md), or **Down to the Subway** from [Grand Central](places/grand-central.md).
- **Subway:** ride to Times Sq–42 St.
- **Leave:** the **turnstiles** take you up to Times Square.
