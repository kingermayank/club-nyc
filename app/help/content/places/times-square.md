Times Square is the heart of Club NYC and where most New Yorkers start. It's bright, loud and always busy, just like the real thing (minus the costumed characters asking for tips).

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click Times Square.
- **Walk:** from [Rockefeller Center](places/rockefeller-center.md) or [Grand Central](places/grand-central.md) (via 42nd St).
- **Subway:** ride to Times Sq–42 St, then take the turnstiles up.

## The Dance Jam {#dance-jam}

Every 8 minutes, Times Square throws a 3 minute **Dance Jam**. Get to the middle of the plaza and press [[D]] to dance. Dance for 20 seconds to earn 20 coins in tips and the **Dance Jam** stamp. [All about the Dance Jam](events/dance-jam.md)

## Broadway Theater {#broadway-theater}

Click **Watch Tonight's Show**, then **Buy a Ticket (5)**. You watch from the orchestra seats: the lights dim, the curtain goes up, and the cast (New Yorkers in full costume) sings tonight's show, with the words shown above the stage. There are three shows, and they change from night to night: *Phantom of the Subway*, *The Pizza King* and *Bagels!*

- **Skip** ([[S]]) jumps to the curtain call. **Leave** ([[Esc]]) ends the night at once.
- At the end: **Bravo!** The card tells you what's on tomorrow night, and offers **Watch Again (5)** or **Back to the Theater**.
- Your first show earns the **Curtain Call** stamp and a **Playbill** to keep.
- Click the **Box Seats** or **The Chandelier** for a bit of Broadway history.

## I ♥ NY Souvenirs {#i-love-ny-souvenirs}

The souvenir shop has tees and hoodies, caps and beanies, foam crowns and torches, snow globes, postcards and gifts, and toy taxis. Click a shelf to open the catalog for that section, and point at an item to try it on before you buy. The **Mirror** and the **Changing Room** open your wardrobe. [How shopping works](shops-and-wardrobe.md)

Click the big neon sign (**I ♥ NY · Photo Booth**) to learn how the logo was born (in a taxi, in red crayon), then **Pose Under the Neon** for a [photo-booth](events/photo-booths.md) strip with everyone nearby.

## Secrets to Find {#secrets}

- **The pigeons are listening.** Try saying hello to them. More than once. More than one pigeon.
- **Smile, you're on the big screen.** Every so often the biggest billboard has a message for someone special. Keep an eye on it.
- **The welcome gift is one per New Yorker,** so pick the one that matches your vibe.
