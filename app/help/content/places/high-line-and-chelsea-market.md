The High Line is a park built on an old freight railroad, high above the West Side. Take the stairs down for Chelsea Market, where the Oreo was invented.

## The High Line {#the-high-line}

### Getting Here {#getting-here-high-line}

- **Map:** click The High Line.
- **Walk:** from [SoHo](places/soho.md), or up the stairs from Chelsea Market.
- **Subway:** 14 St.

### Things to Do {#things-to-do-high-line}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Gelato by the flavor: pistachio, strawberry, chocolate, stracciatella, lemon or mango sorbet, or two scoops | **Gelato Cart** | 3 each |
| Lounge in the sun | **Rolling Loungers** or the bench | Free |
| Look out over the Hudson | **Hudson River View** | Free |
| Learn about the water towers | **Water Tower** | Free |
| Race up the High Line on a Citi Bike | The **Citi Bike Dash** signpost by the planting beds ([Citi Bike Dash](multiplayer-games/citi-bike-dash.md)) | Free, and it pays |

## Chelsea Market {#chelsea-market}

![Inside Chelsea Market: a brick hall with a pipe waterfall, a factory clock and four shopfronts.](/help/img/place-chelsea-market.webp "Chelsea Market, in the old Nabisco factory.")

A long brick hall under the High Line, with a taqueria, a raw bar, a bookshop and a doughnut stand. Click a shopfront to step up to its counter.

### Getting Here {#getting-here-chelsea-market}

- **Map:** click Chelsea Market.
- **Stairs:** down from the High Line (and back **Up to the High Line**).

### Things to Do {#things-to-do-chelsea-market}

| What | Where | Cost |
| --- | --- | :---: |
| Order a taco, hand over your ticket, shave the adobada off the spit and say "con todo" | **Dos Tacos No. 2** | 2 to 3 |
| Shuck a half dozen oysters, dress them and slurp | **The Lobster Spot** | 6 |
| Buy the lobster roll | **The Lobster Spot** | 30 |
| Pull the lever, fry six mini doughnuts and shake them in sugar | **Doughnut Dept.** | 3 |
| Pick a book off the table and flip through it | **Postman Books** | Free |
| Take a book home | **Postman Books** | 5 |
| Make a wish | **The Waterfall** | 1 |
| Take a seat | Bench | Free |

### Tips {#tips-chelsea-market}

- At the taqueria you pay first. The ticket goes to the taquero.
- An oyster opens at the hinge. Turn the shell over when you are done.
- There are four sugars at Doughnut Dept. and six books on the table.
