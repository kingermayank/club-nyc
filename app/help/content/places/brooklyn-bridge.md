The walk across the Brooklyn Bridge is a New York rite of passage. The Brooklyn side is still being built, so for now the bridge is all about the view, and the two best little shops in the city.

## Getting Here {#getting-here}

- **Map:** click Brooklyn Bridge.
- **Walk:** from [Chinatown & Little Italy](places/chinatown-and-little-italy.md) or [Wall Street](places/wall-street.md).
- **Subway:** Brooklyn Bridge–City Hall.

## The Skyline Painter's Gallery {#gallery}

"All originals. Some more original than others." Click the painter's easel to browse. Point at a painting to hear about it, and click its price to buy it. Paintings go to your apartment's storage, ready to hang. [Decorating](apartment/decorating.md)

| Painting | Coins |
| --- | :---: |
| Still Life with Slice | 10 |
| Manhattan at Dusk | 12 |
| Taxi in the Rain | 12 |
| The Bridge at Night | 14 |
| Central Park in October | 14 |
| His Majesty, the Pigeon | 15 |

Your first painting earns the **Art Collector** stamp.

## Fridge Magnets {#magnets}

The souvenir stand sells nine fridge magnets, 3 to 5 coins each: the Dollar Slice, Yellow Cab, Lady Liberty, Everything Bagel, City Pigeon, Subway Token, The Big Apple, Dirty-Water Dog and Empire State. They go straight onto the fridge in your [apartment](apartment/README.md#your-fridge). Collect all nine for the **Magnetic Personality** stamp.

The stand also has **Shirts & Hats** from the I ♥ NY catalog.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> The Brooklyn Bridge opened in 1883 as the first steel-wire suspension bridge. A year later, P.T. Barnum marched 21 elephants across it to prove it was safe. The seagulls have been unimpressed ever since.
