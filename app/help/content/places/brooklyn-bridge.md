The walk across the Brooklyn Bridge is a New York rite of passage. Here it’s all about the view, and the two best little shops in the city.

## Walk to Brooklyn {#walk}

Click the arrow at the far end of the promenade and walk the bridge: past the first stone tower, along the second half with DUMBO, the clock tower and the carousel ahead, all the way to the Brooklyn end. Brooklyn itself isn't open in Club NYC yet, so a barricade there says so. Click **Go Back to Manhattan** (or press Escape, or **Turn Back** on the way) to walk home. The first walk earns the **Walked the Bridge** stamp.

## Love Locks {#love-locks}

Couples and friends have clipped padlocks to the bridge for years and thrown the keys into the East River. Here it's the same railing for everyone: every New Yorker's locks hang side by side.

1. **Get a lock.** Click **Love Locks** (or **Souvenir Stand**, then **Love Locks**) and pick one from the tray on the counter. A lock comes with its key, 12 coins.
2. **Make it yours.** On the newspaper: pick an enamel colour and a shape (classic, round or heart), grab a spray can and hold the mouse down over the lock to paint it, and type up to four initials. **Wipe** starts the paint over.
3. **Clip it on.** Click **Hang It**, carry the lock along the railing and click a strap to snap it shut. It swings, and so do its neighbours.
4. **The key.** Drag it and fling it up and over the rail into the river. The harder you throw, the further it flies. Or click **Keep the Key**.

Point at any lock to see whose it is. Click one of yours to **Unlock It** with a key you kept, or with a **Spare Key** (5 coins at the stand): it comes back off the railing and into your pocket. Each New Yorker keeps their eight newest locks on the railing; hang a ninth and the city's bolt cutters take your oldest.

## Fridge Magnets {#magnets}

The souvenir stand sells nine fridge magnets, 3 to 5 coins each: the Dollar Slice, Yellow Cab, Lady Liberty, Everything Bagel, City Pigeon, Subway Token, The Big Apple, Dirty-Water Dog and Empire State. They go straight onto the fridge in your [apartment](apartment/README.md#your-fridge). Collect all nine for the **Magnetic Personality** stamp.

The stand also has **Shirts & Hats** from the I ♥ NY catalog.

> [!NYC] Fun Fact
> The Brooklyn Bridge opened in 1883 as the first steel-wire suspension bridge. A year later, P.T. Barnum marched 21 elephants across it to prove it was safe. The seagulls have been unimpressed ever since.
