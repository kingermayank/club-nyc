Club NYC is in early access, and your feedback shapes what we build next. We read everything.

## Have an Idea? {#ideas}

Come hang out in the [Club NYC Discord](/discord). It's where we share first looks at new places, ask what to build next, and hear what you'd like to see. Discord is for ages 13+ (older in some countries), and our server has its own rules.

## Found a Bug? {#bugs}

Tell us in the [Discord](/discord). To help us fix it fast, include:

1. **Your in-game name.**
2. **Where you were** (for example, "Times Square" or "in Halal Rush").
3. **What you did,** step by step.
4. **What happened,** and what you expected to happen.
5. **Your browser and computer** (for example, "Chrome on a Windows laptop").
6. **A screenshot,** if you can.

> [!TIP] Check First
> Some things that look like bugs have quick fixes. Take a look at [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) first.

## Someone Breaking the Rules? {#rule-breaking}

That's not a bug report: use **Report** on their player card, so moderators see their recent chat. [Report a Player](safety.md#report-a-player)

## Contact Us {#contact}

For anything else, including privacy questions and appeals, our contact email is on the [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) and [Community Rules](/rules.html) pages.

> Every bug report makes the city a little better. Every bug report about pigeons is read twice.
