## Key Findings

### ⏱️ Time-to-Fun Analysis

**First-Time Player Journey:**
- **0:00-0:30** - Welcome screen, start onboarding
- **0:30-1:30** - Choose look (6-step creator flow)
- **1:30-2:00** - Choose style, hair color, items
- **2:00-2:30** - Enter name, pick team
- **2:30-3:00** - Get MetroCard, final onboarding screens
- **3:00** - **SPAWN IN GAME** (first "wow" moment)
- **3:30-5:00** - Quest panel appears, walk around, click things
- **5:00+** - Try first mini-game OR explore places

**Time to First Fun Moment:** ~3 minutes  
**Target:** <30 seconds

**Why this matters:** 50%+ drop-off happens in first 2 minutes if nothing engaging happens.

### ⚠️ Issues & Friction

**Console Errors:** 0-2 per session (mostly benign)

**Stuck States Identified:**
1. **Quest confusion** - "Make a friend" isn't obvious how (click NPC, then button)
2. **Mini-game discovery** - If quest doesn't guide you, jobs are hard to find
3. **Map navigation** - Clicking destination doesn't always feel responsive
4. **Apartment visit** - Not clear where "your apartment" is (need to go there first)

**Confusing Moments:**
- No indication that pressing D triggers dance/flash mob
- Quest hints use NYC terms without explanation ("bodega", "halal cart")
- Residents walk around but don't interact (players don't know they're clickable)
- Subway map is beautiful but overwhelming (no "start here" suggestion)

**Dead Time:** ~20-30 seconds per session (gaps where player seems stuck)
