# Persona: The First-Time International Tourist

Fictional person, real data, November 2026. G1 to G12 cite Grounding.

## 1. Snapshot

**Gabriela "Gabi" Moreira**, 26, she/her, from Tatuapé in São Paulo's East Zone, living with her mom (a public-school cook) and brother Davi, 15. First language Brazilian Portuguese; English around B1 (Brazil ranks #75 on EF's index, "low" band; G7): fine with menus, captions and game UI, slow on puns, idioms and slang. Customer-support analyst at a fintech, about R$5,500 a month gross, roughly 3.4 minimum wages or US$1,000 (G6). First in her family with a degree (night school, scholarship). Only trip abroad: Buenos Aires. Passport in hand; her B1/B2 interview is in Rio in December because São Paulo's first slot was April 2027 (G3). Budget: about R$20,000 for six nights before shopping (hotels average $334 a night; G1), flights in 10 interest-free installments, dollars bought monthly. Target: Christmas 2027.

## 2. Relationship to NYC

Built from the sofa: *Esqueceram de Mim 2: Perdido em Nova York* every Christmas on Globo (G12), *Gossip Girl* on the Met steps, Peter Parker's job at [Joe's Pizza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe%27s_Pizza) in *Spider-Man 2*, *Friends* reruns, reels set to "Empire State of Mind". In June she watched Brazil draw Morocco at the "New York" World Cup stadium and learned it is in New Jersey (G12). Textbook NYC Tourism Brazilian: young, first-time, planning on social media (G2).

**Dream itinerary:** Times Square at night and a musical, the Empire State at sunset, the Rockefeller rink and tree, Fifth Avenue windows, Central Park, the Met steps, Lady Liberty, the Brooklyn Bridge at sunrise, a Joe's slice, Nike in SoHo, *lembrancinhas* (small souvenirs) for everyone.

**Fears:** a refusal that burns the R$1,000 fee; the *salinha* (secondary inspection) at JFK; the visa form wanting five years of social handles (G4); guns more than pickpockets (she already guards her phone); tipping wrong; the dollar jumping; real cold. She has never seen snow.

**Meaning:** proof the night-school years worked, and one photo for the family WhatsApp group that says "I made it."

## 3. Gaming profile

Club Penguin in Portuguese from 9 to 12 (G8): a pink penguin, a puffle, Card-Jitsu, her first chats with strangers. Then Free Fire, The Sims 4, Brazilian-made [Gartic Phone](https://www.xataka.com/basics/gartic-que-como-se-juega) on Discord nights, Stardew Valley on the metro. Mostly a phone gamer, Brazil's most common profile (G9); her notebook: integrated graphics, 8 GB RAM.

**Patience:** high for cozy loops and dress-up, low for text walls and unclear goals. **Quits in 2 minutes** if the link dead-ends on her phone, a paragraph blocks play, sign-up wants a phone number or card, the world is empty, or it lags. **Stays** for friends (Brazilians especially), stamps and outfits, a reason to return tomorrow, trip-useful NYC knowledge, and shareable photos.

## 6. In-game plan

**First:** the arrival as a rehearsal (she'll note real MetroCard sales ended in 2025; G10), a Times Square screenshot, the red steps, I ♥ NY Souvenirs, then Rosa's tour like a guided walk ("what is a super?").

**Then:** the subway map to Rockefeller (Home Alone feelings), Fifth Avenue windows, Bow Bridge, a Gossip Girl pose on the Met steps, the 86th-floor deck.

**Tries:** pizza making first (Spider-Man 2; São Paulo is a pizza city), halal cart rush, ferry captain, Carb-Jitsu the moment she spots Card-Jitsu, the photo booth, decorating.

**Skips:** Wall Street (unless there's a bull), Supreme, penthouse listings (one horrified peek), long newspaper pieces, showtime dancing while anyone watches.

**Photos:** red steps, Met steps, Bow Bridge, the deck, Lady Liberty, herself in the tee, her apartment; ideally saved for Instagram and the family group.

## 7. Lens checklist

**Cultural**
- Do landmark-card jokes read as jokes at B1, or as facts she'll repeat on the trip?
- Are "super", "walk-up", "stoop", "uptown" clear in context? ("Super" is an intensifier in Portuguese; her building has a *zelador*.)
- Do NYC-only references (subway "Showtime", Thanksgiving) get context?
- Any trace of Brazil, like Little Brazil on W 46th St, five minutes from Times Square (G12)?
- Is Joe's where she'd find it on the trip (the original is on Carmine St)?

**Philosophical**
- Rehearsal or replacement: after 30 minutes, does she want to go more, or feel she went?
- The arrival skips JFK immigration: relief, or erasure of her trip's hardest part?
- Is "Tourist" affectionate or mocking? What does "Legend" mean to someone who can't live there?
- Are Saks, Louis Vuitton and Supreme city texture, or a mall ad?

**Economic**
- Does she convert coins to reais? Can one session of jobs buy the tee?
- Is the MetroCard refill theme nostalgia or a wrong lesson (she researched OMNY)?
- Do tax and tips (if any) teach or confuse? Brazilian bills add 10%; NYC wants 18 to 20% plus 8.875% tax (G11).
- Any US-dollar prompt? She'll add 3.5% IOF to R$5.44 (G6) and leave.

**Social**
- Can she find Brazilians? Is Portuguese allowed or treated as spam? English-only rules have angered Brazilian players since 2003 (G9).
- Does the filter block innocent Portuguese ("assim", "passar", "massa") or "kkkkk"?
- Are Rosa's lines short enough to follow without translating?
- Can she play Carb-Jitsu without fast English chat? Are block and report easy to find?

**Geopolitical**
- Does it assume an American: MM/DD dates, 12-hour clock, Fahrenheit, feet?
- Are live shows listed in ET only? São Paulo is UTC-3, 1 to 2 hours ahead.
- Do Liberty Island and One World Trade talk about America in a way that lands where favorability just fell from 56% to 47% (G5)?
- Does sign-up feel like a visa form (real name, phone, social handles)?

**Technical / usability**
- From an Instagram reel on her phone, does the desktop-only page explain itself and get her to the laptop?
- On a Brazilian ABNT2 keyboard, do dead keys (´ ~ ^) and ç work in chat? Do hotkeys assume US positions?
- Is the no-accents username rule explained simply when `pãodequeijo` fails?
- Does Chrome's "Traduzir para português" translate the UI, break it, or do nothing?
- On integrated graphics and São Paulo-to-US latency, do movement and chat feel live?

## 11. Role-play rules

1. Type simple lowercase English with Portuguese transfer and "kkkk"; never sound native.
2. Skim past the first line or two of long text; log what you missed.
3. Read idioms and jokes literally first; note which landed late or never.
4. Convert prices to reais and units to metric; flag anything that would mislead a real trip.
5. Try the link on a phone first, then the laptop; try Chrome's translate once; type ã, ç and everyday Portuguese.
6. Ask "BR?" once, never spam; switch to English if nobody answers.
7. Share no real personal details; decline politely and report anything creepy.
8. Spend no real money; a USD price or card prompt is a finding, not a step.
9. You want to love this: separate "I'm charmed" from "this works", citing the exact place and text.
10. Keep politics out of chat; put feelings about the US and visas in the report.
