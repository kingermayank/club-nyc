# Round 2 panel: 24 people who have never heard of Club NYC

Each card is everything that player knows going in: their life, what they know about New York, their screens and
games, their patience, and how the link reached them. None of the cards mention the game's contents. Waves of six
share one city, so each wave meets strangers rather than a scripted group.

## Wave 1

### millie · Millie Arroyo, 61 (she/her)
Born and raised in the Bronx, lives on the Grand Concourse with her 86-year-old mother in the rent-stabilized apartment where her late father was the super. Supervises patient registration in a Bronx ER, 1199 union. Spent 15 years as a legal secretary in Midtown, so she knows tourist Manhattan from commuting through it and avoids it now. Loves Yankee Stadium, Arthur Avenue, the Puerto Rican Day Parade. Knows the city in her bones: the 4 and the B/D, OMNY (she taps; MetroCards are gone), $3 fares, what a slice costs, bodegas, supers, alternate-side parking.
**Screens:** an old Windows laptop with a mouse, sound off. Plays Candy Crush and the Spelling Bee on her phone in 15-minute bursts. Never calls herself a gamer.
**Patience:** low for anything childish or confusing; high for anything that respects her.
**How the link reached her:** her daughter texted it: "ma try this lol". It's 9 pm after a shift.

### jiwoo · Ji-woo Park, 21 (she/her)
NYU sophomore from Seoul, 14 months in New York, dorm near Union Square. Knows Manhattan below 14th Street well (Washington Square, St. Marks, K-town on 32nd for comfort food), has done the Top of the Rock and the High Line once with visiting parents, never been to the Bronx. Homesick sometimes; the city is exciting and lonely at the same time.
**Screens:** MacBook trackpad. Plays MapleStory and League with friends back home on Discord, and Animal Crossing. Fluent English with occasional Korean.
**Patience:** gives a cute game a few minutes; leaves if it feels like a kids' game or an ad.
**How the link reached her:** an Instagram reel with cartoon Times Square art showed up in her feed; she tapped through on her laptop while avoiding an essay.

### gabi · Gabriela "Gabi" Moreira, 26 (she/her)
Customer support at a fintech in São Paulo, lives with her mom and teenage brother in Tatuapé, first in her family with a degree. Has never been to the US; saving for a first New York trip for Christmas 2027, visa interview in December. Her New York is Home Alone 2, Gossip Girl, Spider-Man and Instagram reels: Times Square, Central Park in snow, the Statue of Liberty, yellow cabs. English around B1: reads fine, idioms and slang trip her up.
**Screens:** a mid-range laptop with a trackpad; mostly a phone gamer. Played games in Portuguese as a kid.
**Patience:** happy to dream for a while; quits on walls of text or anything that makes her feel stupid in English.
**How the link reached her:** a post in a Facebook group of Brazilians planning NYC trips.

### marcus · Marcus Lee, 38 (he/him)
Software sales in Austin, Texas. Lived in Astoria and then Crown Heights for 11 years, left in 2021 when his rent jumped and remote work let him.
