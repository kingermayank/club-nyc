# Neighborhoods, home and the systems in every room: making it alive

Round 2 design pass, 2026-11-11. District: SoHo (Greene St), Supreme (`thread`), Nike (`sneaker-vault`), Ralph's Coffee (`coffee`), Chinatown & Little Italy (`chinatown`), Joe's Pizza (`pizza`), the three homes (`apartment`, `loft`, `penthouse`), and the systems that run through every room: info cards, shops and the catalog, buying food (`vend`), the Club NYC Times, photo booths, furniture and house parties, city locals, stamps, the phone and the toolbar.

## How to read this

- **Today** is what the code does right now, read from `app/src/data/rooms.ts` and the feature files on Nov 11. The working tree is moving fast: while this was being written, the SoHo mural, the SoHo bike rack and the Chinatown lanterns were switched from cards to moments (uncommitted).
- **Effort.** S: a day or less, reuses an existing component, no new art. M: two to four days, a new moment file, small sprites or a small server list. L: a week or more, new room art, a server feature or multiplayer sync.
- **Copy in quotes is proposed.** About 30% humor, never in instructions, no em dashes.
- **Tester quotes** come from the round 2 journals and reports in this folder (Millie, Marcus, Kayla, Chloe, Billie, Diego, Jiwoo, Lukas, Ron, Tyler) and a few from round 1.
- **House rules applied everywhere below.** A miss is a joke, never a loss: treats you've bought are yours (`vend` re-equips owned food for free), so trying a ritual again costs nothing. No FOMO: drops stay in stock all week, daily finds come back tomorrow, the line is optional. 13+: no lottery, beer or cigarettes at the bodega; the penthouse bar pours seltzer.

## The district today, in one number

About 60 things in these nine rooms respond to a click. Of those: **14 open an info card** (8 of them have no button except "Got It": the SoHo hydrant, the Supreme neon, Nike's vault, Ralph's menu and coffee sacks, the Little Italy arch, Joe's oven and Joe's menu), **20 make your avatar say one line**, **9 open a shop catalog** (six in Supreme, three in Nike), **4 charge you instantly** (dumplings, boba, fruit, soda), **3 open a choose() menu** (the SoHo vendor, Ralph's counter, Joe's counter), **3 are moments** (wired today) and **1 is a game** (Carb-Jitsu at Joe's back table). Two more objects are traced and painted but do nothing at all (Ralph's "BOOK NOOK" staircase and its plants).

The testers said it plainly. "I read facts and click Got It; no game pulled me" (Millie, who left at Grand Central). "No slice in my hand, no fold, no bite. I wanted to see the thing" (Marcus, after ordering at Joe's). "The Soda Fridge gave a one-line joke and a coin sound, nothing more. Cute but a dead end" (Billie). And the one thing Millie loved was the one place a verb sneaked onto a card: "The CLANK is the best thing so far. Bang on the pipe."

## The info card: a one-page recommendation

**What's wrong.** A card puts a frame between the player and the thing: a picture on the left, history on the right, the pigeon's joke, a button. In this district it is the default (14 of about 60 clickable things), and 8 of those have nothing to do but "Got It". It teaches players that clicking means reading, and readers leave: "Everything is a text popup" (Tyler), "I read facts and click Got It" (Millie). The moments testers remember are the places a verb sneaked in: the radiator's "Bang on the Pipe", the swipe at the turnstile, stamping your MetroCard.

**The rule: the click is a verb, and the fact rides along.**

1. **A click makes your New Yorker do something to the thing, in the room, within half a second**: walk over, reach, pull, tap, sit. No overlay opens first.
2. **The thing answers**: a sound, an animation on its own cutout, a person's line, a change that stays.
3. **The fact arrives inside the action, never as a panel.** It's said by whoever works there (the counter guy, the barista, the host under the arch), printed on what you get (the cup sleeve, the receipt, the pizza box lid, the back of the photo strip, the sack's stencil), or run in tomorrow's paper. Each fact you trigger is filed silently in **City Notes**, a Stamp Book tab that collects facts the way Flag Roll Call collects flags, with a small "+ City Note" chip like "+XP". (This keeps your Nov 11 call on the landmark moments: no plaque, no caption strip, no button row.)
4. **It leaves a trace**: something in the world, on you or at home is different afterwards (your cup on the shelf, your headshot on the wall, leftovers in the fridge, sugar on your face, a pigeon that knows you).

**The ladder.** Every clickable thing sits on one rung, and nothing clickable sits below Touch.

| Rung | Length | What it is | Example here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glance | none | a hover label only; not clickable | the coat hooks, the manhole |
| Touch | 1 to 3 s | the object reacts, your pose, one line (`touch()`) | the Nike neon buzzes; Joe's pizza boxes topple and you restack them |
| Moment | 5 to 30 s | a verb, feedback, a payoff and stamps (easy, hard, secret) | fold the slice; bang the pipe |
| Job | 1.5 to 3 min | the games host: instructions, play, results with the board | Slice Factory, Bagel Counters |

**If a panel has to exist** (browsing many things to buy, the newspaper, choosing between prices): the verb is the first and biggest button; the picture shows the thing as it is now (your size, your name on it); two lines of text at most; the joke comes as the reaction after the verb; and "Got It" is banned, because a panel with nothing to do is a bug. Choices with prices become chips over the person selling them, not a modal (Ralph's, Joe's and the SoHo vendor today).

**Every moment needs a reason to repeat**: a daily seed (`nycDay` + `hash`, as the Bull does), a skill target with an easy, a hard and a secret stamp, or a "with them" variant whenever someone stands within the photo-booth radius (share the basket, hold a leash, the second cup).

**How to change it in code, everywhere at once.**

- `card()` in `rooms.ts` takes a required `verb` and an optional `note` id; the card text moves to `data/notes.ts` (City Notes). A card with no verb doesn't typecheck.
- Add `touch({ anim, sfx, pose, line, note })` to `roomfun.ts`, using `cutoutImg()` from `moments/kit.ts` to animate the object itself.
- Keep `picCard` for galleries where you browse things to buy (paintings, magnets) and for the quiet 9/11 Memorial card. Everything else migrates.
- Add a check to `tools/` (next to `pixel_audit`) that lists every object by rung and fails when a new object opens a `picCard` without actions. Put one number on Launch HQ: the share of clickable things at Moment or above (in this district today: 3 of about 60).

**Migration for the 8 verb-less cards here:** hydrant → the dog walker's stop; Supreme neon → "DROP TODAY" in the Thursday Line; the Vault → your vault; Ralph's menu → the cup sleeve in the pickup ritual; coffee sacks → scooping beans for the espresso; Little Italy arch → the host; Joe's oven → the reheat beat in the fold; Joe's menu → the counter guy's one-breath menu ("Plain, pepperoni, white. The sign is old. The pizza isn't."), which also answers the testers who flagged the 99-cent board.
