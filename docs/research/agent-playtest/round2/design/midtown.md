# Midtown: arrivals and getting around

Round 2 design audit. Rooms: `times-square`, `broadway`, `iloveny`, `rockefeller`, `fifth-ave`, `toy-palace` (Saks Fifth Avenue), `maison-malle` (Louis Vuitton), `grand-central`, `subway`, `empire-deck`, plus the map, the subway rides, the taxis and the arrival story.

**How this was checked.** I read `app/src/data/rooms.ts` as it stood at 09:26 on Nov 11 (other agents were wiring moments while I worked, so "Today" is that snapshot), every feature module those rooms call, the room art, and the round 2 journals. Then I fired every Midtown object on the dev server in headless Chromium on an offline `?profile=` slot: each one opened what the code says it opens, with 0 console errors. Quotes from testers are from `round2/journals/`.

**Effort.** S = 1 to 2 days with an existing kit, no new art. M = 3 to 5 days: a new moment or feature file, maybe one small sprite. L = 1 to 2 weeks: new scene art and/or state shared between players.

**Four enablers that make many rows below cheap.**

1. **The city clock.** `app/src/data/jam.ts` runs the Dance Jam on a clock every client agrees on, with no server. The same trick gives shared train arrivals, curtain times, the Zamboni, the studio's ON AIR, the track posting and the Saks light show.
2. **A relayed room event.** The server still relays the old snowball message (`t: 'sb'`, x, y in `server/src/game/realtime.ts`). A generic sibling (`t: 'fx'`, kind, x, y) lets other players see your moment: your pigeons, your toy cab, your steam ring, your scent trail, a static zap, who boarded the train.
3. **A small city store on the server**, rate limited like the guestbook, for traces that should outlast a visit: a styled mannequin, today's meetups under the clock, Lou's case, the acrobats' record.
4. **Art plates.** Anything that moves away from where it is painted (the train, a cab, the pigeons) needs its painted copy taken out of the background. That is why the Pizza Rat only hops on his spot (see the note in `features/subwayLife.ts`).

Copy in quotes below is proposed copy: hints of 2 to 4 words, rewards of 1 to 4 words, no fact text inside a moment.
