# Every place, leveled up

2026-11-11. The swipe-in, the Big Board and the memorial went from a card to something you do. This is the same pass for every other place: what it does today, what’s off, what it becomes. Screenshots are in the published page; the shots came from a private copy of the game with every object clicked.

## Five rules

- **Be there.** The thing fills the screen and you’re at it. First person when it’s an object; the room itself when it’s a place.
- **One verb.** Your hands do it: swipe, squeeze, fold, row, hold. Never a button that does it for you.
- **The true detail.** The station’s own bullets. GPUs sold out. A white rose on a birthday. One per thing, inside the action, never in a caption.
- **Something to keep.** A stamp, a streak, a sticker, a rose that stays. Easy, hard, and a secret.
- **The right volume.** Jokes at the Big Board, silence at the pools. Not every place should be a game.

## Times Square and Broadway

### Times Square

The most visited room in the game, and every click returns a speech bubble or a teleport. (24 of 24 testers started here)

- **Sal’s Dog** (Make it played, Medium). Today: Click the cart: a bubble, two coins gone, a hot dog in your hand.
  - Sal lifts the lid: steam, dogs bobbing in the water.
  - Fish one out with the tongs. The third try always works.
  - Pick a bottle and squeeze: your mustard line follows the mouse.
  - Ketchup gets a long, silent stare.
  - From your second day, Sal starts your usual before you ask.
  - Keep: Sal’s Regular (3 days), secret: The Stare
  - Evidence: Street carts were the second most-named true New York detail (19 of 24 testers). Every tester starts beside this one.
- **Hail a Cab** (Make it played, Large). Today: “TAXI!” in a bubble, then the island map opens.
  - Curb view: hold to raise your arm.
  - Lit roof light, free. Dark, taken. One pulls over.
  - The back seat: Taxi TV, the meter, the city sliding past.
  - You get out where you picked. The tip screen opens at 25%.
  - Keep: Hailed It, secret: Into the Street (step off the curb to hail, like a local)
  - Evidence: gabi: “I hoped to ride it or see a yellow cab drive.” 8 testers named yellow cabs as true New York.
- **Pigeons That React** (Make it played, Small). Today: “Coo coo!” in a bubble.
  - Walk through them: they burst up, circle and land behind you.
  - Coo at one and it follows you, head bobbing.
  - Three in tow earns Flock. A crowd scatters the whole square.
  - Evidence: Pigeons were the third most-named true detail (12 testers).
- **TKTS Under the Red Steps** (New, Medium). Today: The steps are three seats that locals usually take.

  - It gets you into tonight’s show at the Broadway Theater.
  - Evidence: billie (round 1): the red steps have 3 seats and locals take them.
