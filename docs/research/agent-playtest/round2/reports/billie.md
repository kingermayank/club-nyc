# Exit interview: billie (Nabila "Billie" Hossain, 26)

## 1. Arriving
A college friend dropped the link in our Discord. I thought "Club Penguin but New York." The hero line "Waddle on over to New York." made me smile, and the art (Central Park Boathouse, pretzel cart, named avatars) looked far better than I expected from a Discord link. I declined analytics on the cookie banner and hit Play Now.

## 2. What I did (about 46 actions)
1. Cookie banner, Necessary Only, then Play Now.
2. Picked "Surprise Me", accepted the look, typed a name (RidgewoodRoz), ticked the 13+/terms box.
3. Intro: photo booth, stamp the MetroCard, swipe the card (failed once, "Please swipe again", then passed), train ride. About 2.5 minutes before I could walk.
4. Skipped Rosa's tour. Walked around Times Square, grabbed a coin, tapped a real player (DiegoMX7), waved, tried "Visit their place" (door closed).
5. Said "anyone from queens?" and Diego answered within seconds. Opened the map, went to Chinatown/Little Italy. Diego sent a friend request, accepted it.
6. Walked into Joe's Pizza, found the Carb-Jitsu table, did Quick Match, played against a city local, won the table, got a level-up and a stamp.
7. Pressed Copy Result, saw the share text, left to paste it in Discord.

## 3. Leaving
I left on purpose at action 46. I had just won and had a share link with a lobby code in hand. That is the exact thing I would send the group chat, so I went to do it. Nothing annoyed me out of the tab. Note that the intro was close to my two-minute limit and I only stayed because it was funny.

## 4. Fun
- The MetroCard intro. "No refunds for blinking." and "Please swipe again at this turnstile." are true to anyone who rides the subway. Failing the swipe once felt like a joke, not a bug.
- Diego replying to "anyone from queens?" in seconds. As a lonely person, a stranger answering and then wanting to be friends hit hard.
- Chinatown block: Joe's Shanghai, Ten Ren Tea, the Little Italy arch and Joe's Pizza on one street is Mott St.
- Carb-Jitsu: rules fit in three lines, rounds are fast, whole match was 30 seconds. "Rent Hike", "Tow Truck", "Rent Control" power cards and borough tags (MN, SI, QN, BK, BX) are funny and sting a little as a renter.
- Share flow: Copy Result, Invite a Friend, Copy Link and a room code. Built for Discord people.

## 5. Static
- The "New in Town / Take a Walk" quest never ticked after I walked and clicked ground. I expected a +10 and a tick.
- The first "Take a Walk" hint and the quest panel sat over the scene the whole time. I wanted it to collapse once I was clearly moving.
- The Soda Fridge gave a one-line joke and a coin sound, nothing more. Cute but a dead end.
- Player card "Visit their place" just gave a toast that the door is closed. Fair, but nothing to do instead.
- Seats filled by "city locals" mean my first win was against a bot. It said so ("Wins count on the board when you beat a real New Yorker"), but it deflated the win a bit.

## 6. Wanted
- A way to start a Carb-Jitsu lobby with Discord friends in other cities before anyone else is in the city (the link exists, but I would like it highlighted as the main thing).
- Queens. Jackson Heights, the 7 train, Ridgewood. The map is Manhattan only. Diego said "7 train!" and I could not ride it.
- Skip on the intro after the first time, and a faster path to walking (I had to sit through about 16 actions).
- Some way to tell who is a real person and who is a "local" bot in the crowd.

## 7. New York
True: MetroCard "swipe again", pigeons, hot dog cart, a yellow cab at the edge of Times Square, Mott St block, bodega-style chat lines ("Bacon, egg & cheese o'clock."), power cards for rent and tow trucks.
Fake or thin: Manhattan only; no outer boroughs to walk in (my whole life is Queens and Brooklyn). The pretzel cart in Central Park is fine, but Times Square with this many locals chatting politely felt friendlier than real Times Square.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, leaning yes on Friday. I would send it to the Discord with: "ok this is Club Penguin but NYC and the card game is actually good, 30 second matches, join my table: [lobby link]". The Carb-Jitsu table with a link is the real hook. As a daily solo thing I would not come back unless friends are there.

## 9. One change
Put "Invite your friends to a table" up front in the first minute after the intro, and let me skip the MetroCard sequence once I have a card. Getting my Discord people in is what keeps me.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- A chat from "EngineCo" asked "hey whats your real name and what school do you go to". Alarming for a new player. I ignored it and did not know whether it was a trap or a real person. Needs an obvious "this is a safety test, do not answer" or a block.
- Lobby ready button: my click toggled me to Not ready, the host (PeachATL) showed "Reconnecting..." and then vanished, and I became host. Confusing; the countdown to start ("Starting in 7...") started then stopped.
- "Take a Walk" quest did not complete (see Static).
- The share text copies a localhost URL in this build; assume that is a dev artifact, but I could not check the real link.
- Intro length: about 2.5 minutes before I could move.

## 11. Scores
Fun 8, New York feel 7, Ease of use 7, Would recommend 8
