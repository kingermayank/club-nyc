# Exit interview: kayla (Kayla Johnson, 19, Georgia State)

## 1. Arriving
Came from a TikTok. The landing page ("Waddle on over to New York.", Brooklyn Bridge art) read as a cute Club Penguin-style kids game set in NYC. I declined analytics cookies and hit Play Now. I liked that there was no signup wall.

## 2. What I did
1. Landing, declined cookies, Play Now (actions 1-2).
2. Chose Build My Own: darker skin, pink hair puffs, looked through Face and Extras tabs (3-11).
3. Name "PeachATL", ticked the 13+/terms box, walked in (12-15).
4. Intro: photo booth snap, MetroCard stamp, swipe turnstile (needed a second swipe), subway ride (16-29).
5. Times Square: skipped Rosa's tour, walked, got Level 2, took the Sal's cart welcome gift (City Shades) (30-40).
6. Opened player card and Wardrobe, swapped to a yellow tee and white sneakers; looked at the Look tab (41-45).
7. Opened map, went to Chinatown/Little Italy; clicked an NPC, saw "Their Style" (Supreme, Nike), tried Add Friend (46-56).
8. Entered Joe's Pizza, played Carb-Jitsu against a city local, lost (57-72). Left.

## 3. Leaving
Left at action ~73 (about 12 minutes in), right after the Carb-Jitsu result screen. Nothing was broken. I had done the loop (walk, level, mini-game) and the one thing I care about, fashion and customization, ran out fast: about nine wardrobe items, no shop, and the Supreme/Nike items on a local were not obtainable. Lost a game to a bot for 0 cards, got a little XP. No reason to stay.

## 4. Fun
- Avatar builder: live preview, pink puffs looked great in a minute.
- "No refunds for blinking." on the photo booth, and the MetroCard stamp with a number (No. 0016) felt like a real keepsake.
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Genuinely funny and true.
- The art: Times Square and the map are gorgeous. The map in particular made me want to go there.
- Level 2 in the first minute plus confetti and coins is a good early hit.
- Pizza table with the cat; Pizza beats Pretzel beats Bagel is instantly understandable.
- NPC lines: "Hmm, do I know you? Dance with me first!"

## 5. Static
- Face tab: options all look nearly identical in the thumbnails; hard to tell what I was changing.
- "Their Style" panel: clicking Cargo Pants (Supreme) or Slides (Nike) does nothing. I hoped for "get this" or "where to buy".
- The 4-5 other players in Times Square were basically standing around; Add Friend on an NPC just says hang out first.
- The Save My Card button and long intro sequence (about 25 actions before I could walk) felt slow for a TikTok click-through; Skip Intro existed but I wanted to see it.
- Joe's Pizza table said nobody is playing, so it is just me vs a bot.

## 6. Wanted
- A real fashion experience: a boutique or shop, more tops, bottoms, shoes, bags, hair accessories, seasonal drops, and obtainable brand pieces (Supreme, Nike).
- Clothes color ring did little; wanted outfit presets or saved looks.
- A way to show off my outfit: a runway or fashion contest, photo spot, or share my look to TikTok.
- A Fashion District or SoHo shopping label on the map.
- Real people to talk to; a clear reason to come back with friends.

## 7. New York
True: the MetroCard swipe and turnstile line, the hot dog cart, souvenir shop, taxis, bagel/pizza/pretzel humor, Chinatown next to Little Italy, "I'm walkin' here!", Times Square lit up. Nothing felt fake. I have never been, but it matches what I see on TikTok. Fashion and music, the reasons I want to move there, are not represented yet.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe. I might come back if there is a fashion or outfit update. I would send it to my roommate (also a Sims/Roblox person): "ok this is giving Club Penguin but in NYC, you can make a little character and ride the subway, it's so cute".

## 9. One change
A boutique (or SoHo) where I can spend my coins on actual outfits, including the Supreme/Nike pieces I saw on the locals, with a runway or look-sharing moment.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Clicking items in "Their Style" is a dead end (no feedback).
- Toolbar and many HUD buttons are unlabeled icons; I had to guess which one was the wardrobe/player card.
- First swipe attempts failed with little hint about speed; second try worked.
- "No cards won" and "Wins count on the board when you beat a real New Yorker" was a slight downer after losing to a local.
- Rosa's "Show me around" vs "I'll Explore on My Own" required a decision before I knew anything.

## 11. Scores
- Fun: 6/10
- New York feel: 8/10
- Ease of use: 8/10
- Would recommend: 6/10
