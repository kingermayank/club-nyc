# Exit interview: wei (Wei Chen, 20, Baruch accounting, Flushing)

## 1. Arriving
Cousin's Instagram story. I thought "Club Penguin but New York", nostalgia bait. The hero art (Central Park, pretzel cart) looked better than I expected. Declined analytics cookies, hit Play Now.

## 2. What I did
Landing page, Play Now, Surprise Me avatar, name WeiBao7, tick the age/terms box. MetroCard photo booth, stamp, swipe mini-game (failed twice: "Please swipe again", "A little quicker"), rode the 1 to Times Square. Skipped Rosa's tour, walked around, opened the I Heart NY shop (catalog), took the free hat from Sal's hot dog cart. Opened the subway map, rode to Chinatown. Clicked Joe's Shanghai and Ten Ren Tea (a line plus coins). Typed "anyone from queens" in chat, nobody answered. Opened Halal Rush from a notice, served one order, started a second. Left at about 64 actions (roughly 10 minutes).

## 3. Leaving
After the second halal order I realised the loop was the same order again, and everything else was clicking shops for a line of text and coins. I asked for Queens in chat, got no reply, and the map has no 7 train or Queens. Nothing was pulling me forward, so I closed it.

## 4. Fun
- Halal Rush. Controls were obvious, first plate worked, "+$12 Yesss!". Overcooked-lite, and white sauce / hot sauce is correct.
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Painfully accurate, and the swipe being finicky is the joke.
- "Soup dumplings. Nibble, sip, then eat." Someone actually knows how you eat them.
- Fast level-ups and coin pops felt good early. The Times Square and Chinatown art looks great.

## 5. Static
- Souvenir shop: a catalog panel with 8 pages of tees. I wanted to walk around and mess with stuff.
- Joe's Shanghai and Ten Ren Tea: click, one line, coins. A picture with text.
- The other people in rooms (BroadwayBen, DeliDanny, ConeyKai) say canned lines ("Race you to the pizza place?"), never reply. Feels like bots.
- The 5 "New in Town" quests are tutorial checkboxes.

## 6. Wanted
- Anyone to talk back, or a clearly real player.
- Queens/Flushing and the 7 train, which the intro showed me.
- Something to do in Chinatown beyond clicking storefronts.
- A way to skip the 3-4 second waits during the train ride.

## 7. New York
True: Joe's Shanghai, Ten Ren, correct 新鲜蔬菜 sign, soup-dumpling line, halal cart at W 49 St and 5 Av with white and hot sauce, turnstile joke, rat carrying a slice.
Fake: the subway map is Manhattan only, with no Queens. The intro board lists the 7 but the map has no 7. A "2 / 7 Av Express" goes to Chinatown (the 2 does not go to Canal St that way). Chinatown here is the tourist postcard version (Mott St), nothing like Flushing's.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, but probably not tomorrow. Halal Rush alone isn't enough. I'd send it to my cousin: "the halal cart game is kinda good, the rest is cute but empty."

## 9. One change
Make the rooms feel populated by real people who respond, or give me a second hook like Halal Rush in each neighborhood. Add Flushing and the 7.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Souvenir shop exit door is huge and sits where I walked when leaving; I re-entered the shop by accident.
- The MetroCard swipe needs a specific speed; the hints help but the first drag said "All the way through" and did nothing.
- "Show Me" in the quest panel did not visibly point to the hot dog cart.
- Clicking shop fronts gives different coin results (+57, then -4) with no explanation.

## 11. Scores
Fun 6, New York feel 6, Ease of use 7, Would recommend 5.
