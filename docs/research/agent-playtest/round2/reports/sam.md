# Sam Okafor (sam) - exit interview

## 1. Arriving
A viewer DM'd me "you'd either love or hate this". I landed on a marketing page: "Waddle on over to New York", Brooklyn Bridge art, a Club Penguin pitch set in NYC. I thought: cute, probably shallow, but I will go check the subway first. Accepted necessary-only cookies.

## 2. What I did
Play Now, Surprise Me avatar, name IRTSam, ticked age/terms box. Intro: MetroCard photo booth, stamp the card, then a turnstile swipe (failed once on purpose, "Please swipe again at this turnstile" got a real laugh), rode a 1 to Times Square. Skipped Rosa's tour. Opened the island map, then the subway station and the subway diagram, hovered stations, rode to Grand Central (got put on the 7, correct). Clicked the zodiac ceiling, got the "painted backwards" fact. Used the map to get to Brooklyn Bridge. Left around action 39.

## 3. Leaving
I left because I had seen what I came for (subway and landmarks), not because I was bored or annoyed. A longer session would need a reason beyond sightseeing; I'd return for Rush Hour Run.

## 4. Fun
- The turnstile swipe minigame, with the "Please swipe again" failure. True to life.
- Subway station: pizza rat, NEW YORK tile mosaic, yellow tactile edge, busker line "Stand clear of the closing doors, my love".
- Hovering stations shows real service bullets (Grand Central 4 5 6 7 S; Brooklyn Bridge-City Hall 4 5 6 J Z). Correct, and nobody checks that.
- Grand Central ceiling: backwards stars and the uncleaned dark patch are both real.
- 7 train chosen automatically for Times Sq to Grand Central.

## 5. Static
- The island map: looks like an illustration, not a map. Nothing to learn from it, just a teleport menu.
- Subway car interior while riding: pretty but a scene transition, nothing to do on the ride.
- Intro bullet strip and in-car strip list lines without any logic (1 2 3 4 5 6 B D F M A C E).

## 6. Wanted
- A real route: pick a start and end, see the transfers, ride in the correct car.
- Express vs local behaviour: trains skipping stations, a visible difference.
- The 5, 7, J, Z, N/Q/R/W, A/C/E as drawn lines (they only appear as hover bullets or not at all).
- Real-time or timetable flavor: train countdown, delays, service changes.
- Click-for-history hooks on the 1904 IRT stations (City Hall, 28th St tile, Old City Hall loop).

## 7. New York
True: line colors and families, shuttle at 42 St, Grand Central's 4/5/6/7/S, four-faced clock, ceiling trivia, 1 stops at 59 St-Columbus Circle, 14 St, WTC, South Ferry; Times Sq-Grand Central by the 7; MTA car interiors with orange and yellow seats; "stand clear of the closing doors".
False or sloppy: the map shows Manhattan as a fat blob with the Statue of Liberty glued to the Battery and no avenues; the subway diagram puts the 1 and N at Rockefeller Center (that is B/D/F/M); Times Sq has only 1 2 N on the diagram (really also 3 7 Q R W S A C E); the in-car line strip is a random mix; the Whispering Gallery sits off the main concourse (it's downstairs); the departures board is placed on the wrong wall.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe. I'd come back for Rush Hour Run and to see the other stations, and I'd probably make a short video about the subway side. I'd send it to a Mini Metro friend: "A cozy NYC world where the subway is mostly accurate and the MetroCard swipe fails the way it does in real life. Try the ceiling at Grand Central."

## 9. One change
Make the subway a real system: a proper diagram with all the lines, express and local behaviour, and a ride that reflects the route.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- The subway panel legend says six lines but the ride used the 7, which is not in the legend.
- Times Square intro says "1 train" to Times Square while the strip shows a mix of lines.
- Tooltip bullets and drawn lines disagree (J/Z/5/7 appear only in the tooltip).

## 11. Scores
Fun 7, New York feel 8, ease of use 8, would recommend 7.
