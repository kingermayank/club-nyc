# Kevin O'Brien (41, Boston PM, checking it for his 13-year-old)

1. **Arriving**: Son mentioned it at dinner; I looked it up. Landed on a Club Penguin-style game set in NYC. Cookie banner first; I chose "Necessary Only". Hero is friendly and clearly aimed at kids and nostalgic adults.

2. **What I did**: Declined analytics, went straight to Safety, then FAQ ("Is it free?"). Clicked Play Now as a guest, chose Surprise Me, typed a name, ticked the 13+ box. Did the MetroCard photo booth intro, skipped Rosa's tour. Typed a phone number in chat to test the filter. Tried to click the one real player to find Report/Block. Opened Account tab. Left (about action 26).

3. **Leaving**: I had what I came for. Left because the safety questions were mostly answered, and the one thing I couldn't verify (the report button) wasn't worth more time. Not annoyed, just done.

4. **Fun**: Typing a phone number and seeing it turn into "[removed]" instantly was the best moment for me. Safety page was short, plain and had no fluff. The FAQ answer "nothing to buy, coins can't be bought or sold" is the line a parent wants.

5. **Static**: The Safety page reads like a rules poster. "Come Hang Out on Discord" link makes me nervous with kids (moves chat off-site, which the rules themselves forbid). Nothing about parents anywhere.

6. **Wanted**: A "For Parents" page: can chat be turned off or limited to preset phrases? Who moderates and how fast? What happens to the selfie in "Use a Photo"? Whether the Discord is moderated. Parental consent at 13 (the age gate is only a checkbox).

7. **New York**: Times Square with a pretzel/halal-cart flavored crowd and names like HalalForLife and EggCreamEmma felt right. A super named Rosa is funny. I only saw Times Square so can't say more.

8. **Coming back**: Maybe. I'd come back to check the report flow and read the parent info if it existed. I'd message other parents: "Safe-looking Club Penguin-style game for 13+, free, nothing to buy, chat filtered. Check the Safety page before your kid plays."

9. **One change**: A clear Parents page, and a visible Report/Block button when I click another player.

10. **Bugs and confusion**: Clicking RonInTucson (the only real player) opened my own wardrobe panel instead of his player card, so I never saw Report/Block. Escape didn't close the panel. "Use a Photo" selfie option for 13-year-olds with no explanation up front. Age gate is self-declared checkbox only.

11. **Scores**: fun 6, New York feel 6, ease of use 7, would recommend 7.
