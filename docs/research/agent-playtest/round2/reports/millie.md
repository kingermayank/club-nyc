# Millie Arroyo (61) exit interview

## 1. Arriving
My daughter texted "ma try this lol" at 9 pm after a shift. I figured it was some kids' penguin thing. The landing picture (Rockefeller rink, "Waddle on over to New York") was pretty, but "Remember waddling around..." is a line for my daughter's generation, not mine. I clicked Necessary Only on the cookies and Play Now.

## 2. What I did (about 48 actions)
- Skipped "Use a Photo" (not putting my face anywhere), took Surprise Me, called myself BronxMillie, ticked the 13+ box.
- Intro sequence: photo booth, stamp the MetroCard, swipe the MetroCard through a turnstile (needed a retry, then Space), subway ride into Times Square.
- Rosa the super offered a tour; I said "Explore on my own". Walked, got a free NYC cap from Sal's hot dog cart, hit Level 2.
- Opened the map (Manhattan only), went to My Place (5th-floor walk-up). Clicked the radiator and banged the pipe. Read the newspaper. Opened the phone menu.
- Took the map to Grand Central, clicked the departures board, typed "any Bronx people here?", got no reply, left.

## 3. Leaving
Action 48, about ten minutes in. Nobody answered my chat. The one line I got back, "Dance with me! Press D", is clearly a bot. Everything I clicked was a fact popup that I read and dismissed with "Got It". No game or task pulled me in. I was tired and my tea was cold. I stopped because I'd seen the charm and found nothing to do, not because anything annoyed me.

## 4. Fun
- Rosa, "your building's super", with "I've got a boiler to fix." My father was the super in our building, so that hit me.
- The radiator. Heat season facts (68 degrees, Oct 1 to May 31) are right, the CLANK is right, and "My neighbor bangs back. We're basically friends now." made me smile.
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Every New Yorker knows that line from the old cards.
- Free hat from the hot dog guy, "first one's on the house."
- The newspaper: date and weather correct, the 1904 subway nickel from City Hall to 145th is correct, "I came for the bagel and stayed for the bagel."
- Grand Central looked real: the four-faced clock, Whispering Gallery, the board, the ceiling.

## 5. Static
- Grand Central departures board: a fact card with "Got It". I wanted to see trains and times, or pick a train home.
- Newspaper: a wall of text I skimmed. I wanted short headlines I could tap.
- The hot dog cart gift, level-up and stamp toasts: points and coins with no reason to care.
- The "New in Town" checklist ("Make a Friend": click any New Yorker): it told me what to do instead of letting me find it.
- The other "New Yorkers" in Times Square and Grand Central just stand there or loop a canned line. They don't talk back.

## 6. Wanted
- The Bronx: the 4 train, Yankee Stadium, Arthur Avenue, the Concourse. The map said "Map of Manhattan" and stopped.
- A person to answer me.
- To skip the intro. It's five screens (booth, stamp, card, swipe, ride) before I could walk anywhere. The Skip Intro button was there, but I did not trust it not to skip my character.
- Something like a real job, a daily puzzle, or a Spelling Bee-sized thing I could finish in 15 minutes.

## 7. New York
True: the radiator and heat law, the fire escape and water tanks, 44 platforms at Grand Central, "please swipe again," the 1904 subway, Rosa the super, Times Square being something you push through, the hot dog cart.
False or dated: MetroCards. They're gone. We tap OMNY. "Daily MetroCard refill" and "swipe in" feel like someone who hasn't ridden lately. Also the whole picture is tourist Manhattan: soup dumplings, souvenirs, Times Square. That's the part I avoid, and it leaves out the five boroughs most people live in.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, leaning no. If I came back it would be for the walk-up and Rosa, not for the game. I'd send it to my daughter only if it had the Bronx. What I'd text her: "It's cute, the radiator got me. Where's the Bronx?"

## 9. One change
Put a real Bronx in, even one block of the Grand Concourse with a bodega and a super's stoop, and have someone or something answer when I talk.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Swipe card: my first drag was rejected ("All the way through the reader") and I wasn't sure how far or how fast; "press Space" fixed it.
- I wasn't sure whether the "Use a Photo" option would upload my face to somewhere. I avoided it.
- Chat to the other players got no reply, and I couldn't tell who was a real person and who a bot.
- Toolbar has about ten icons with no labels until you hover.

## 11. Scores (out of 10)
- Fun: 6
- New York feel: 6 (strong details, but Manhattan-tourist and MetroCards are out of date)
- Ease of use: 6 (clear, but too many steps up front)
- Would recommend: 5
