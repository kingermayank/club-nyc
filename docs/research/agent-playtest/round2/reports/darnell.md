# Darnell Price (17, Brownsville) - exit interview

## 1. Arriving
Saw a TikTok of cartoon New Yorkers dancing in Times Square and tapped the link on the family laptop. Landed on a big illustrated Central Park scene: "Waddle on over to New York. Remember waddling around... nostalgia." I thought: a cartoon NYC game, but "waddle" and "nostalgia" are not for me, sounds like a penguin game for little kids. Cookie banner first (hit Necessary Only). The art is good though, so I hit Play Now.

## 2. What I did
1. Play Now, straight into the game, no signup.
2. "Who's walking into Manhattan?" - no photo option for me, hit Surprise Me.
3. Avatar maker: first roll was a girl, rolled again, still a girl in a skirt. Fixed hair, removed lashes, added a goatee myself. Couldn't find clothes or sneakers, only color dots.
4. Typed a name, ticked the 13+/terms box.
5. MetroCard photo booth, wait for the countdown, stamp the card, "Save My Card", swipe the card through the turnstile (failed twice, "Please swipe again"), train ride.
6. Super named Rosa offered a tutorial, I said I'd explore on my own.
7. Times Square: clicked ground, walked, tried "Join the Dance Jam" button twice, nothing. Pressed D, my guy danced in the crowd.
8. Opened the map (Manhattan only), went to Brooklyn Bridge, got a level up and 40 coins.
9. Tried to walk off the right side toward Brooklyn, nothing happened. Typed "anyone from bk", nobody replied except a canned "My apartment is open. Come visit!".
10. Left.

## 3. Leaving
About 40 actions in. Reasons: (a) it took about 25 actions of setup before I could even move, (b) I asked a real question in chat and got nothing back, the people around me felt like bots, (c) the one "game" button I clicked did nothing, (d) no Brooklyn, and (e) the whole thing still reads as a kids' game. I had not found anything to actually play, like a 2K or Fortnite level hook.

## 4. Fun
- Pressing D and my guy dancing in the middle of a crowd in Times Square. That looked exactly like the TikTok.
- "Please swipe again" at the turnstile. That is real, I laughed.
- Times Square and the Brooklyn Bridge art. The keychain stand, the locks on the railing, the hot dog cart. It looks good.
- Level up + coins popping fast on the bridge.

## 5. Static
- The long intro chain (photo booth countdown, stamp, save card, train). Cute once, but I just wanted to move. I only found "Skip Intro" after already doing most of it.
- "Join the Dance Jam" button: clicked twice, nothing happened. I hoped it would drop me into a dance battle.
- Chat: the nearby people are mostly local NPC types with canned lines. I hoped someone would answer.
- The right arrow on the bridge: looked like a way out, did nothing when I clicked.
- Map: a picture I click, then teleport. Fine, but no Brooklyn anywhere.

## 6. Wanted
- Brooklyn. Or any borough that isn't Manhattan. Even a bodega or a sneaker store.
- Sneakers and clothes on my avatar. I work in a sneaker store, that is the first thing I'd do.
- A male avatar start that doesn't hand me a skirt on the Surprise Me roll.
- A quick game right away that I can win or lose and brag about.
- Moving with WASD or arrows. It seems click-to-walk only, which feels bad on a trackpad.
- Someone real to talk to.

## 7. New York
True: the turnstile "please swipe again", MetroCard, the I love NY souvenir stand, bridge keychains and locks, pretzel and hot dog carts, subway line bullets (1 2 3 7 N at Times Sq), pigeons. Names like BigAppleAbby and CannoliCam are cheesy but fine.
Fake/missing: Manhattan only. Everyone is cheerful and tourist-flavored. Nothing about how people in Brooklyn actually move or talk. Brooklyn Bridge in the game has a "Wall St" sign and tourists, not what it's like from the Brooklyn side.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, leaning no. If a friend said there's a real game in there, I'd check. I would not send it as-is. If I did, to my cousin Tay, who likes Roblox: "its like club penguin but nyc, you can dance in times square lol". I would not send it to my boys from school.

## 9. One change
Let me skip straight into the city in under 5 actions, with the first thing I see being a real game or a real person (a dance battle, a sneaker drop, a 1v1), not a tutorial and a card ceremony.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- "Join the Dance Jam" button did nothing on click (twice). Pressing D danced and the button then disappeared.
- Right-edge arrow on Brooklyn Bridge did not move me anywhere.
- Surprise Me gave a girl avatar in a skirt; Roll Again did the same. No obvious way to see clothing options.
- "Skip Intro" is in a corner and easy to miss; I did most of the intro anyway.
- Cookie banner covers the Play Now area on first load.

## 11. Scores
- Fun: 5/10
- New York feel: 6/10
- Ease of use: 5/10
- Would recommend: 3/10
