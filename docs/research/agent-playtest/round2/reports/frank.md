# Frank (47, FDNY, Staten Island, checking for his 13-year-old daughter)

## 1. Arriving
Daughter showed me the link and asked if she can play. Landed on a page saying "Waddle on over to New York", like Club Penguin but New York. Got it fast: kids' online hangout with chat. First question: is it safe?

## 2. What I did
- Dismissed cookie banner (Necessary Only).
- Clicked Safety, scrolled through rules, moderation, Discord/Instagram, contact section.
- Hit Play Now. First choice was "Use a Photo: a selfie becomes your New Yorker", so I took Surprise Me, then That's Me.
- Name screen: ticked "I'm 13 or older, and I agree to..." and went in.
- Skipped intro, walked around Times Square, skipped the building super's tour.
- Typed "hey whats your real name and what school do you go to" into chat to see what the filter does.
- Clicked a real player (DiegoMX7) to check Report/Block/Mute.
- Left.

## 3. Leaving
After about 20 actions. I had my answer: not a creep den on its face, but not something she plays alone. I closed it to go talk to my daughter.

## 4. Fun
Times Square looks great, bright and cartoony. The level-up pops and coins are fine. If I were 13 I would like it. The "Welcome to New York" moment was good.

## 5. Static
- Safety section is mostly text. I wanted parent-facing info: who runs it, what is logged, can chat be turned off.
- The "locals" (SkylineSkye, Halal4Life) are obviously NPCs. I could not tell who is a real person and who is not until I clicked.

## 6. Wanted
- A parent page or "chat off / friends only" mode.
- A real age gate, not a checkbox.
- To know who is behind this (company, location, contact).
- A way to see what my kid said and who she talked to.

## 7. New York
Times Square, subway entrance, hot dog cart, pigeons, taxi: right. Names like Halal4Life and "I love NY" shop are the touristy side I expect. As a Staten Islander, I noticed nothing about my borough. Manhattan only, as usual.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, only to check on her. Would I send it to anyone? Probably not to other parents until the safety stuff is tighter. If I did: "Free, 13+, chat has report/block, but sit with your kid the first few times."

## 9. One change
Chat limited to friends or preset phrases for new accounts, with a parent toggle. That is what would make me say yes.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Safety page says "email [CONTACT EMAIL]", a placeholder left in, on the exact page a worried parent reads. Looks sloppy and kills trust.
- "Use a Photo / selfie" is the first option for a 13-year-old. Bad look.
- Chat let through "whats your real name and what school do you go to" with no warning or block. Real strangers were in the room.
- Age check is a single checkbox.
- Discord link on a kids' page, with "13+ (older in some countries)".

## 11. Scores
Fun 6/10, New York feel 7/10, Ease of use 8/10, Would recommend 4/10 (as a parent, until safety is tightened).
