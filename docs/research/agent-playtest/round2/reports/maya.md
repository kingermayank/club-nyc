# Exit interview: maya (Maya Goldstein, 16, UWS)

## 1. Arriving
A classmate posted the link in the group chat with "this is so us". I landed on a hero of the Brooklyn Bridge with a cartoon Club Penguin-ish look and "Waddle on over to New York." I figured it was a Club Penguin clone set in NYC. I picked "Necessary Only" on the cookie banner and hit Play Now.

## 2. What I did (about 31 actions)
1. Cookie banner, then Play Now.
2. Picked "Build My Own", changed hair a little, "That's Me".
3. Typed the name JoesOnBway, ticked the 13+ box, walked in.
4. Intro: MetroCard photo booth (picked Big Grin, snapped), stamped the card ("New Yorker No. 0006"), swiped it at a turnstile. First swipe: "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Second swipe worked and a 1 train arrived.
5. Landed in Times Square with a few other players. Rosa the super offered a tour; I chose "I'll Explore on My Own".
6. Opened the map and clicked the green area at the top for Central Park. It put me on the Empire State Building observatory with confetti and Level 2.
7. Tried the binocular viewer (it listed "Central Park" as something to look at) and said "is there a central park" in chat. No reply.
8. Opened the map again, clicked The Met. Empty steps, pretzel and hot dog carts, then left.

## 3. Leaving
I left after about 30 actions at The Met. It was pretty and funny, but I'd seen the joke. There was nobody to talk to (my chat got no answer, and BroadwayBen just danced), no Central Park or UWS, and I couldn't tell what I was supposed to do next besides walk around and collect stamps. I'd screenshot it for the group chat and close it.

## 4. Fun
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." That is exactly every New Yorker's life, and it made me laugh. It also made the swipe feel like a real little game.
- "No refunds for blinking" on the photo booth, and stamping my own MetroCard with a member number.
- The avatar maker. It's quick, cute and colorful.
- The map art and the Met scene (fall leaves, fountains, pretzel cart, taxi). It looks like a real place.
- The confetti level-up with a title ("Day-Tripper"). It's a small hit of reward.

## 5. Static
- The cookie banner and terms checkbox. Standard, but it's friction before the fun.
- Binocular viewer: just a list of "Central Park, water towers". I hoped to see them or click one, and instead I only moved the mouse around.
- Chatting: I asked a question and nothing happened. I hoped for a reply or a reaction from the other people.
- "New in Town" quest panel sat on screen with "Take a Walk, click anywhere on the ground". It feels like homework in a world with nothing pulling me in.
- The Met: pretty, but empty. Hot dog and pretzel carts looked clickable and I didn't get to see if they did anything.

## 6. Wanted
- Central Park (a place to just hang out), the Upper West Side, Joe's on Broadway, the East Village.
- The 1 train I actually ride (96th St, 86th St).
- Someone to talk to. A reply to my chat.
- Understand clearly where on the map each place is before I get teleported. The map click went to a different place than I aimed for.

## 7. New York
True: the turnstile swipe, the "I'm walkin' here!" line, pigeons on the street, hot dog and pretzel carts, the Met steps, fall leaves, and the superintendent as the welcoming character (very NYC).
False or off: the whole map is Times Square, Empire State, Wall Street and other tourist spots. Real teens don't go there. Times Square as the starting spot made me roll my eyes (though it is the joke). Nothing is residential or local.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe. I'd come back if my friends were in it with me. Alone, probably no. I'd send it to the group chat: "ok the turnstile part is so us 😭 'please swipe again' i cant. we need central park added tho". I'd send it to the classmate who posted it, and maybe my friend who plays Roblox.

## 9. One change
Put Central Park and a neighborhood hangout (UWS or the East Village) on the map, with other people actually there and talking back. That would make me want to bring my friends.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Clicking the Central Park area on the map took me to the Empire State Building. It wasn't clear what the map click targets were, and the hover label only said "Empire State Building" after I'd clicked.
- The swipe drag was finicky: my first drag didn't register, the second was "too fast", the third worked. A person might give up there. The "press Space" hint was helpful.
- Chat question got no response and no feedback on whether anyone saw it.
- The tutorial offer from Rosa was easy to skip, so I never learned what the 5 "New in Town" steps were for.
- Several chat controls on the bottom bar are icons with no labels, so I didn't know what most did.

## 11. Scores
- Fun: 6/10
- New York feel: 6/10 (the details are right, the places are tourist-y)
- Ease of use: 7/10
- Would recommend: 6/10
