# diego exit interview (full session, about 79 actions)

## 1. Arriving
My cousin in Queens sent the link on WhatsApp. I saw "Club NYC", a cartoon Brooklyn Bridge and "Waddle on over to New York". I thought: Club Penguin, but New York. I clicked Play Now right away and dismissed the cookie box with "Necessary Only".

## 2. What I did
1. Play Now went straight to a character picker, no signup. "Surprise Me", "That's Me", typed a name, ticked the 13+ box. (My random guy looked like a grandma with gray hair. Funny.)
2. Intro: photo booth, stamped a MetroCard, swiped it through a turnstile (first swipe failed with a joke, second worked), rode the 1 train to Times Square.
3. Times Square: Rosa the super offered a tour, I skipped it. Clicked the ground, the hot dog cart, picked a Foam Liberty Crown.
4. A player asked "anyone from queens?"; I answered "my cousins live in queens, 7 train!", sent a friend request, she accepted within seconds.
5. Map, Brooklyn Bridge, bought a pigeon painting. Apartment: bought a sofa and bean bag, placed them.
6. Map, Chinatown / Little Italy, Joe's Pizza. The "Beat It" notice was a leaderboard. Then I found the Carb-Jitsu sign (pizza beats pretzel beats bagel), pressed Quick Match, then "Play a Local" and played CoffeeCarla. Lost 3 cards to 3 cards on the tiebreak ("So Close"), got 20 XP and 3 coins.

## 3. Leaving
I closed the tab after the card game because I had been playing a good while and it was dinner time. I was not bored. I wanted a rematch, but it was a natural stopping point and nothing was pulling me to the next thing. I did not find a second game before leaving.

## 4. Fun
- The Carb-Jitsu card game. Real choices, both flip together, cards have boroughs (BX, QN, BK), and the round results explain themselves ("Knots beat holes"). It took about 50 seconds. This is what I came for.
- Level-ups, coins, stamps popping every few clicks, like Roblox quests.
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Made me laugh.
- "anyone from queens?" then a friend accept. Felt like real people.
- Cute map, click a place and you are there.
- Apartment with a window onto the Empire State Building, placing furniture myself. The painter's jokes ("He would.").

## 5. Static
- Rosa's tour popup (skipped, just reading).
- The cookie banner.
- The intro before the first walk was long: booth, stamp, swipe, train ride.
- Painter and furniture catalog are menus: pick, pay, done.
- Walking to a coin on the ground took long and nothing much happened.
- The "Beat It" bagel notice was just a leaderboard pop-up, I expected a game.

## 6. Wanted
- A game within the first minutes. It took me about 60 actions to stumble on Carb-Jitsu, and I only found it because the notice made me look at Joe's Pizza.
- Queens, the 7 train, Flushing Meadows on the map. Only Manhattan is there.
- Names on the bottom bar icons.
- A rematch button that is obvious right after the game ends (I saw "Rematch!" only as a chat chip).
- More than 3 coins for a whole match.
- A better look than the random grandma without a long editor.

## 7. New York
True: turnstile "please swipe again", pigeons, hot dog carts, I Heart NY stands, tiny walk-up with fire escape and radiator, Joe's Pizza in Little Italy, the rat and slice chat, boroughs on the cards (I noticed QN).
Fake or missing: no Queens on the map. Times Square felt quiet and small compared to the real one, but that is fine for a game.

## 8. Coming back
Yes, probably tomorrow. I want a rematch with CoffeeCarla, and my cousin plays so I can decorate and chat. I would send it to my cousin and my school friends: "es como Club Penguin pero Nueva York, gratis, y se juega en la compu de la escuela. Hay un juego de cartas de pizza".

## 9. One change
Put the card game (or any real game) in the first two minutes, right after Times Square, and shorten the MetroCard intro.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- After placing furniture, a click reopened the Furniture Catalog over the room once (I had to close it).
- Coins went to 171 right after buying a 15 coin painting (level-up bonus, I guess, but it looked odd).
- Friend request accepted instantly, a bit suspicious.
- Many bottom-bar icons have no names.
- Test-tool glitch, not a game bug: my controller session stopped answering ("unknown agent") around action 58 right after clicking "Beat It", and was restored after the coordinator fixed it. The game itself was fine.

## 11. Scores
Fun 8, New York feel 8, Ease of use 8, Would recommend 8
