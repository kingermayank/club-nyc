# Aiko (23, Osaka café worker, A2-B1 English, laptop trackpad)

## 1. Arriving
A friend quote-posted the link on X. I landed on a page with a very pretty Central Park picture and a big green "Play Now". It looked like Animal Crossing in New York. I thought: a cute game, free, easy English. The cookie banner covered the bottom; I pressed "Necessary Only".

## 2. What I did
Scrolled a little on the landing page, then pressed Play Now (action 3). No login. Picked "Build My Own" (not "Use a Photo"; I have no camera and do not want my face in it). Chose skin, fluffy pink hair, a yellow hoodie. Typed the name AikoChan, ticked the 13+/terms box. Made a MetroCard in the photo booth, stamped it, swiped it (failed once "too fast", once "too slow", then worked). Rode the train to Times Square. Skipped Rosa's tour. Walked by clicking the ground, clicked another player and waved, opened the map, went to Grand Central, clicked the Opal Clock, walked into the Whispering Gallery.

## 3. Leaving
Around action 37 (about 6 minutes). Not unhappy. I wanted to save screenshots to send a friend, and the clock popup and tour popups had a lot of English, which made me tired. I would come back later when I have more time.

## 4. Fun
- Character maker: colour names like "Taxi Yellow" and "Subway Green", pink fluffy hair. Cute, no reading needed.
- The photo booth, MetroCard stamp and swipe. The "Please swipe again" joke made me smile.
- Seeing my own girl standing in Times Square with other people.
- The island map. Looks like a toy. Wanted to click every building.
- Grand Central: the gold clock and starry ceiling. Very Gossip Girl.
- Whispering Gallery: my bubble said "(whispers) ...hello?". Small surprise, loved it.
- Coins, XP and "Level 2" popping up after walking. Feels like a reward.

## 5. Static
- The Rosa tour boxes: "Things That Glow Are Clickable" etc. Lots of words. I wanted just a glowing arrow or a short picture.
- The Opal Clock popup: a long paragraph with numbers and history. I wanted one short line plus a picture or a sound.
- The mini checklist "New in Town" kept sitting on screen with text.
- Landing page scroll text, I did not read it.

## 6. Wanted
- Japanese (or simpler English) option, or a translate button.
- Short emoji-style ways to talk to people (chat in English is scary). The smiley/wave buttons helped.
- A way to take a cute screenshot of my character in Times Square.
- Photo option felt scary; a "draw my face" version without camera would be nice.
- Time Square arrows and places clearer without reading.

## 7. New York
True (from TV): yellow taxi, Times Square lights and Broadway sign, I Love NY souvenirs, pretzel and hot dog carts, Grand Central clock, subway sound "please swipe again". Pigeons everywhere made me laugh. I cannot judge deeper; I have never been. Nothing looked fake to me. I did not see a Sex and the City place (a brownstone or cupcake shop?) and wanted one.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe to yes. I would come back this weekend after work to try the other places and decorate my home. I would send it to my friend who posted it: "Look, it's like Animal Crossing but New York! You can make a pink hair girl and walk in Times Square. Free!"

## 9. One change
Show less English at the start: replace the tour and info paragraphs with one short line each and pictures, or add a language switch. That would keep me playing much longer.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- "Who's Walking into Manhattan?" was a hard sentence to parse.
- "Make It Official" / "Stamp it" unclear until I saw the card.
- Swipe minigame with a trackpad was fiddly (two failures). Space key hint helped, but it is small.
- After closing the clock popup, a second click on the gallery door only partly worked (I walked there on a later click). Not sure it was a bug.
- No visible reason for the "1" and "2" mail badge; I did not open it.

## 11. Scores (out of 10)
- Fun: 8
- New York feel: 8
- Ease of use: 7 (cute and easy, but English text and the swipe)
- Would recommend: 8
