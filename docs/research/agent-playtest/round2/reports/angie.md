# Exit interview: angie (Angie Brandt, 53, Greenwood IN)

## 1. Arriving
My daughter texted the link: "Mom you'd actually like this". I landed on a cartoon page: "Waddle on over to New York." I figured it was a Club Penguin-style kids game (my daughter played that), set in NYC. Cookie banner first; I picked "Necessary Only".

## 2. What I did
1. Clicked Play Now. No sign-up, straight into character creation.
2. Skipped "Use a Photo" (no thanks), picked Surprise Me, got a guy in sunglasses and a beanie. Fine.
3. Typed the name AngieB, ticked the 13+/terms box, walked in.
4. Intro: photo booth, stamped my own MetroCard, swiped it at a turnstile (failed once, "Please swipe again", then got in).
5. Times Square: clicked the ground, Rosa the super offered a tour; I took it. Clicked the hot dog cart (free, coins, Level 2).
6. Clicked "Join the Dance Jam" three times and pressed D; eventually my character said "Dancing...".
7. Opened the map, went to One World Trade. Clicked the 9/11 Memorial, read it, placed a white rose.
8. Left (action ~42).

## 3. Leaving
Not annoyed, just done for now. I'd seen the nice parts, it was near dinner, and I wanted to text my daughter. Nothing pushed me out: nothing asked for money, email or a photo.

## 4. Fun
- "No refunds for blinking." at the photo booth and the MetroCard stamping, with my member number. Sweet.
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." My daughter says exactly that. Made me laugh.
- Times Square looks gorgeous, like a picture book.
- Hot dog cart: "A classic dirty-water dog. Mustard only." Instant reward, no cost.
- The map is charming.
- The 9/11 Memorial: quiet, factual, no jokes, a white rose. This is what made me trust the place.

## 5. Static
- "Join the Dance Jam" button: nothing visible happened for several clicks. Only later did I see "Dancing..." over my head. I wanted a clear "you joined" and a visible dance.
- Checklist item "Take a Walk" stayed unchecked after I walked, even after the tour. Looked broken.
- Other players mostly stand in a clump and don't respond; the crowd felt a bit like scenery.
- Toolbar icons have no labels; I had to guess what each did.

## 6. Wanted
- Pick a character that looks like me (middle-aged woman); the random one was a guy and I didn't know how to change it quickly.
- Something to do that I recognize: a simple puzzle or word game (Wordle-ish), the Thanksgiving parade, pizza.
- A plain "how much does this cost / is it safe" answer visible without hunting.

## 7. New York
True: turnstile swipe, dirty-water dog, pretzel carts, taxis, subway, rats on the platform (saw one in the swipe scene), crowded Times Square. The memorial treatment felt right. Fake or odd: Times Square this friendly and tidy; no trash bags or sticker shock, which is probably the point.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe. I'd come back if my daughter is on at the same time. I'd send it to my sister: "Silly but sweet, a cartoon New York you can walk around in. Free, no signup. Try the 9/11 memorial and the hot dog cart."

## 9. One change
Show what to do right after the tour in plain steps, with one thing I can finish in five minutes (a game or the parade), and make the checklist actually tick.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- "Take a Walk" quest not completing.
- Dance Jam button gave no feedback.
- Swipe step: fiddly with a trackpad; took three tries and timing hints were vague.
- Unlabeled icons everywhere in the bottom bar.

## 11. Scores
Fun 7, New York feel 8, Ease of use 6, Would recommend 7
