# Exit interview: jiwoo (Ji-woo Park, 21, NYU sophomore)

## 1. Arriving
An Instagram reel with cartoon Times Square art showed up while I was avoiding an essay. I tapped through on my laptop. The landing page ("Waddle on over to New York.") told me right away it was a Club Penguin-style game set in NYC. I worried it might be a kids' game, but the art was good enough that I hit Play Now after one short scroll. I declined analytics on the cookie banner.

## 2. What I did (about 61 actions, roughly 10 minutes)
1. Landing page, cookie banner, scrolled once, Play Now.
2. Character creator: skipped "Use a Photo", chose Build My Own, picked skin and hair, then a name (JiwooUnion) and the age/terms checkbox.
3. MetroCard intro: photo booth, stamping the card, swiping it (failed twice, third try worked), subway ride.
4. Arrived in Times Square, clicked a coin, Rosa popped up and I picked "explore on my own". Walked around, got a stamp and Level 2.
5. Opened the map, went to Chinatown & Little Italy, then into Joe's Pizza.
6. Played Carb-Jitsu against a "city local" for about 8 rounds. Never reached a result.
7. Pressed Esc ("No hard feelings"), kept playing, then left.

## 3. Leaving
Around action 61. The card game had gone on for 8 rounds, I had won 3 pizza rounds and it still didn't end. The rule line says "from 3 boroughs", and all my pizza wins were Bronx cards, but nothing told me that mid-game and there was no tally of my wins. It felt broken, so I lost interest. Add to that: I never found anywhere near my own life (Washington Square, St. Marks, K-town), never made a friend, and the essay was still waiting. I wasn't annoyed, just not hooked yet.

## 4. Fun
- The Times Square scene itself. It looks like the reel, with pigeons, I♥NY Souvenirs, Broadway marquee and the red steps. That was the "wow".
- "No refunds for blinking" in the photo booth, and the MetroCard with "Member No. 0004".
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Every NYU student has heard it. Failing the swipe was funnier than succeeding.
- The map art, and Chinatown/Little Italy: Joe's Shanghai, Joe's Pizza, a boba shop, the Little Italy arch.
- Joe's Pizza interior: checkered floor, a cat, a penguin photo on the wall (Club Penguin nod).
- Small quick rewards: stamps, coins, level-up at the start, like Animal Crossing.
- Result lines: "Pizza beats Pretzel. It's a whole meal." / "Bagel beats Pizza. Breakfast comes first."
- "You'll forfeit this match. No hard feelings."

## 5. Static
- The "Rosa" intro popup: a talking-head card with two buttons. I skipped it, and the checklist later just tells me to click the ground.
- Locals' speech bubbles ("Coffee first. Then sightseeing.") are cute but don't lead anywhere when you click them.
- Waiting in the Carb-Jitsu lobby alone ("Play a Local" is a bot): hoped a real person would join.
- The "Dancing..." and name tags on the landing page look like a live game but are just a picture.

## 6. Wanted
- A running score or win tracker in Carb-Jitsu, and a clear message of why a 3-win didn't count.
- To find Washington Square, St. Marks, Union Square, K-town on the map. The map seems to have about 20 places, none of them downtown-college life.
- To click the name of a real player and say hi (the "Make a Friend" quest sent me looking for someone, but players in Times Square were across the screen and I went off to the map).
- A way to skip the long MetroCard intro on a replay, and a more forgiving swipe on a trackpad (two failures).
- Korean food / bubble tea to actually order something.

## 7. New York
True: the turnstile line, pigeons, the 1 train announcement, Joe's Pizza and Joe's Shanghai, Chinatown next to Little Italy, "Walking fast counts as cardio", the Broadway marquees, the "boiler to fix" super.
Fake or off: $.99 slices (not for years), a squashed Manhattan where Times Square sits next to the Empire State Building and Chinatown is by the Brooklyn Bridge (fine for a cartoon), everyone being cheerful and nobody cranky or in a hurry. The city I know is also a bit lonely, and this one feels friendly, which might be the point.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe. If someone I know is on, or if there's a Village / Union Square area, yes. I'd send it to my roommate Hana: "its like Club Penguin but New York, the Times Square art is so cute and the turnstile joke is real. 5 min, free, no download."

## 9. One change
Show me what to do next beyond tutorial popups, e.g. put a real person next to me from the start and let me add them. And make Carb-Jitsu say clearly how a match is won, with a scoreboard.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Carb-Jitsu: 3 pizza wins did not end the match. "From 3 boroughs" is only in the pre-game text, and no on-screen tally. Looked like a bug.
- MetroCard swipe: "too slow" vs "too fast" window is narrow for a trackpad drag.
- The 4th-ish toolbar icons have no labels until you hover (map was fine, the others I never figured out).
- Notification "2 people are hanging out at Times Square" came up mid-game, with a Go button I could not use because the game was a full-screen panel.

## 11. Scores (out of 10)
- Fun: 7
- New York feel: 7
- Ease of use: 6
- Would recommend: 6
