# Ellen Park (72), exit interview

## 1. Arriving
My niece emailed it: "Imo, look, it's the city!" I landed on a cartoon Central Park with the Boathouse, a pretzel cart and little people with name tags. "Waddle on over to New York." I thought: some kind of children's game, but pretty, and the headline was big enough to read with my glasses.

## 2. What I did
1. Cookie banner: Necessary Only. Pressed the green Play Now.
2. "Who's Walking into Manhattan?": skipped "Use a Photo" (no thank you), chose Surprise Me. Got a grey-haired lady with darker skin than mine.
3. Typed a name (BellevueEllen), ticked the age/terms box, Walk into the City.
4. Photo booth, snapped, stamped the MetroCard, swiped it (three tries: "please swipe again", "too slow", then in).
5. Rode the 1 train to Times Square. Rosa the super gave me a short tour (walk, glowing things, chat, map).
6. Landed in my own 5th-floor walk-up in the East Village (radiator, fire escape, water tower). Read The Club NYC Times.
7. Opened the map, picked Grand Central (near my old Murray Hill).
8. Walked into the Whispering Gallery (got a stamp), said hello in chat asking about Murray Hill. Closed the tab.

## 3. Leaving
After about 40 actions, calmly. I had seen what my niece meant, got a stamp, and only 2 other people were online and nobody answered me yet. Nothing annoyed me enough to leave; it was a good stopping point. I would close it feeling warm, not frustrated.

## 4. Fun
- MetroCard with my photo: "No refunds for blinking."
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile": I heard it for 35 years.
- Times Square: billboards, I LOVE NY souvenirs, hot dog cart, taxi. Rosa, "your building's super", with a boiler to fix.
- My apartment with the hissing radiator and fire escape.
- The newspaper: soup dumpling joke, the 1904 subway opening for a nickel (true).
- The map, and above all Grand Central: the four-faced clock, the zodiac ceiling, the Whispering Gallery where my character whispered "hello?" on her own. That got a smile and a stamp.

## 5. Static
- Landing page chat bubbles ("anyone in Times Square?") look alive but I could not tell.
- The Dance Jam countdown at the top while I was stuck in setup: I never knew what to do with it.
- Nobody replied to my chat in Grand Central. The other people mostly said their own jokes ("That pigeon just judged my outfit") rather than answer me.
- The bottom toolbar icons: ten little pictures with no words; I had to guess.

## 6. Wanted
- To look like me (Korean, grey hair). Random gave someone else and I lost my skin-colour click when the screen moved on.
- Bigger text option; the character maker labels and Skip Intro are small with reading glasses.
- To skip the swipe test without feeling tested.
- Bellevue, First Avenue, the Upper West Side, any hospital. The map has none of them. A person to talk to who knew the old city.

## 7. New York
True: the turnstile line, train announcements and transfers, radiator, walk-up, Grand Central details (clock, gallery, zodiac), Track 29, 1904 subway nickel. Fake or missing: it is a MetroCard (the city went to OMNY, though I miss them), and Manhattan on the map has no east side hospitals or the whole Upper West Side, which is most of my life there.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, leaning yes, if I knew someone else would be on. To my niece I would say: "I made a little person, rode the 1 train and stood in the whispering gallery. The turnstile joke got me. Where is Bellevue on it?"

## 9. One change
Make the first minutes gentler for slow hands: large text option and a clear Skip on the swipe, and let me pick my own look (skin included) before a random one is locked in.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Skin colour click was lost: "That's Me" moved on before my click landed (felt like the screen advanced early).
- After Rosa's "Let's Go!" I suddenly was in my apartment with a newspaper open and a level-up toast; I did not know how I got there.
- Snap! step has a pause with nothing saying wait.
- Test-tool glitch, not a game bug: my session was cut off around action 21 ("unknown agent"), then restored by the coordinator.

## 11. Scores
Fun 7, New York feel 8, Ease of use 6, Would recommend 7
