# chloe: exit interview

## 1. Arriving
A link in a cozy-games Discord. I thought it was a Club Penguin-style browser game set in New York. The landing page's illustrated Central Park looked lovely, so I clicked Play Now straight away. I turned analytics off on the cookie banner.

## 2. What I did
1. Declined analytics, clicked Play Now.
2. Avatar creator: chose Build My Own over Photo or Surprise Me, tried skin, hair, hair colour, and the colour ring around the avatar.
3. Typed the name PixelMaple, ticked the 13+/terms box, walked in.
4. Intro: MetroCard photo booth, stamped the card, swiped it (first try too fast, "Please swipe again", second try worked), took a subway ride into Times Square.
5. Rosa the super offered a tour; I skipped it after two steps.
6. Walked around Times Square and got a stamp. Went into I ♥ NY Souvenirs, tried on and bought a tee and a Foam Liberty Crown.
7. Found "My Apartment" in the toolbar. Opened Decorate, placed a bean bag, lamp and monstera.
8. Opened the Flatbush Flea furniture catalog and bought a Pepperoni Rug (45 coins). Placed it. Left.

## 3. Leaving
I left around action 60. Nothing pushed me out. I had seen the loop I came for (dress up, decorate) and had 53 coins left, with nice items costing 60 to 90. It felt like a good stopping point. I'd return later for more coins.

## 4. Fun
- The MetroCard swipe mini-game. "Please swipe again at this turnstile" got a real laugh. Too fast fails, so there is a skill bit with a joke attached.
- "No refunds for blinking" at the photo booth.
- The subway arrival with the announcement, then arriving in Times Square. The art is lovely and consistent.
- Shop try-on preview on the avatar. It's easy to buy hats and silly things.
- The apartment: window with the Empire State Building, radiator, fire escape. The Pepperoni Rug and Everything Bagel Bag are exactly my humour.
- The XP, level-up and stamp toasts all feel generous and not spammy.

## 5. Static
- Rosa's tour was a popup and text. I wanted it to walk me to the things, so I skipped it.
- Other players and locals mostly say canned lines ("Stand clear of the closing doors!"). Fun once, but I couldn't tell who was real.
- The decorate tray covers half the room. Items spawn at my feet, not where I want them, and I had to drag them elsewhere.
- Photo booth and "Save My Card" had no obvious payoff. I didn't know what saving does.

## 6. Wanted
- A preview of the room before buying furniture, the way the shop previews clothes.
- Place items by dragging from the tray straight onto the floor, and a way to hide the tray while arranging.
- Rename or theme the apartment, and wallpaper or floor options (I only saw furniture).
- To see other people's apartments. I saw a visit icon but didn't go.
- A clearer way to earn coins fast. I wanted to know the coin loop without grinding.

## 7. New York
- True: the subway announcement, "Please swipe again", the super, the walk-up with a radiator and fire escape, pretzel carts, pigeons, and Times Square being a tourist trap with a souvenir shop. The tiny kitchen is very NYC.
- Fake or odd: the apartment is a bit big and sunny for an East Village fifth-floor walk-up. I only visited NYC once, so I can't judge much more.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe to yes. I'd come back to earn coins and finish the apartment. I'd send it to my Discord cozy-games friends: "It's a Club Penguin-ish New York hangout with an Animal Crossing-style apartment you decorate. The art's gorgeous and it's free in the browser."

## 9. One change
Make decorating easier to do. Let me place and move furniture without a tray covering the room, and show me a preview of items before I spend coins.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- After my first click on a coin balance, it went from 70 to 95 when I bought the 15-coin tee. I think a welcome gift arrived at the same time but I didn't see a toast, so it was confusing.
- My first Decorate session placed the lamp and bean bag but not the monstera. I had to re-open Decorate to place it. I didn't know items stay in storage until placed.
- Several toolbar icons have no labels. I found My Apartment only by trying one.
- The welcome-gift task got ticked off without me clicking Sal's hot dog cart (I never did it).

## 11. Scores
- Fun: 8
- New York feel: 8
- Ease of use: 7
- Would recommend: 8
