# Exit interview: priya (Priya Raman, 31, Jersey City, MacBook + trackpad, Instagram ad)

## 1. Arriving
Instagram ad. Landed on a gorgeous Brooklyn Bridge illustration: "Waddle on over to New York." and "Remember waddling around... Imagine all that nostalgia, but in NYC." I never played the game this refers to, so the pitch was lost on me. I figured it was a cute cartoon NYC chat game. Free, no signup wall, which I liked. Cookie banner: I picked Necessary Only.

## 2. What I did (about 46 actions, roughly 8 minutes)
Cookie banner, scrolled the landing page once, Play Now. Picked Surprise Me for my character (skipped Use a Photo, I'm not uploading my face), typed a name, ticked the age/terms box. MetroCard photo booth, stamp, and swipe (failed twice, "Please swipe again"). Arrived in Times Square, took the walk quest, skipped Rosa's tour, clicked the hot dog cart (Level 2). Opened the map and went to One World Trade (my commute), clicked the Oculus and ended up on Wall Street. Read the Exchange card and the Big Board ticker. Rode the subway to Times Square, danced with the busker, got Level 3, started the Showtime rhythm mini-game, scored 0, left.

## 3. Leaving
In Showtime. The arrows fell, I pressed keys, and the score stayed at 0 with no sign of whether my presses registered. After that I couldn't see what the next thing worth doing was. Nothing was broken or rude; I just had no pull to stay. A rhythm game is also not my kind of game.

## 4. Fun
- MetroCard intro: "No refunds for blinking", the stamp, "New Yorker No. 0014". Short, charming, and it made the account feel earned.
- "Please swipe again at this turnstile." The failed swipe was painfully true and funny.
- Times Square art, the subway platform (pizza rat, the busker, the NEW YORK tile wall) and the One WTC plaza. Genuinely beautiful and detailed.
- The Big Board ticker: "GPUs in stock 0.00", "rent still went up more", "Figma went public. The logo is still in review." As a tech worker these landed.
- "Day-Tripper" and "Commuter" level titles.

## 5. Static
- Landing page: the nostalgia line meant nothing to me. A picture of a nice bridge plus a sentence I didn't get.
- Other players: BullMarketBrad, MuseumMaya and EggCreamEmma stood in place and said a canned line. I couldn't tell if they were people or bots. Nothing happened when I tried to interact.
- Wall Street Exchange card, Oculus: a tap gives a text card. I wanted to go inside or do something.
- Quest chips (Take a Walk, hot dog cart): chores that hand out coins but aren't play.
- Showtime: score 0 with no feedback, so "nothing happens."

## 6. Wanted
- A one-sentence reason to be here ("what do I do in this game?").
- Oculus to lead to PATH, which is the thing I actually ride.
- To chat with a real person and see it was a real person.
- Clear feedback in Showtime on hit or miss, and an easier first pass.
- Settings and sound options (I can't tell if there was any).

## 7. New York
True: Oculus wings, memorial pool, pretzel cart, orange autumn trees at WTC, subway line names and the turnstile, pizza rat, rent jokes, the ticker. Roughly the real feel of Times Square.
Fake or off: Charging Bull is at Bowling Green, not in front of the Exchange. Clicking the One WTC tower took me to Wall Street via the Oculus, which makes no sense. There is no PATH anywhere, so an entire class of commuters is missing. Central Park on the map is tiny. Everyone is in a cartoon world with no one rude, which is not New York.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe, leaning no. I enjoyed ten minutes but nothing pulled me back. I'd send the Big Board screen to a coworker: "This NYC game has a stock ticker where GPUs in stock are 0.00, made me laugh", not as a game to play.

## 9. One change
Give me a first-minute hook that isn't a chore: a quick, forgiving game or a real person to talk to, plus a landing line that explains the game to someone who never played the thing it's nostalgic for.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- Clicking One World Trade's tower teleported me to Wall Street (it was the Oculus exit hover, but I had no idea).
- Showtime score stayed 0 after several presses and I couldn't tell why.
- Many toolbar icons have no labels. I didn't know what most of them did without hovering.
- First swipe attempt: a fast and a slow swipe both failed with unclear feedback. It's funny, but I needed three tries.
- Rosa's tour popped up unprompted right after the first move.

## 11. Scores
Fun 6/10, New York feel 7/10, Ease of use 6/10, Would recommend 5/10.
