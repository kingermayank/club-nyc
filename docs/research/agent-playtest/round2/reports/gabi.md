# Exit interview: gabi (Gabriela Moreira, 26, Sao Paulo)

## 1. Arriving
A post in a Facebook group of Brazilians planning NYC trips. I landed on a pretty page of Central Park in fall with cartoon people and thought "a Club Penguin-like game about New York". I did not know the word "Waddle" in the headline. I said no to analytics cookies (Necessary Only).

## 2. What I did
1. Clicked Play Now. Chose Build My Own, avatar looked like me already, typed GabiSP, ticked the terms box to continue.
2. MetroCard photo booth, stamp, then the swipe mini-game (failed 2 times: "please swipe again", "too slow", then "You're in").
3. Arrived in Times Square. Clicked the taxi (nothing), then met Rosa the "super" and took "Show me around". Walked, got XP.
4. Hot dog cart gave a free gift. I chose the Foam Liberty Crown. Level 2.
5. Opened the map, clicked the Statue of Liberty, went to Battery Park, bought a ferry ride (free passenger), arrived at Liberty Island, got stamp "Lady Liberty".
6. Tried the Tower Viewer. Left.

## 3. Leaving
Action 45, about 7 minutes in. I left because I had already got the thing I came for: I stood at the Statue of Liberty with a Liberty crown on my head. Nothing was bad. I was not bored, I was satisfied and wanted to show my mom and the group. The only "meh" right before was the viewer with Roman numerals (July IV, MDCCLXXVI), which I skipped.

## 4. Fun
- Times Square first look: yellow taxi, hot dog cart, I LOVE NY, Broadway sign. This is my Instagram New York.
- MetroCard booth: "No refunds for blinking." Easy English and funny.
- The turnstile swipe: "Please swipe again at this turnstile." Real joke, I laughed. Also felt like a real mini-game.
- Free Foam Liberty Crown, then the same crown showing on my character at the real Statue.
- Map of Manhattan: simple, pretty, I knew where to click. Ferry to Liberty Island: short, felt like a trip.
- Coin, XP and stamp sounds, confetti. Small rewards often.

## 5. Static
- Clicking the taxi in Times Square: nothing happened, I hoped to ride it or see a yellow cab drive.
- Tower Viewer at the Statue: a picture where I move the mouse. I hoped for a view of the city or a photo to keep.
- "New Yorkers online" and the people around me: names like KnishKing sit still, I could not tell who is real or a bot. Not much to do with them.
- Landing page: nice, but I only wanted to click Play.

## 6. Wanted
- Central Park in snow (my dream). I did not get to it.
- Ride a yellow taxi.
- Save my card or progress: I did not dare click "Save My Card", unsure if it asks for email.
- Portuguese or simpler English. A phone version (I am mostly a phone gamer).
- A way to show off my crown and stamps to my friends (a share picture).

## 7. New York
True: Times Square with the red stairs and screens, hot dog cart, ferry dock to Liberty, MetroCard swipe jokes, orange Staten Island ferry, pigeons. It looks like the NYC I know from movies.
Fake or odd: I cannot judge much since I have never been. The Statue looked close to Battery Park, which in the real map I think is far. Rosa "building's super" and "boiler" I did not understand, it may be real NYC talk but it was lost on me.

## 8. Coming back
Maybe. I would come back to see Central Park and snow, and if it works on my phone. I would send it to my Facebook trip group. Message: "Gente, olha esse joguinho de Nova York, tem Times Square e Estatua da Liberdade, da pra jogar de graca e pegar uma coroa da Liberdade! Link: (it)". English words would not matter, it is easy.

## 9. One change
Make it work well on a phone and add a small Portuguese option (or a "simple English" mode). That would keep me much longer and I would send it to everyone.

## 10. Bugs and confusion
- "Waddle", "super", "boiler", "Day-Tripper", "Lox Pink" (avatar name), "Roman numerals" all hard for B1 English.
- Swipe game: first attempt gave no help about how fast to drag. The hint "press Space" was clear only after reading.
- Taxi click does nothing, no feedback.
- "Rosa" popup appeared after my click on the taxi, so I was not sure if the taxi caused it.
- No clear sign of where Central Park is on the map (it is there but not named).

## 11. Scores
- Fun: 8
- New York feel: 8
- Ease of use: 7
- Would recommend: 7
